# P1 numbers to 100: compare magnitudes

**Source check:** 16 November 2026. The [EDB 2017 supplement, 1N3 objective 3](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf#page=13) requires comparing number magnitudes and reserves `<` and `>` for 2N1. The [2018 Key Stage 1 notes](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS1_e.pdf#page=11) also describe comparing tens, then units, and ordering groups of numbers. This release covers choosing the larger of **two** numerals only.

## Bilingual item brief

English: “Which number is larger, 42 or 37? Choose the number.” Chinese: “哪個數較大：42還是37？選出該數。” Both values are between 21 and 100, distinct, and displayed in their spoken order. The pupil selects one numeral; no inequality symbol is requested. Twelve permanent `compare100a` cases include same-tens decisions, reversed digit pairs, tens-boundary pairs and 100 versus 90. One mark requires the larger displayed numeral. No partial credit.

The key compares tens first; when tens match, it compares units. The practice hint points to the relevant place without revealing the answer, then allows one retry and shows the worked step. Three distinct cases record correct first attempts separately. A wrong answer may mean a pupil compared only units; that is a hypothesis, not an observed misconception.

## Scope and next objectives

This task assumes numeral reading from 1N1. It does not assess counting, writing 21–100, odd/even, tens and units as a separate representation, ordering three or more numerals, counting in groups of 2/5/10, or estimation. The next 1N3 releases should address objectives 1, 2, 4 and 5 separately. A full-unit claim requires objective-by-objective review and suitable tasks or a teacher-led rubric for each objective.
