# 1S2 · 2-D shapes: source and assessment audit

**Status: verified resource complete.** See the dated checkpoint below for evidence and unperformed validation limits.

## Source check

Checked 16 November 2026 against the complete 1S2 entry in [2017 EDB supplement, printed pp.17–18 / PDF pp.20–21](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf#page=20), including the continuation-page remarks, and [2018 KS1 explanatory notes, printed pp.15–16 / PDF pp.21–22](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS1_e.pdf#page=21).

Five objectives: intuitive points/straight lines/curves; drawing and making lines; recognising five shape types; drawing and making them; composing with those types. Remarks distinguish physical marks from dimensionless points and breadthless mathematical lines; two fixed points determine one straight line but admit different curves. Simple non-self-crossing polygons only; no formal concave/convex vocabulary. Rotation preserves type. Tools, materials and freely composed arrangements are permitted. No equal-side, angle-measurement or regular-polygon requirement is added.

## Existing family: what is actually assessed

`triangle1-0` through `triangle1-8` ask only which of A/B/C is a triangle. Every variant has one rotated triangle, one quadrilateral and one circle; the correct position cycles. This is partial objective3 evidence: triangle selection under rotation. Neither a quadrilateral/circle distractor nor the worked explanation proves the pupil can name those types. It assesses no pentagon/hexagon naming, point/line concept, physical construction, drawing or composition. Its permanent semantics remain unchanged.

## Technical and release gates

Static black-and-white SVG with consistent strokes; no colour-dependent cue. Three-column cards on desktop/print, two on narrow screens. Figures are native markup with neutral accessible labels that do not reveal answers. All answer material is hidden by default and revealed by the existing keyboard-operable button; language sections remain mutually exclusive. Key printing begins on a new page. No fixed page count is promised.

Only this plan and `activities/p1-2d-shapes.html` were authored. New digital recognition families would be useful for repeated practice of the other shape types, but are not required to supply these authored teacher-led tasks and cannot replace drawing/making evidence. Do not count this sheet as digital-family coverage.
