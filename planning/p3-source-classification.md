# Curriculum, HKEAA assessment references and MathQuiz extensions

Reviewed 19 November 2026 against both supplied documents, `2026_3ME_final.pdf` (84 pages) and `2026_3MC_final.pdf` (82 pages), including their item-to-basic-competency tables.

## Three source roles

1. **EDB curriculum framework:** use [EDB curriculum documents](https://www.edb.gov.hk/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/index2.html) for teaching units, objectives and permitted content. Existing codes such as `3N1` retain that curriculum meaning.
2. **HKEAA TSA assessment reference:** HKEAA develops and administers TSA on behalf of EDB. Its P3 design assesses P1–P3 across Number, Measures, Shape & Space and Data Handling. Codes such as `KS1-N1-1` identify end-of-Key-Stage-1 basic competencies, not a new P3-only syllabus. Refer to [HKEAA's role](https://www.hkeaa.edu.hk/en/sa_tsa/tsa//1000), [official papers](https://bca.hkeaa.edu.hk/web/TSA/en/2026priPaper/P3Index.html) and [BC descriptors](https://www.bca.hkeaa.edu.hk/web/TSA/en/BC/P_BC_M.pdf).
3. **MathQuiz authoring:** practice categories, original contexts, Easy/Normal/Difficult levels and additional checks are designed by MathQuiz. They are not official HKEAA items, official proficiency levels or extra EDB requirements. An assessment-design extension is not automatically outside the EDB curriculum; a separate curriculum-objective check is needed before making that claim.

## Count and code interpretation

The P3 catalogue has **50 MathQuiz practice types**, comprising 35 existing curriculum-linked types and 15 TSA-informed additions. It does not contain 50 official curriculum objectives. The EDB curriculum inventory is unchanged. `3R1` remains a clearly labelled local cumulative-review group, not an EDB or HKEAA code.

The added types may be grouped under related curriculum units for navigation. This grouping does not make the added question format itself an official curriculum unit. Older P1/P2 learning is legitimately assessed in the P3 TSA and should be labelled cumulative review, not new P3 content.

The design specifies one basic competency per official item, minimises linked problems, aligns distractors to BCs and uses familiar contexts. The combined tasks and every added Difficult check are therefore labelled **MathQuiz extension practice relative to the assessment design**. They remain optional teaching material; they are not evidence of official TSA item equivalence or a new EDB curriculum requirement.

## Visible source labels

- The P3 directory explains the three roles and shows HKEAA references and related BC codes on the 15 added practice cards.
- Fixed-level quizzes, printed papers, worked keys and Smart practice show a source note for each added question.
- Variant-level labels distinguish TSA-informed adaptations from extensions. In particular, a one-to-one pictogram remains an adaptation while a two- or three-per-symbol pictogram is labelled an extension; Difficult checks are always labelled as MathQuiz-designed extensions.
- Labels identify the authority and the authorship separately: **HKEAA TSA reference; MathQuiz original practice**.

No mathematical operands, answers, marks, IDs, progression rules or original 1,260 P3 tasks change in this clarification.
