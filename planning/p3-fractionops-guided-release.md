## Scope and source

Reread the existing official [EDB KS1 notes, PDF54–55 / printed48–49](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS1_e.pdf#page=54) and [unit source audit](p3-fractions-i.md), including the continuation restrictions. No fresh download is claimed. The earlier foundations cover 3N5.1–2. These five lessons address 3N5.3–4: comparison with a shared numerator or denominator; diagram-based addition or subtraction of at most three like-denominator fractions. Operands, intermediate amounts and results stay from zero to one. Operations are pure addition or pure subtraction, with oral solving before horizontal recording. No simplest-form demand, unlike-denominator operations, improper/mixed fractions or symbolic combined-numerator rule is introduced. The [existing activity sheets](../activities/p3-fractions-i.html) remain unchanged.

| Lesson | Screen experience | Separate observation |
|---|---|---|
| Same denominator | Aligned same-sized strips; compare marked counts | Make equal partitions, speak a comparison, then write its symbol |
| Same numerator | Compare covered endpoints; positive and zero cases | Overlay prepared strips, explain part size and record help |
| Pictured addition | Non-overlapping A/B shares; whole result included | Draw new shares, explain aloud, write a horizontal equation |
| Pictured subtraction | Crossed removals and remaining X parts; zero included | Remove actual shares and explain the unchanged whole partition |
| Three fractions | Three shares joined or two successive removals | Explain each intermediate amount before writing a pure-operation equation |

Five lessons contain 45 practice tasks, ten guided examples and ten sample checks. Checks use new numerical/represented tuples; task wording and concepts recur. Choice options represent distinct amounts, use the pictured denominator and never demand simplification. Equivalent written answers are explicitly accepted in practical work. These samples are not an independent readiness certificate.

## Models, accessibility and records

All comparison strips have identical whole widths and aligned ends. Operation diagrams describe the whole, each share and removal in text. Letters distinguish addition shares; cross marks show removals and X marks show what remains. No colour judgement is necessary. Zero, one and equal comparisons are included. The initial operation picture has no written equation. Learners acknowledge explaining it before revealing horizontal notation; trying another picture hides the equation and resets that acknowledgement. Acknowledgement remains checked while its revealed equation is shown. It is self-report and does not certify oral performance.

Model changes are temporary, unscored and unsaved. First responses, one supported retry, observations and original inquiry predictions stay separate. The investigation asks for different non-overlapping shares with the same total, including zero and unsuccessful predictions. A new `fractionops` topic record avoids extending and risking truncation by older foundation tabs. The foundation data, lesson/check IDs and count-specific scorer are unchanged. Shared navigation and useful-first links connect both routes without locking access. All fourteen topics and three learners remain isolated.
