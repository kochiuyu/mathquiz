# 1N2 · Basic addition and subtraction: objective review

Status: supplementary material authored; independent review, browser/print QA, release and live verification pending. No closure or classroom-mastery claim.

## Sources and scope

Checked 16 November 2026 against [EDB 2017 supplement, PDF pages 11–12](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf#page=11) and [2018 KS1 notes, printed pages 3–4](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS1_e.pdf#page=9). Objective labels below are paraphrased.

Facts derive from the two one-digit parts learnt in 1N1, total at most 18. A subtraction such as 13 − 7 = 6 is in scope; operations such as 12 + 5 and 17 − 5 are not required here. Show problems mainly with pictures, ask for oral solutions before horizontal records, and avoid column form. Use addition to represent joining/increasing, subtraction for removal/comparison. Pupils recognise sum/difference as result words. Zero work includes an empty collection after removal and all four properties: 0 + n, n + 0, n − 0, n − n. Inverse exploration stays within 10. The commutativity label and the term whole number are not required; no negative-number teaching is introduced by the subtraction contrast.

## Existing digital evidence, audited in builder.js

- `add1`: seven joining-context addition variants with totals at most 10.
- `pictureadd1`: 81 variants, two positive one-digit groups and horizontal addition statement; all totals 2–18.
- `storyadd1`: 81 adding-on contexts using the same scope with pictures, numeric total answer.
- A fixed pencil addition question is extra addition evidence only.
- None assesses subtraction, the zero cases, an inverse exploration or swapping addends as a pupil action. Explanations in a key are not evidence that pupils have performed those actions. 1N1 `otherway1` explicitly rejects merely swapping parts, so it cannot certify the commutative property.
