## Six additional original drafts

Grade placements are provisional. Catalogue labels were compared, but a complete generated-question duplicate audit is still required before implementation. These are candidate extensions or formats, not six approved new curriculum skills.

### HK005 — Check equal sharing (P2)

**English:** Share 19 counters equally among 4 trays, leaving as few counters as possible. A pupil puts 3 in each tray and leaves 7. Is the sharing finished? Correct it.

**中文：**把19粒圓片平均分放在4個盤子，餘下的圓片要盡量少。有同學每盤放3粒，餘下7粒。分完了嗎？請改正。

**Answer:** Not finished. Each tray can receive another counter: 4 in each tray, 3 remaining. The remainder must be less than the number of trays. Related skills: `share2a`, `divremain2a`, `divcheck2a`.

### HK006 — Compare offers for the quantity needed (P6)

**English:** Identical notebooks cost $25 each at shop A. Every group of 3 costs the price of 2; other notebooks cost $25 each. Shop B charges $20 each. Which shop is cheaper for exactly 2 notebooks, and for exactly 3?

**中文：**同款記事簿在A店每本25元，每3本只收2本的價錢，其餘每本25元。B店每本20元。剛好買2本時哪店較便宜？剛好買3本呢？

**Answer:** For 2, A costs $50 and B $40: B. For 3, A costs $50 and B $60: A. Compare percentage and decimal-money question banks, including `pctchange6a`, before classifying novelty.

### HK007 — Count without double-counting (P4 optional)

**English:** Cards are numbered 1–60. Select multiples of 4 or 6, including multiples of both. How many cards are selected? Explain how to avoid counting a card twice.

**中文：**數字卡標有1至60。選出4或6的倍數，包括同時是兩者倍數的數。共選出多少張？說明怎樣避免重複點算。

**Answer:** 20: 15 + 10 − 5. Related skills: `multiples4a`, `commonmultiples4a`. A list or sorting diagram should precede any formula.

### HK008 — Numbers unchanged by reversal (P3 optional)

**English:** Using only digits 1, 2 and 3, with repetition allowed, list all three-digit numbers unchanged when their hundreds and ones digits are swapped.

**中文：**只用數字1、2、3組成三位數，數字可以重複。列出所有把百位和個位數字對調後仍不變的數。

**Answer:** 111, 121, 131, 212, 222, 232, 313, 323, 333. Compare place-value tasks and the existing digit-target collection; this uses equal outer digits, not near-target arithmetic.

### HK009 — Repair a measurement with gaps (P1)

**English:** A strip is exactly 5 equal tiles long. A pupil covers its span with 3 tiles and two gaps, each gap one tile long, then calls it 3 tiles long. Explain the mistake and show a correct measurement.

**中文：**一條紙帶剛好長5塊相同方塊。某同學用了3塊方塊，中間留下兩個各長1塊方塊的空隙，剛好排到紙帶兩端。他說紙帶長3塊方塊。指出錯誤，並示範正確量度。

**Answer:** Fill both gaps: 3 + 2 = 5 equal units, with no gaps or overlaps. Teacher-supported length extension. An original diagram is required before pupil release; no physical measurement is claimed.

### HK010 — Find the starting amount (P1)

**English:** After receiving 4 beads, Mei has 11. How many did Mei have before? Complete: □ + 4 = 11.

**中文：**小美收到4粒珠子後，共有11粒。她原本有多少粒？填寫：□ + 4 = 11。

**Answer:** 7. The event increases the amount, but the unknown is the starting amount. Related skills: `parts18a` and addition/subtraction stories; this is an extension, not a new official skill.

## Research status and next step

There are now **10 Hong Kong draft candidates**: the previous four on payment, budgets, story writing and itineraries, plus these six. None has been added to the pupil question banks. The six new answer models were independently checked by arithmetic and exhaustive enumeration where applicable; teacher explanation quality and pupil difficulty remain unverified.

Progression should move from a diagram or supplied case, to independent application, to checking alternatives or explaining an error. Larger numbers alone are not a harder level. The updated [question research workflow](question-research-workflow.md) includes local source queries, source-answer checks, old-code mapping and separate source/format labels.
