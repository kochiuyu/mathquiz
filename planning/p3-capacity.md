## Source audit

Seven objectives cover capacity concept, intuitive/direct/improvised comparison, L/mL recognition, measurement/comparison, and recording. Remarks require suitable units/tools and estimation after experience. Recognise lower-case unit symbols and 1000 mL = 1 L. Mixed-to-single recording is included; reverse conversion and decimals are not required. Avoid sorting many containers simultaneously.
