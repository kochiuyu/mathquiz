# P5 simple equations · family brief

Status: live from 16 November 2026 as `equation5a`. Manual pupil and hands-on screen-reader checks remain deferred.

## Curriculum match

Primary 5, **5A2 Simple equations (I), objective 2**: solve simple equations. The [2017 EDB learning-content supplement](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf#page=82) lists `x + b = c` and `x − b = c` among the allowed forms and asks pupils to check their answers. The [2020 Key Stage 2 notes](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS2_e.pdf#page=39) explain equality and the inverse-operation approach. This first family covers those two one-step forms with whole-number solutions. It does not assess modelling a word problem or every 5A2 equation type.

## Bilingual item contract

| Element | English | 繁體中文 |
|---|---|---|
| Prompt | Solve `x + 7 = 19`. Write the value of `x`. | 解方程 `x + 7 = 19`，寫出 `x` 的值。 |
| Answer | `12` | `12` |
| First hint | Subtract 7 from both sides to keep the equation balanced. | 等號兩邊同時減去 7，保持方程平衡。 |
| Worked check | `x = 19 − 7 = 12`; check `12 + 7 = 19`. | `x = 19 − 7 = 12`；驗算 `12 + 7 = 19`。 |

Twelve permanent variants cover addition and subtraction, with positive whole-number answers. One labelled answer field accepts surrounding spaces and full-width digits, but rejects decimals, calculations and units. Each worked key substitutes the answer back into the equation. Practice gives an operation-specific hint and a retry before the worked check, then two distinct follow-ups. Test all variants, correct/wrong/blank marking, bilingual views, paper/PDF, old IDs and phone width. Classroom and hands-on screen-reader checks are deferred.
