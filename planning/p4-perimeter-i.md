## Source scope

[EDB KS2 printed15–16/PDF21–22](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS2_e.pdf#page=21) and [supplement printed67/PDF70](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf#page=70) specify three objectives: perimeter concept; measuring/comparing two-dimensional boundaries; square/rectangle formulae. Simple regular and irregular shapes, estimation after measurement experience, and composite square/rectangle outlines are included. Formula derivation through measuring and recording sides is supporting evidence. Exclude crossed/self-touching outlines, protruding branches and holes from required perimeter calculation. Concave simple L/U shapes are not excluded. More than five operands may occur without complicated arithmetic.

## Geometry and independent review requirements

New perimeter4 figure schema separates ordered boundary vertices, labelled edge lengths and internal seams. A common affine scale preserves mathematical lengths. All required polygons must be simple, connected, positive-area and without holes; concavity is permitted. Independent verification compares vertex-sum perimeter with exposed-unit-edge counting for rectilinear unions, checks actual diagram labels/scales and tests rotation/translation invariance.

Anchor examples: L boundary22cm;4×3 and2×2 rectangles sharing2cm give18cm, not22; a6×5 outer shape with a2-wide,3-deep exterior notch has28cm, not its bounding-box22. Shared edges must cancel twice. Square side5 and rectangle8×2 both have20cm perimeter, demonstrating different shapes can have equal perimeters.
