# 6M5: Area (III)

## Learning coverage

2 numbered objectives. Tasks explore equal-circle-piece rearrangements, A = πr², and half-, quarter- and three-quarter-circle areas. Finite curved pieces only approximate a rectangle-like shape; they are not asserted to form an exact rectangle. Calculations state π = 3.14 or 22/7. Inverse radius from area and general sector formulas are not required.

Sources: [EDB 2020 KS2 notes](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS2_e.pdf#page=62) and the [2017 primary mathematics supplement](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf).

## Digital families

- `circleareamodel6a`: 12 variants; choice; equal-sector dissection supports circle-area formula; finite shapes not exact rectangles.
- `circlearea6a`: 12 variants; numeric cm²; circle area with explicit π = 3.14 or 22/7.
- `fractioncirclearea6a`: 24 variants; numeric cm²; half/quarter/three-quarter areas via fractions, not general sector formula.


New scalar responses use explicit exact integer, decimal or lowest-term fraction contracts; earlier graders are unchanged. Easy offers a method cue, Normal preserves independent application, and Difficult adds a contextual check. These are provisional teaching demands, not empirically validated difficulty ratings.
