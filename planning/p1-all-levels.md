# P1 question levels: all twelve units

## Decision and delivery

**Easy / Normal / Difficult** describes the demand of a task, not a child's ability. Numeric variations do not create a progression by themselves. Existing core questions have not all been relabelled easy; adult fluency is not evidence of difficulty for a beginning learner.

The initial 1N1–1N2 pilot was published in [6ebc99d](https://github.com/kochiuyu/mathquiz/commit/6ebc99d43cde8775854cc0445ab73f3b0bb8c17d), with 72 questions and 53/53 local/public checks. The all-P1 extension provides **432 tasks: 12 units × 3 levels × 12 tasks**. This includes **396 automatically marked questions** in eleven units and **36 teacher-observed investigations** in 1F1. It adds 360 tasks and preserves the original 72 question payloads.

[Open P1 levels](../p1-levels.html). Every unit is available from the selector and its main resource-browser group. Each level has three task formats with four versions each. Four reproducible three-task sets partition the twelve tasks; teachers can also select all twelve. English and Traditional Chinese routes, worked keys and pupil papers use the same IDs and order.

These remain **provisional teaching levels**, not calibrated difficulty ratings, pupil labels or mastery thresholds. No timer, ability ranking, promotion algorithm or negative marks are used. Adults may read prompts aloud. More reading is not intended to be the source of difficulty.

## Curriculum limits and source audit

Re-read the official [EDB KS1 explanatory notes, PDF pp.11–24](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS1_e.pdf#page=11) for the ten added units on 18 November 2026, alongside the repository's unit briefs. The first two units retain their earlier pp.7–10 audit. The website fetch timed out; the same official PDF was downloaded and its relevant text extracted locally.

| Unit | Boundary / what screen answers do not establish |
|---|---|
| 1N1 | Quantities/positions within 20; unit counting; verbal comparisons; positive one-digit parts, total at most 18. A different bond is more than a swap. |
| 1N2 | One-digit parts, total at most 18; inverse/check tasks within 10. No negatives, column algorithms or unknown-letter equations. |
| 1N3 | Through 100; grouped collections below 100 using 2/5/10; no multiplication or inequality symbols. Numeral recognition is not listening evidence. |
| 1N4 | At most two-digit operands, sum below 100, no-borrow subtraction. Two-number stories only; no bracket notation or required written explanation. |
| 1M1 | Two lengths/distances, equal units, no gaps/overlap, aligned starts. Distance is straight separation. Straighten cords without stretching. |
| 1M2 | Prices/totals at most HK$10; no change. Exchange within {10¢,20¢,50¢,$1} or {$1,$2,$5,$10}. Price readings, not decimal calculations. Labelled tokens test values, **not real coin appearance**. |
| 1M3 | Whole-centimetre scales; both endpoints matter. Schematic diagrams **must not be physically measured**. Body references vary between people. |
| 1M4 | Hour/half-hour readings; whole-hour durations at most 12; finish/duration, not finding start time. Calendar retrieval, no date differences, minute arithmetic, 24-hour notation or leap-year rules. November 2026 is a fixed calendar. |
| 1S1 | Five broad families. No subtype names, oblique solids, nets, construction or face/edge counting. Touch/roll/stack needs real models. |
| 1S2 | Points, lines, simple shapes, three to six straight sides and circles. No angle measures or regular-polygon requirement. Drawing thickness is not mathematical breadth; composition uses the outside boundary. |
| 1S3 | Simple pictures, fixed reader viewpoint, seven positional terms. No facing-person reversal, compass/turns or complicated relational puzzles. |
| 1F1 | Integrated inquiry, not a separate content course. No fixed score or claim to complete a pupil's ten-hour programme. No multiplication, general formula or formal proof. |

Every unit links to an existing hands-on/oral activity. Screen answers do not demonstrate physical measurement, listening, real-coin recognition, drawing, solid handling or an inquiry process. Diagram descriptions help, but shape drawings need suitable tactile/real-model alternatives when a pupil cannot inspect them. A matching model must not disclose the requested name.

## Inquiry assessment

Each level has four counter totals, four prepared measurement setups and four triangle-piece counts. The guidance changes with level. Preparation and reference counts are in the key; pupil measurement pages do not reveal the prepared counts. Adult preparation is required.

Pupils record predictions, attempts, checks and revisions using speech, drawings or the optional text area. Notes transfer on a deliberate language switch in the same browser session. **Notes are not saved to a server or retained after leaving/reloading.** Use **Print / save this record** first. The paper view is a blank sheet; the worked key gives preparation and criteria. Another set resets the record.

Teachers record independent / supported / not yet / not observed, with evidence and prompts. These are observation categories, not points. A final number alone cannot demonstrate inquiry; 1F1 has no automatic mark. Revision is part of reasoning, not a penalised error. Teacher reference counts are not pupil targets.

## Marking, routes and compatibility

- Marked questions give credit per response part. Blanks are not marked; retry clears the attempt; repeated submission cannot add credit. Feedback explains the idea. Whole-number fields accept full-width digits, leading zeros and equivalent `.0` forms, rejecting prose and scientific notation.
- URLs include unit, level, set, view and language. IDs/order are deterministic. Invalid routes refuse a substitute set. Language changes preserve answers and submitted state.
- This remains **separate from the mixed-quiz builder**, with 432 separate IDs. It changes neither core family/variant counts nor resource-completion flags. The original 72 payloads and core generator/marking payloads are preserved.
- Browser print / Save as PDF is supported. Direct PDF download and mixed-builder integration are not included. Pupil paper and key are separate views; no fixed page count is promised.

## Classroom evaluation

Teachers can freely vary the support after teaching. Record first attempts, prompts/read-aloud, representation, misconceptions and explanation. Compare a shared concept, not raw marks from sets with different part counts. Review reading/access problems before relabelling mathematics.

Check whether normal adds application and difficult adds reasoning, whether choices are unambiguous and whether children can express understanding without excess language. Use that evidence to revise tasks before automatic placement or mixed-builder integration. Other year groups are outside this P1 release.
