## Access and reuse boundaries

- Search results pointed to older Radin Mas P3 slides, Unity P6 parent material and a Fern Green P3–P5 workshop PDF. Direct requests returned 404, so they are not counted as reviewed questions.
- Singapore MOE's syllabus PDF returned a robot-verification page. Its contents were not reviewed in this pass; no attempt was made to bypass verification.
- NCETM identifies separate copyright holders for text and artwork. No blanket reproduction permission is assumed for any source. Use independent wording, values and diagrams, with a source link.
- UK Year 2, Singapore P3 and Hong Kong P2 are not interchangeable. Map prerequisites, number ranges and vocabulary to the Hong Kong curriculum before assigning a grade.
- Source region describes provenance, not difficulty or official endorsement. Keep **MathQuiz adaptations / MathQuiz改編練習**, task format and source attribution separate.

## Four additional original draft candidates

These extend the previous ten Hong Kong drafts. The current unpublished queue is **14 candidates: 11 Hong Kong, two UK and one Singapore**. They are not 14 approved new skills. Existing-skill labels were compared, but a full generated-question duplication and prerequisite review remains necessary.

### HK011 — Repair an equal-total grid

Suggested HK P3 optional extension. Source: EdUHK primary monthly problem above. Related work: addition and the existing linked-clues collection.

**English:** Each row, column and both diagonals should total 18. Exactly one entry was copied wrongly. Repair the grid.

**中文：**每行、每列及兩條對角線的總和都應是18。有一個數抄錯了，請改正。

```
 9   2   7
 4   6   9
 5  10   3
```

**Answer:** Change the second-row, third-column 9 to 8. All eight lines then total 18. Independent enumeration of single-cell replacements from 0–30 found only this repair. This is an original error-checking task, not a reproduction of the source's letter puzzle.

### UK001 — Move a place-value counter

Suggested HK P1 optional extension of `tensones1a`; source: NCETM Year 2 PDF page 10.

**English:** Put exactly five counters into Tens and Ones columns; either column may be empty. List all represented numbers. Starting with all counters in Ones, move one at a time to Tens. How much does the number increase each move?

**中文：**把剛好5粒圓片放在十位和個位欄，任何一欄都可以留空。列出所有表示的數。開始時全部放在個位，每次移一粒到十位；每次數值增加多少？

**Answer:** 5, 14, 23, 32, 41, 50. Each move removes 1 and adds 10, increasing the value by 9. All six allocations were enumerated. This draft adds an explicit counter-transfer explanation; future artwork must be original.

### UK002 — Pair the two leftovers

Suggested HK P1 teacher-supported extension of `oddeven100a`; source: the NRICH task above.

**English:** One tray has 7 counters and another has 11. Pair counters within each tray, then combine the trays. How many pairs are there? Explain why no counter is left unpaired.

**中文：**一個盤有7粒圓片，另一個有11粒。先在每盤內把圓片兩粒一組，再把兩盤合起來。共有多少組？說明為甚麼沒有圓片落單。

**Answer:** Nine pairs. Three pairs and one single, plus five pairs and one single, become nine pairs. A finite check of odd counts 1–99 also passed, but the general explanation comes from joining the two single counters. Teacher judgement is needed for an unrestricted pupil explanation.

### SG001 — Find the smaller amount

Suggested HK P2 application extension; source: Haig Girls’ School PDF page 17. Compare subtraction and mixed-story banks, including `mixedstory3digit2a`.

**English:** The red box has 139 beads, which is 52 more than the blue box. How many beads are in the blue box? How many are in both boxes altogether? Show the comparison with a bar model.

**中文：**紅盒有139粒珠子，比藍盒多52粒。藍盒有多少粒？兩盒合共有多少粒？請用線段圖表示兩者的比較關係。

**Answer:** Blue: 139 − 52 = 87; both: 139 + 87 = 226. Check: 87 + 52 = 139. Independent story, values and unknown direction; an original bar diagram is still required before release.
