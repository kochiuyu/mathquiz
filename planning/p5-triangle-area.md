# P5 triangle area · family brief

Status: live from 16 November 2026 as `triarea5a`. Manual pupil and hands-on screen-reader checks remain deferred.

## Curriculum match

- **Primary 5, 5M1 Area (II), objective 2.** The [2017 EDB learning-content supplement](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf#page=87) requires recognising and using the area formula for triangles, parallelograms and trapeziums. The [2020 Key Stage 2 explanatory notes](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS2_e.pdf#page=41) link the formula to a base and its corresponding perpendicular height. This family covers only the area of a triangle with a labelled base and height, not the whole unit.
- **Prerequisites and limit:** Pupils multiply whole numbers and divide an even product by two. All lengths are in centimetres, and every answer is a whole number of square centimetres. The schematic drawing is not to scale. No missing-side equation or physical measurement is requested.

## Bilingual item contract

| Element | English | 繁體中文 |
|---|---|---|
| Prompt | Find the area of the triangle. Its base is 8 cm and its perpendicular height is 5 cm. | 求三角形的面積。底邊長8厘米，對應的垂直高是5厘米。 |
| Printed answer | `____ cm²` | `____ 平方厘米` |
| First hint | Use the base and the perpendicular height, not the sloping side. Multiply them, then divide by 2. | 用底邊和對應的垂直高，不要用斜邊。相乘後再除以2。 |
| Worked step | `8 × 5 ÷ 2 = 20 cm²` | `8 × 5 ÷ 2 = 20 平方厘米` |

Twelve permanent variants use small whole-number dimensions and whole-number areas. Each shows an outlined triangle, a labelled horizontal base, a dashed perpendicular height, and a right-angle marker. The diagram's accessible description names the base and height without stating the area. One mark requires the exact whole-number numeral, including full-width digits; reject decimals, calculations and typed units. Practice gives one targeted hint and one retry, then the worked solution and two distinct follow-ups. Check all variants against an independent `base × height ÷ 2` calculation, correct/wrong/blank answers, bilingual builder/pupil/paper/key/practice, old links, phone width and direct PDF.
