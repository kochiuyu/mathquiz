## Models, records and marking

SVG scales have ten equal intervals with labelled endpoints/midpoint, a visible water boundary and an equivalent text description. They are illustrative, not calibrated physical tools. A changing water level leaves the example full capacity fixed. Pouring examples distinguish valid comparisons from spilled trials; equal/different cup controls explain why counts can mislead. The 100 mL model starts empty, reaches 1000 mL = 1 L after ten additions and restarts without saving marks.

The two-container notebook requires an already-measured reference, new target and original estimate with unit before accepting the measured result, tool, reflection and optional help. Original records cannot be replaced by later measurement. Full capacity, rather than incidental current contents, is requested. Human observation of tool use is separate from these self-reports.

First answers and one supported retry remain separate. Inquiry predictions/plans precede results and remain intact; the investigation tests whether a taller container must hold more. Each topic/learner has its own record. Existing length notebooks retain their original IDs and behavior. Damaged, future and oversized data remain protected, stale tabs cannot overwrite, and blocked storage is reported as temporary. Models earn no marks.
