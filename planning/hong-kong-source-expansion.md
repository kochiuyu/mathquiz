## Original bilingual drafts

Suggested HK years are provisional prerequisites, not a direct transfer of a source's grade label. Every draft is an extension or alternative presentation of existing mathematics. Explanation quality needs teacher judgement; numeric checks alone do not establish mastery.

### MQ-HK012 — Recurring calendar dates · P1 optional · H1

**English:** In a fictional 30-day month, the 1st is Monday. A reading club meets every Tuesday and Thursday. From the 1st to the 14th inclusive, on which dates does it meet? One stamp is given at each meeting: how many stamps? What date and weekday is three days after the last meeting?

**中文：** 在一個虛構的月份中，全月有30天，1日是星期一。閱讀小組逢星期二和星期四聚會。由1日至14日（包括首尾兩天），聚會日期是哪幾天？每次聚會獲一個印章，共有多少個？最後一次聚會的三天後是幾日、星期幾？

**Answer / 答案:** 2, 4, 9, 11; four stamps; 14th, Sunday。2、4、9、11日；4個印章；14日星期日。

Progression: highlight one weekday → follow two weekly schedules → correct an inclusive-date error. Supply a clear original calendar. Compare the P1 Time activity and `tsacalendar3a`; the cumulative P3 assessment label does not itself make calendar reading P3-only.

### MQ-HK013 — What does the quotient count? · P2 · H2

**English:** A: Share 24 markers equally among 6 pots. B: Put 24 markers into pots with 6 markers in each. Both use 24 ÷ 6 = 4. In each story, what does 4 count?

**中文：** 甲：把24枝畫筆平均放進6個筆筒。乙：把24枝畫筆放進筆筒，每筒放6枝。兩題都用24 ÷ 6 = 4。每題的「4」分別表示甚麼？

**Answer / 答案:** A: four markers per pot. B: four pots。甲：每筒4枝。乙：4個筆筒。

Progression: match pictures and units → compare both stories → repair a correct numeral with a wrong unit. **Merge into existing practice:** `divgroup2a` already checks quotient/divisor units; compare with `share2a`. The contrast is a teaching option, not a new skill.

### MQ-HK014 — Enough containers for every object · P2 · H2

**English:** There are 26 paintbrushes. Each holder can hold at most 4. What is the smallest number of holders needed to hold every brush? Explain why 6 holders are not enough.

**中文：** 有26枝油畫筆，每個筆筒最多可放4枝。要放好所有油畫筆，最少需要多少個筆筒？解釋為甚麼6個筆筒不夠。

**Answer / 答案:** Seven; six hold at most 24, leaving two brushes。7個；6個最多放24枝，餘下2枝需要另一個。

Progression: draw the partly filled final holder → find the minimum → contrast full packs with enough containers. The source asks about full packets; this is our explicitly different application. Compare `divremain2a`, `divgroup2a` and HK005 (completed equal sharing). Check contextual reading demand before implementation.

### MQ-HK015 — A correct total can hide a wrong chart · P2 · H3

**English:** The fictional lost-property list is: pencil, ruler, eraser, pencil, pencil, eraser, ruler, pencil, eraser, pencil. A pictogram uses one symbol per item and shows pencils 5, erasers 2, rulers 3. Correct it. Does a correct total of 10 prove each category is correct?

**中文：** 一份虛構失物記錄依次是：鉛筆、間尺、擦膠、鉛筆、鉛筆、擦膠、間尺、鉛筆、擦膠、鉛筆。象形圖每個圖形代表一件物品，卻畫成鉛筆5件、擦膠2件、間尺3件。請改正。總數10件正確，是否代表每一類都正確？

**Answer / 答案:** Pencils 5, erasers 3, rulers 2; no—the incorrect categories also sum to 10。鉛筆5件、擦膠3件、間尺2件；不是，錯誤分類的總數也可以是10件。

Progression: classify records → complete symbols → repair a same-total error. `pict2a` already checks categories and total counts; the proposed addition is an audit against the original unordered list. Create new icons. The source lesson and slide example counts differ; our data is independent and does not combine them.

### MQ-HK016 — Height is not the sloping side · P5 · H4

**English:** A parallelogram has base 6 cm, sloping side 5 cm and perpendicular height 4 cm to that base. A pupil uses 6 × 5 = 30 cm². Correct the calculation and explain which length must be perpendicular to the chosen base. Does rotating the card change its area?

**中文：** 一個平行四邊形的底是6厘米、斜邊是5厘米，而該底所對應的高是4厘米。一位同學用6 × 5 = 30平方厘米計算面積。請改正，並說明哪一條長度必須與所選的底垂直。把圖卡轉動後，面積會改變嗎？

**Answer / 答案:** 6 × 4 = 24 cm². Use the perpendicular height; rotation preserves area。底乘對應高，面積24平方厘米；轉動後面積不變。

Progression: highlight a right angle → match base/height → explain the wrong method on a rotated card. **Direct overlap confirmed:** `heightmatch5a` and `paraarea5a` already reject the sloping-side misconception. Keep this as an alternative visual/context variant. Coordinates (0,0), (6,0), (9,4), (3,4) give the stated dimensions; these are implementation notes, not pupil text. Original diagram still required.

### MQ-HK017 — Same area, different perimeter · P4 · H4

**English:** Cut a 6 cm by 4 cm rectangle into two 3 cm by 4 cm rectangles. Join them along their 3 cm edges, with no gap or overlap, to make an 8 cm by 3 cm rectangle. Find area and perimeter before and after. Which stays the same?

**中文：** 把一張6厘米 × 4厘米的長方形紙分成兩張3厘米 × 4厘米的長方形。把兩張紙沿3厘米的邊拼接，不留空隙、不重疊，拼成8厘米 × 3厘米的長方形。拼砌前後的面積和周界各是多少？哪一項不變？

**Answer / 答案:** Area stays 24 cm²; perimeter changes from 20 cm to 22 cm。面積都是24平方厘米；周界由20厘米變成22厘米。

Progression: count tiles → calculate perimeter → reject the claim that equal area implies equal perimeter. H4 uses P5 parallelogram rearrangement; our independently simplified rectangle task requires P4 area/perimeter concepts. Compare `areaformula4a`, `perimeterformula4a` and the garden collection. Physical cutting, if used, requires teacher observation; the program must not claim pupils performed it.

### MQ-HK018 — Equal rounded values, different exact counts · P5 optional · H5

**English:** Fictional library records show 1,260 books borrowed in April and 1,340 in May. A chart rounds both values to the nearest hundred. What values appear? A pupil says borrowing was exactly equal. Is that justified? Find the actual difference.

**中文：** 一組虛構圖書館記錄顯示：4月借出1260本書，5月借出1340本。統計圖把兩個數都四捨五入至百位，會顯示甚麼數？有同學說兩個月借書量完全相同，這個說法成立嗎？實際相差多少本？

**Answer / 答案:** Both display 1,300; May's exact count is 80 greater。都顯示1300本；實際5月多80本，不能說完全相同。

Progression: round values → compare raw and rounded records → explain lost precision. `barapprox4a` already distinguishes exact and rounded counts; compare `compoundround5a`, `multidigitround5a` and `chartcritique6a`. This is an extension, with a possible P6 chart-critique follow-up. Grade and rounding-place scope still need a full objective check.

### MQ-HK019 — Repair proposed pie-chart angles · P6 · H6

**English:** A fictional activity budget is $120: $40 saved, $50 for materials and $30 for travel. A proposed pie chart labels these sectors 120°, 180° and 90°. Can this represent the budget? Correct the wrong angle and verify the total.

**中文：** 一份虛構活動預算共有120元：儲起40元、材料費50元、交通費30元。有人建議圓形圖的三個圓心角依次是120°、180°和90°。這能表示該預算嗎？請改正錯誤的角度，並驗算總和。

**Answer / 答案:** No: 390° is impossible for one full pie. Materials should be 150°; 120° + 150° + 90° = 360°。不能，原來合共390°；材料費應是150°，改正後合共360°。

Progression: match a share to a sector → calculate a missing angle → verify both angles and amounts. Compare `pieread6a`, `tsapie6a`, `chartcritique6a`. Present the invalid proposal as a table of labels, not a geometrically possible whole pie; draw the corrected chart independently. Never request pupils' actual household budgets.

## Priorities and verification

Prioritise HK015, HK014, HK017 and HK019 for the next implementation review: they add raw-record checking, interpretation of a remainder, conservation reasoning and representation repair. HK012 and HK018 are useful contextual variants. HK013 and HK016 should merge into existing skills because direct overlap was found in current source code.

**Eight of eight draft answer checks passed** in [the reproducible checker](../tools/check-hk-research-drafts.py): calendar enumeration; two group models; minimum-container enumeration; independent frequency counts; polygon coordinates and rotation; unit-square areas and exposed-edge perimeters; rounding interval; rational pie-angle calculations and inverse amounts. These are draft correctness checks, not browser, accessibility or classroom tests.

The comparison covered current directory metadata and selected actual generated-feedback code in `p2-skill-data.js`, `p4-skill-data.js`, `p5-skill-data.js` and `p6-skill-data.js`. It was not an exhaustive comparison of all generated variants. Full curriculum mapping, original diagram rendering, bilingual editorial review, marking design, browser checks and pupil trials remain pending before any draft enters pupil practice. No runtime files or marking rules change in this research release.
