## Sources and scope

Checked 16 November 2026 against [EDB 2017 supplement, PDF pages 9–11](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf#page=9) and [2018 KS1 notes, printed pages 1–2](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS1_e.pdf#page=7). Objectives below are paraphrased; the linked source remains authoritative.

Use numbers 1–20; count forward/backward in steps of one. Compare quantities and number magnitude without inequality signs; the numerical difference belongs to 1N2. Number bonds use two one-digit parts with totals 2–18, with hands-on work, oral presentation and symbolic recording. The names of the three arithmetic signs are required, but formal names for ordinal/cardinal concepts are not. Here symbolic statements record a composition/decomposition rather than calculation drills. Zero parity is addressed in the 1N3 extension, not added as a separate 1N1 objective.
