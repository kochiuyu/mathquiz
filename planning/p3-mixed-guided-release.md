## Source and teaching scope

Reread the existing official [EDB KS1 notes, PDF52–53 / printed46–47](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS1_e.pdf#page=52) and [earlier source audit](p3-four-operations-i.md). No new download is claimed. The four objectives cover brackets, three-number addition/subtraction through four digits, multiplication with addition/subtraction of at most four numbers, and problems. Two separate bracket pairs are included; nested brackets and division mixed calculations are not required here. All four given equality forms are recognised concretely; symbolic conversion and distributivity application belong to 4N5. Estimation follows exact-method practice. The [existing activity](../activities/p3-four-operations-i.html) and earlier banks remain unchanged.

## Models and records

The column model keeps the value 4052 across exchanges, including moving one thousand through a zero-hundreds place, before showing subtraction and the following addition. Intermediate and final response fields retain their roles; swapping values is not accepted. Counter diagrams distinguish circles/squares by shape and removed objects by crossing-out, with textual group descriptions. Both sides of a supplied equality describe the same collection. Choosing the other factor order and adding/removing counters demonstrates the four patterns without asking learners to transform an expression.

Temporary model actions are unscored and unsaved. The estimate notebook reuses the existing topic-isolated record contract: the first estimate/reason cannot be replaced, exact reveal requires acknowledgement of paper calculation, and a later reflection stays separate. Acknowledgement is self-report, not observed evidence. First responses and one supported retry, practical observations and initial inquiry predictions retain their separate meanings. The inquiry asks pupils to predict and test bracket placements, including a case that does not change, retaining unsuccessful trials. Earlier number limits, 24-hour notation and practical notebooks are preserved.

These were repaired before publishing. A new static gate and stronger browser assertions check intact text and complete rendered expressions in both languages. Browser checks cover both languages, all task stages/models, four widths, practical records, estimate/reveal/reflection, new topic links and compatibility. Full bar-chart and numbers browser suites are included; other topic browser suites are not fully rerun. Earlier resources are compared against the current baseline.
