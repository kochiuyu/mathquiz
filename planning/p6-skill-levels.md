## Exact response contracts

New scalar fields retain the scoped P6 decimal, whole numeral or lowest-term fraction/mixed-number rules. Algebra construction requires the directly stated relationship on corresponding sides, not merely the same solution. Valid spaces around operators remain supported; mixed algebra coefficients use explicit parentheses such as (1+1/2), not ambiguous adjacent numerals. Original shape/data counts and pct2/mean1 retain legacy numeric equivalents. speed6a retains its strict whole-numeral measurement grader. Extra fields declare their own representation.

## Sampling

All 12 variants are used for a 12-variant family. For 24-variant families, indices 0,1,6,7,8,9,14,15,16,17,22,23 represent both parity-dependent units/π approximations and all applicable subtype cases. Legacy families with 15 or16 variants use deterministic floor(i×count/12) sampling. This includes all six equation forms and all four division combinations. The detailed per-skill map is [p6-level-sampling.json](p6-level-sampling.json). No invented diagram adaptations are used. Retired pct1 is not selectable.

## Independent contextual checks

Number checks include equivalent division, first-operation intermediates, visible-precision bounds, purchase shortfalls, percent complements/changes, cross products, and substitution of a different explicitly stated candidate. Bounds are explicitly not-above/not-below so exact multiples are unambiguous. Measures checks distinguish curved/straight boundaries, original/opposite turns, tank area and depth rise, graph segment change, constant-rate counterfactuals and unshaded circle areas. Shape/data checks use actual symmetry axes, mirrored grid distance, redistribution, totals, alternate intervals, plotting errors, missing group totals and specific chart-purpose limitations. Some checks are single-step intermediates rather than a separately difficult problem; no empirical difficulty claim is made.

## Practical objectives and limits

Measuring/drawing angles, physical displacement, symmetry construction, speed observations, chart drawing and inquiry remain teacher-guided activities with blank observations. Smart responses cannot prove these practical acts occurred. A completed Smart session means three first-try whole-task answers per tier without hints; it is not lasting mastery.

Completion metadata retains 69 previously completed units; all P6 units remain incomplete pending live verification. Legacy routes and the Smart engine are preserved.
