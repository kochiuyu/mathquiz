## Learning coverage

2 numbered objectives. Tasks connect 1 cm³ with 1 mL and 1 m³ with 1,000 L, use internal cuboid dimensions, and measure displacement by cup readings, tank rise or collected overflow. Direct displacement requires full submersion, an insoluble object, and complete collection/readings without trapped air.

Sources: [EDB 2020 KS2 notes](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS2_e.pdf#page=58) and the [2017 primary mathematics supplement](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf).
