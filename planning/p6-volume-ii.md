# 6M2: Volume (II)

## Learning coverage

2 numbered objectives. Tasks connect 1 cm³ with 1 mL and 1 m³ with 1,000 L, use internal cuboid dimensions, and measure displacement by cup readings, tank rise or collected overflow. Direct displacement requires full submersion, an insoluble object, and complete collection/readings without trapped air.

Sources: [EDB 2020 KS2 notes](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS2_e.pdf#page=58) and the [2017 primary mathematics supplement](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf).

## Digital families

- `capacityvolume6a`: 12 variants; numeric stated unit; cm³/mL and m³/L; cuboid capacity.
- `displacement6a`: 24 variants; numeric cm³; measuring-cup difference, tank rise, overflow collected water.
- `displacementmethod6a`: 12 variants; choice; full submersion, floating/dissolving/overflow method validity.


New scalar responses use explicit exact integer, decimal or lowest-term fraction contracts; earlier graders are unchanged. Easy offers a method cue, Normal preserves independent application, and Difficult adds a contextual check. These are provisional teaching demands, not empirically validated difficulty ratings.
