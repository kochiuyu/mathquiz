# P6 speed after converting minutes · family brief

Status: live as `speed6a` from 16 November 2026. Manual classroom, translation, physical printer and hands-on screen-reader checks remain deferred at the user's request.

## Curriculum match and limits

[EDB 2020 Key Stage 2 explanatory notes, 6M4 Speed](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS2_e.pdf#page=60) include interconversion between hours and minutes (objective 1), recognition of km/h (objective 7), and speed problems (objective 9). The notes explicitly give 90 minutes = 1.5 hours and advise against complicated calculations. This family combines those objectives: convert a single minute quantity to hours, then find speed as distance divided by time. This is a project interpretation, not full coverage or EDB endorsement.

Twelve permanent variants use positive whole-kilometre distances and times from 15 to 180 minutes, excluding 60 minutes so conversion is always needed. Every result is a whole number from 48 to 72 km/h. No chasing problem, travel graph, m/s↔km/h conversion, acceleration, rounded answer or mixed hours-and-minutes interval is included. Prerequisites are dividing minutes by 60, decimal or fractional hours, and distance ÷ time.

One mark requires the exact whole-number numeral for the speed. Outer spaces and full-width digits work; a typed unit, decimal, calculation or fraction does not. A blank stops submission. The printed answer key shows the time conversion and distance/time calculation. Practice uses distinct 30-, 90- and 105-minute cases, one targeted hint, one retry, a worked step and a separate first-try count.
