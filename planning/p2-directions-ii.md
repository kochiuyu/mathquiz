# P2 Directions and positions (II) · 2S3

**Integrated local candidate.** Source and finite mathematics/geometry independently reviewed. Live browser verification and publication pending; no unit-completion, pupil-mastery or delivered-inquiry-hours claim.

## Source audit

Checked 16 November 2026: [EDB supplement PDF 39, printed 36](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf#page=39) and [KS1 notes PDF 44, printed 38](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS1_e.pdf#page=44).

Two objectives: recognise east/south/west/north and use a compass to measure directions. Required short forms are E/S/W/N. Notes require sentences describing one object to the stated direction of another, actual compass measurement and walking in the measured direction. Left/right turns may extend P1 relative-position knowledge. Four main directions only: no eight-point compass, numerical bearings, map scale or magnetic-declination calculations.

## Activity and evidence

[English](../activities/p2-directions-ii.html?lang=en) · [Chinese](../activities/p2-directions-ii.html?lang=zh) · [English key](../activities/p2-directions-ii.html?lang=en&key=1) · [Chinese key](../activities/p2-directions-ii.html?lang=zh&key=1)

| Requirement | Pupil evidence |
|---|---|
| Objective 1: names/abbreviations | Task 1 matches and locates E/S/W/N on a compass, gives clockwise order and opposite pairs. |
| Relative-position sentences | Task 3 describes each object relative to O, then reverses reference/target. Turning the body does not move objects or rotate geographical directions. Task 5 records a north-arrow sketch and full sentences. |
| Objective 2: actual compass measurement | Task 2 holds an actual compass level, waits, identifies the north-seeking end using that instrument's instructions, finds four directions, marks and rechecks after turning. |
| Walking along measured directions | Task 4 walks two small steps north then, after stopping and turning/checking, two east under adult supervision. |
| Optional left/right application | Task 4 contrasts right when facing north (east) with right facing south (west), without further walking. |

Provide a reliable compass with instructions, removable O/N/E/S/W floor cards and labelled A/B/C/D objects. Adult checks compass and clear routes beforehand, away from roads/stairs/water/vehicles and magnetic interference. No blindfolds; stop to read rather than walking while looking down. If readings are unreliable, change location/tool instead of proceeding. Cards are placed following measured directions, not used as a substitute for measuring. Do not assume the north-seeking needle's colour is universal.

Two pupil and two hidden teacher sheets per language. Observable rubric records independent/prompted/not-yet/not-observed, actual actions/words and assistance. A mobility adaptation may have a partner walk from the pupil's instructions, but record the pupil's compass reading separately and do not claim independent walking. Sketch answers cannot prove real compass use.
