## Provisional modelling

[Chinese primary draft](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/2_Pri_Modelling_Explanatory%20Notes.pdf) is explicitly provisional. [Circular 10/2026](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EDBC%202026010_E_Math%20Modelling.pdf) plans KS2 implementation in 2028/29, earlier pilots possible, mathematical topics unchanged. Inquiry offers forward-compatible guided modelling with pupil-selected assumptions, interpretation and light checking. It does not claim delivered hours, memorised stages, a required second cycle or pupil mastery. No automatic AI interaction or pupil personal-data collection is included.
