## Cross-year dependencies

| Thread | P4 foundation | P5 development | P6 application |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Number size and estimation | Place value revisits; rounding chart data to tens/hundreds | Larger places and unknown-quantity estimation | Sensible estimates in calculation and measures |
| Fractions | Equal wholes beyond one, common factors/multiples, same-denominator operations | Unlike fractions, multiplication, division and quantity comparison | Decimal conversion, percentages and non-integral equations |
| Decimals | Meaning to four places; bounded addition/subtraction | Multiplication and rounding | Division and mixed operations |
| Algebra | Explain a quantity relationship in words/models | One unknown → expressions → balanced equations | Non-integral coefficients, brackets and original amounts |
| Area | Covering, standard squares, rectangle rules | Perpendicular heights and polygon rearrangement | Circle area; missing lengths through equations |
| Circles and solids | Shape properties and construction | Circles before spheres; cubes/cuboids before volume rules | Circumference, circle area, capacity/displacement |
| Time and speed | Revisit P3 clock/time work as needed | Fraction/decimal quantities support later rates | Time conversions → fair speed comparisons → travel graphs |
| Data | Larger bar-chart scales and retained raw counts | Large-number labels and compound charts | Averages, lines, pies, appropriate displays and critique |

These are useful-first connections, not a demand to finish a whole previous grade before a new branch. In particular, P5 algebra and P6 symmetry can start alongside number work. Teach P6 line-graph reading before travel graphs, and introduce percentage meaning before percentage-based pie questions.

## Boundaries to preserve

- P4 decimal meaning reaches four places; that does not make four-place arithmetic compulsory. P5/P6 calculation limits are checked in their own units, including after scaling a divisor.
- P4 fractions use common denominators. P5 unlike-fraction work precedes mixed fraction applications. General three-number L.C.M. algorithms are not a new prerequisite.
- Original amounts from known fractional/percentage parts belong in equations. Do not put fractional relative-change or excluded percentage-rate questions into the required arithmetic routes simply because an assessment bank has varied contexts.
- P5 circle naming/drawing does not require π. Circle calculations are P6; arbitrary-angle arcs/sectors, finding a radius from area and cube roots are not compulsory primary work.
- A drawn chart, measured angle, physical net or displacement experiment needs actual pupil evidence. Selecting a supplied picture cannot substitute for construction or tool use.

## Shared pupil and record design

Keep short English and Traditional Chinese instructions, one immediate action and a clear next control. Retain needed mathematical conditions. Help is optional; required reasoning is visible. Adult interpretation and optional records open separately. Apply the [pupil-copy guidance](learner-wording.md) from the first P4 implementation.

Separate year/topic/pupil progress, first answers and supported retries. Preserve original estimates/predictions and later findings. Keep bank scores, lesson marks, physical observations and inquiry records distinct. Reuse the shared learner controls without changing earlier pupil records. Set lesson counts and question inventories during each implementation, except where a first-release contract already specifies them.
