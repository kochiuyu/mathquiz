# 6M4: Speed

## Learning coverage

9 numbered objectives. Tasks cover all nine objectives: single-unit time conversions, intervals, speed meaning, intuitive/direct/improvised comparisons, standard speed units, travel/rest graphs and distance–speed–time problems. No m/s–km/h conversion or pursuit problem is required. Practical comparisons use safe toy or walking observations, not dangerous races.

Sources: [EDB 2020 KS2 notes](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS2_e.pdf#page=60) and the [2017 primary mathematics supplement](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf).

## Digital families

- `timeconvert6a`: 12 variants; numeric stated time unit; h/min and min/s both directions.
- `timeinterval6a`: 12 variants; two numeric fields; start/end/duration, hours and minutes.
- `speedmeaning6a`: 12 variants; choice; speed concept, standard and improvised unit meaning.
- `speedcompare6a`: 12 variants; choice; same-time/same-distance and fair improvised comparison.
- `speedgraph6a`: 24 variants; numeric stated unit; distance-time graphs, travel/rest segment interpretation.
- `speedproblem6a`: 24 variants; numeric stated unit; speed/distance/time, same-unit contexts and simple time conversions.


New scalar responses use explicit exact integer, decimal or lowest-term fraction contracts; earlier graders are unchanged. Easy offers a method cue, Normal preserves independent application, and Difficult adds a contextual check. These are provisional teaching demands, not empirically validated difficulty ratings.

## Practical work

The [bilingual activity](../activities/p6-speed.html) supplies visible pupil tasks, hidden teacher models and blank observation records. Scored questions support understanding but do not certify measuring, construction or real-world observations.
