# P5 addition of unlike fractions · family brief

Status: live from 16 November 2026 as `fracadd5a`. Manual pupil and hands-on screen-reader checks remain deferred.

## Curriculum match

Primary 5, **5N2 Fractions (III), objective 2**: add and subtract not more than three fractions with different denominators. The [2017 EDB learning-content supplement](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf#page=76) cautions against complicated calculations; the [2020 Key Stage 2 notes](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS2_e.pdf#page=31) explain finding common denominators and reducing answers. This family assesses only the addition of two proper fractions with unlike denominators. It does not claim the entire unit.

## Bilingual item contract

| Element | English | 繁體中文 |
|---|---|---|
| Prompt | Calculate `1/2 + 1/4`. Write the answer as a fraction in simplest form. | 計算 `1/2 + 1/4`。把答案寫成最簡分數。 |
| Answer | `3/4` | `3/4` |
| First hint | Find a common denominator. Rewrite both fractions before adding the numerators. | 先找公分母，把兩個分數通分，再把分子相加。 |
| Worked step | `1/2 = 2/4`; `2/4 + 1/4 = 3/4`. | `1/2 = 2/4`；`2/4 + 1/4 = 3/4`。 |

Twelve permanent variants use small denominators, integer least common multiples and sums no greater than one. Each has two labelled answer fields, numerator and denominator. Accept full-width digits; require a positive denominator and lowest terms. An equivalent unsimplified answer gets a reduction hint. No typed slash or units are needed. Practice starts with the example, allows one retry after a hint, then shows the worked solution and two distinct follow-ups. Paper and key derive from the same record. Test all variants, marking boundaries, both languages, paper/PDF, old links and phone width. Classroom and hands-on screen-reader checks are deferred.
