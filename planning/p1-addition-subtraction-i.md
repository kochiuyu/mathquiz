# P1 addition and subtraction (I): first released forms

**Source check:** 16 November 2026. The [EDB 2017 supplement, 1N4](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf#page=14) and [2018 Key Stage 1 explanatory notes](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS1_e.pdf#page=13) specify two-digit addition including carrying, two-digit subtraction without borrowing, addition results below 100, and checking subtraction by addition. The same unit also includes three-addend addition, the grouping property of addition, column forms and simple word problems.

## Released task forms

`carryadd1a` has twelve permanent pairs of two-digit addends. Every ones-column sum is at least ten and every total is below 100. Pupils write the sum as a whole-number numeral. The worked step explicitly regroups ten ones as one ten, then adds the tens. A wrong answer that omits the carried ten is a **hypothesis** to probe, not an observed error.

`noborrow1a` has twelve permanent two-digit subtractions. In every pair, the minuend's units digit is at least the subtrahend's, so no borrowing is required. Pupils write the difference; the key subtracts units and tens separately and verifies with difference plus subtrahend. A wrong answer that reverses a units subtraction is likewise a hypothesis.

Both families have one-mark, exact-numeral online scoring, with full-width digits accepted and decimals, calculations, typed units and leading zeros rejected. The teacher builder, pupil quiz, paper, worked key and three-question practice share the same IDs. Practice has a targeted first-error hint, one retry, a worked step and a first-try count. The written prompt does **not** assess pupils' column-form layout or spoken explanations.

**Still open in 1N4:** addition of three numbers, grouping of addends, column forms of addition and subtraction, and simple two-number word problems. The released forms also need an objective-by-objective review before any full-unit claim. Manual classroom, translation, physical printer and screen-reader checking remains deferred at the user's request.
