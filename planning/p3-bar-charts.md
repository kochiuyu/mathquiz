# P3 reading a bar chart · item brief

Status: **live since 15 November 2026** as `bar3a`. The twelve permanent variants, one-field marking rule, paper, key and practice follow this brief. Manual pupil and hands-on screen-reader checks remain deferred.

## Curriculum match

- **Year and unit:** Primary 3, 3D1 “Bar charts (I)”. [EDB's 2017 primary supplement, objective 2](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf#page=57) requires interpreting bar charts. Objective 1 is recognising them; objective 3 is constructing them. The first family assesses one interpretation task: read one named bar's value from a chart.
- The [2018 Key Stage 1 explanatory notes, 3D1](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS1_e.pdf#page=68) limit the charts to one-to-one, one-to-two and one-to-five representations and require both horizontal and vertical forms. They also advise equal bar widths and spacing. This brief includes all three scales and both orientations.
- **Prerequisite and limit:** Pupils can read whole-number axis marks and identify the requested category. A single bar-reading question does not assess collecting data, constructing a frequency table or chart, comparing categories, the whole 3D1 unit, or pupil mastery. The curriculum match is this project's interpretation of EDB sources, not EDB approval.

## Released item

Show a simple titled bar chart of three familiar categories. Ask how many pupils are represented by one named bar. Draw a zero origin, labelled value axis, regular grid lines and equally wide, equally spaced bars. Keep the chart's unit as pupils. For horizontal charts, categories label rows and the numerical axis runs left to right; for vertical charts, categories label columns and the numerical axis runs bottom to top.

| Element | English | 繁體中文 |
|---|---|---|
| Pupil prompt | How many pupils chose **walking**? Read the walking bar and write the number. | 有多少名學生選擇**步行**？讀取「步行」的棒形，寫出人數。 |
| First example | The axis counts by 2. The walking bar reaches the fourth step after zero, so `4 × 2 = 8` pupils. | 數軸每格代表2人。「步行」的棒形由零起到第四格，所以 `4 × 2 = 8` 人。 |
| First wrong-answer hint | Find the named bar. Check what one axis step represents, then count the steps from zero to its end. | 找出題目指定的棒形。先看數軸每格代表多少，再由零數到棒形末端。 |

**Chart and access contract:** Use a solid bar with clear contrast in grayscale, distinct grid lines, printed axis numbers and an axis title. The three category labels must remain readable on a 360-pixel phone and A4 paper. The accessible chart description identifies orientation, category labels and numerical scale, then describes each bar's endpoint by tick position, without simply announcing the answer. The question and chart stay together on paper. The answer field is labelled “Number of pupils” / “學生人數”; the printed unit is “pupils” / “名學生”. Paper, key and practice derive from the same permanent item record.

**Released variants:** Twelve stable `bar3a-0` through `bar3a-11` records. Four charts use a one-to-one scale, four use one-to-two and four use one-to-five. Within each scale, use two horizontal and two vertical charts. Each has three categories with distinct, whole-step bar lengths; include one zero bar, at least one bar reaching the top labelled value, and target categories in all three positions. Store scale, orientation, bar step counts, category labels, target index, chart title, bilingual prompt and worked step. Old item IDs and links remain unchanged.

## Marking, practice and release checks

- **Online:** One mark for the exact whole-number pupil count of the named bar. Accept surrounding spaces and full-width digits after normalisation. Reject a bare step count when the scale is 2 or 5, decimals, calculations and typed units when the unit is printed next to the field. Require a response, including explicit `0` for a zero bar. Apply the rule only to the new family.
- **Paper and key:** Print a blank followed by “pupils” / “名學生”. The key names the target bar, scale, step count and multiplication. Check zero, top value and both orientations in English and Chinese PDF output.
- **Practice:** Start with a scale-of-2 chart where walking is 8 pupils, then offer the scale-counting hint after a wrong first try and one retry before the worked key. Follow with distinct charts using scales of 1 and 5, including the other orientation. Track first-try success separately.
- **Automated release checks:** Enumerate all twelve records. Independently verify scale and orientation coverage, equal bar widths/spacing, endpoint alignment, answer = target steps × scale, bilingual labels and worked keys. Exercise correct, wrong, blank and full-width answers; hint/retry and distinct practice follow-ups; phone width; English/Chinese A4/PDF; old IDs and links. Manual pupil and hands-on screen-reader checks remain deferred and must not be marked complete.
