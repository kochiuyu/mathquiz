# 6N4: Percentages (II)

## Learning coverage

1 numbered objective. Relationships as percentages of a stated whole, quantities represented by percentages, and amounts after stated increases or decreases.

Sources: [EDB 2020 KS2 notes](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS2_e.pdf#page=54) and the [2017 primary mathematics supplement](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf).

## Digital families

- `pctrelation6a`: 12 variants; Find a percentage of a stated whole.
- `pctquantity6a`: 12 variants; Find the quantity for a percentage.
- `pctchange6a`: 12 variants; Amounts after percentage changes.

## Response and practice contracts

`number6.js` parses bounded nonnegative integer, decimal and lowest-term fraction/mixed-number responses using exact rational arithmetic. Each field declares its representation; units and scientific notation are not silently accepted as numeric input. Earlier grading contracts remain unchanged.

Every family provides bilingual hints that do not simply reveal the canonical answer. Easy adds a method cue, Normal retains independent application, and Difficult adds contextual intermediate calculations or checks. Difficulty labels remain provisional.

## Practical work

The [bilingual activity](../activities/p6-percentages-ii.html) contains six task groups, visible supplies, hidden teacher models and 1 blank objective record per language. No pupil observation or mastery is prefilled.

## Verification limits

Independent rational/AST, actual generated HTML, assessment and minimal-DOM practice-recovery checks cover these families. Minimal-DOM testing is not browser verification.
