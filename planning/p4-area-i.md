## Geometry and inference requirements

New area4 rendering uses mathematical coordinates and a common isotropic scale for paired/overlay shapes. Equal tiles must be non-overlapping and cover the stated region exactly. Standard unit meaning does not imply physical screen calibration. Direct strict containment requires remaining uncovered area; equality requires equal occupied regions under the given placement. Partial overlap alone does not establish the comparison.

Independent cell-center inclusion/union counts must agree with a separate shoelace area and rectangle row-count oracle. Test L18cm², joined rectangles16cm² and U24cm²; shared boundary has zero area, unlike a duplicated overlap region. If overlapping rectangles are used, subtract their overlap once. Unit-square labels, inverse answer units and actual rendered coordinates must agree. Standard-unit comparisons use one common unit, not hidden conversion.

Indirect comparisons must hold under every assignment satisfying the premises. A smaller than B and C makes A smallest but does not determine B versus C. Do not count raw tiles of unequal area as equivalent units.
