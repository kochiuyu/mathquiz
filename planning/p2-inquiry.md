## Genuine investigations and scope

| Opportunity | Pupil choices and observable process | Related P2 mathematics |
|---|---|---|
| A · useful equal packing | Choose and justify a usefulness criterion; predict; plan systematic trials across four allowed pack sizes; physically test; check multiplication/division records; compare with another criterion; preserve corrections; transfer total 12 to 14 and limit conclusions. | 2N3 multiplication and 2N6 grouping division/remainders. Divisors 2–5, quotients 2–7. No algebra, two-digit divisors or counting all partitions. |
| B · personal-reference length estimates | Choose/calibrate a personal reference; agree endpoints and measurement method; record estimates before measurement; compare different pupils' references; choose a possible improvement; record original-method AND revised-method estimates for the same new target before measurement; compare both with its measured length, accepting equal/worse outcomes without claiming causal or universal benefit. | 2M1 metre/centimetre measurement and ever-ready ruler estimation. Actual tools and objects, no decimal metres or formal error calculation. |

A is open in criteria, reasoning and method, even though the finite trial outcomes can be checked. Ways mean pack sizes and their counts/remainders, not counter identities or pack order. All four permitted sizes are required for a complete trial record, not every possible partition. B has no fixed numerical answer; measured endpoints, references and honest prior estimates matter. Both methods must estimate the same new target with agreed endpoints before its measurement is revealed. Comparing an old target with a new target would confound method and target. Equal or worse revised estimates are valid findings; this small comparison establishes neither universal benefit nor causation.

Teacher-only finite checks for A: sizes 2/3/4/5 give quotient/remainder 6/0,4/0,3/0,2/2 for 12 and 7/0,4/2,3/2,2/4 for 14. Seven trays or separated group spaces are necessary. A “fewest packs” criterion must specify whether leftovers are allowed; the rubric accepts reasoned differing recommendations rather than imposing one preferred answer.

B uses two safe indoor straight targets longer than one metre, at least one with nonzero centimetre remainder, and ruler/tape plus an independently calibrated personal reference. No climbing, sensitive body-data collection or required public sharing. No fixed centimetre answer because physical objects differ.

## Evidence and honest status

Four pupil sheets and two hidden teacher sheets per language. Process rubric records question/criterion, prediction, controlled plan, real trial records, checks/revisions, evidence-backed communication and bounded transfer, including the same-target two-method estimate comparison and honest equal/worse outcomes. Use independent/prompted/not-yet/not-observed with actual pupil artefacts. Communication support is permitted but transcription and manipulation help are recorded separately. Correct arithmetic alone is not inquiry evidence.

No digital family is proposed: preset numerical choices would not establish the open decisions, physical investigation and communication required here. Static HTML/key/script and eight finite quotient/remainder checks performed locally; source/mathematical independent review, native translation, mobile/print rendering, practical classroom trials and the wider integrated time record remain pending. No mastery or full-curriculum closure claim.
