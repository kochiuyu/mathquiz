All responses request a reason and a record of assistance. Reasons, drawings and assistance are not automatically marked. Blank assistance means independence is unknown. Calendar, solid bases, angle construction, physical reference estimates and investigation evidence are explicitly sampled in the practical stations, not falsely counted as automated coverage.

## Comparison with earlier materials

Compared proposed prompts with 88,082 earlier strings/activity documents, including guided/practice/check data, worked examples, the broader skill bank and all 15 P2 activity sheets. A token-overlap shortlist helped inspect close examples; it is not a semantic novelty proof. Exact final prompt strings do not occur in earlier data. Manual checks included the activity teacher keys and associated teaching examples.

The first draft's successive subtraction resembled the 63−28−16 sticker exercise and its exchange example used the taught 52−27. These were revised: C1 now infers an unknown event from a final stock; C2 diagnoses a faulty representation of 61. T2 now judges a deadline rather than just entering a finishing time; G2 repairs an incomplete grouping. M2 no longer states the equal totals in its prompt.

Remaining similarities are deliberate: N2 is still boundary counting, G1 uses known facts, G2 still uses complete groups/remainders, M1 is change, S1 uses taught properties and S2 uses a turned north reference. These are fresh contexts and response demands, not a claim that every item is independent transfer. Choice answers and provided properties remain scaffolds. Adults should use new physical examples and ask for reasons; a matching response does not certify mastery. The paper station prompts require new data, words and models, not copying teacher keys.

## Records and interaction

- Versioned key `mathquiz-p2-review-v1-<0|1|2>`; test key includes `test-`. P1/P2 score, cursor, notebook and inquiry keys are never written by this page.
- First response, explanation and help are committed before feedback appears. Exactly one explicit supported retry is stored separately; neither can replace the first. No total score or entry lock.
- Eight stations store their latest observation: independent / prompted / not yet demonstrated / not observed, date, exact task/actions, pupil words and help. Non-default observed status requires date/task. Longer observation history belongs on paper; this is not an append-only assessment log.
- Other form drafts survive a redraw caused by submission. Drafts are not saved across navigation; the browser warns before leaving a changed form where its user-gesture policy permits. Save submitted work before language or learner changes.
- Revisions reject stale cross-tab submissions, preserve the local draft and ask the adult to copy/reload. This is a local-browser convenience, not multi-device sync or a transactional database.
- Blocked storage keeps volatile work with a warning. Unreadable, future-version or oversized data is protected until explicit reset. A failed write keeps subsequent work in memory until reload, avoiding silent overwrites.
- Reset requires opening its disclosure and ticking confirmation. Only this learner's review clears. Reading/transcription help is not itself a mathematical failure.
