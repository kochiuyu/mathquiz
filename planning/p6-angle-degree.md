# 6M1: Angle (degree)

## Learning coverage

3 numbered objectives. Angles are named by their vertex and rays. Tasks cover degrees, straight/reflex/round turns, rotated dual-scale protractors and angle comparisons independent of ray length. Measuring and drawing tasks do not use 0° or 360° as targets.

Sources: [EDB 2020 KS2 notes](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS2_e.pdf#page=56) and the [2017 primary mathematics supplement](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf).

## Digital families

- `angleconcept6a`: 12 variants; choice; degree, angle naming, straight/reflex/round turns.
- `protractor6a`: 24 variants; numeric degree; actual rotated dual-scale protractor; non-reflex/reflex measurement.
- `anglecompare6a`: 12 variants; choice; compare measured angles independent of ray lengths.


New scalar responses use explicit exact integer, decimal or lowest-term fraction contracts; earlier graders are unchanged. Easy offers a method cue, Normal preserves independent application, and Difficult adds a contextual check. These are provisional teaching demands, not empirically validated difficulty ratings.

## Practical work

The [bilingual activity](../activities/p6-angle-degree.html) supplies visible pupil tasks, hidden teacher models and blank observation records. Scored questions support understanding but do not certify measuring, construction or real-world observations.
