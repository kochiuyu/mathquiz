# P5 circle parts · family brief

Status: **source-checked draft, 16 November 2026**. The `circle5a` family is planned; it is not yet released. Manual pupil and hands-on screen-reader checks remain deferred.

## Curriculum match

Primary 5, **5S1 Circles, objective 1** asks pupils to recognise the concept and basic properties of circles. The [2017 EDB learning-content supplement](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf#page=89) includes centre, radius, diameter and circumference. The [2020 Key Stage 2 explanatory notes](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS2_e.pdf#page=45) explain that a radius joins the centre to a point on the circle and a diameter passes through the centre with both endpoints on the circle. This proposed family identifies one pictured radius. It does not assess drawing circles or the whole unit.

## Proposed bilingual item

| Element | English | 繁體中文 |
|---|---|---|
| Prompt | Which labelled line segment is a radius of the circle? Choose one. | 圖中哪一條標示的線段是圓的半徑？選一個。 |
| Worked explanation | A radius joins the centre to a point on the circle. The diameter passes through the centre and joins two points on the circle. | 半徑由圓心連到圓上的一點。直徑穿過圓心，兩端都在圓上。 |
| First hint | Find the centre, then look for a line ending on the circle. | 先找圓心，再找另一端在圓上的線段。 |

Propose nine permanent variants with a circle, marked centre and three clearly lettered segments: one radius, one diameter and one chord not through the centre. Rotate the segments and vary answer positions; keep endpoints unambiguous and line weights distinct in grayscale. Give the SVG a description of geometry without identifying the radius by answer letter. One mark for exactly one selected letter, with a blank response prompting completion. The key explains the defining property. Practice should offer the targeted hint, one retry and two distinct follow-ups. Before release, enumerate variants, verify segment endpoints and centre relations, bilingual rendering/marking, paper/PDF, keyboard access, phone width and old links. Manual checks remain deferred.
