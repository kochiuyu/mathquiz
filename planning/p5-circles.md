# P5 circle parts · family brief

Status: **live, 16 November 2026**. The `circle5a` family has nine permanent variants. Manual pupil and hands-on screen-reader checks remain deferred.

## Curriculum match

Primary 5, **5S1 Circles, objective 1** asks pupils to recognise the concept and basic properties of circles. The [2017 EDB learning-content supplement](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf#page=89) includes centre, radius, diameter and circumference. The [2020 Key Stage 2 explanatory notes](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS2_e.pdf#page=45) explain that a radius joins the centre to a point on the circle and a diameter passes through the centre with both endpoints on the circle. This family identifies one pictured radius. It does not assess drawing circles or the whole unit.

## Proposed bilingual item

| Element | English | 繁體中文 |
|---|---|---|
| Prompt | Which labelled line segment is a radius of the circle? Choose one. | 圖中哪一條標示的線段是圓的半徑？選一個。 |
| Worked explanation | A radius joins the centre to a point on the circle. The diameter passes through the centre and joins two points on the circle. | 半徑由圓心連到圓上的一點。直徑穿過圓心，兩端都在圓上。 |
| First hint | Find the centre, then look for a line ending on the circle. | 先找圓心，再找另一端在圓上的線段。 |

Nine permanent variants have a circle, marked centre and three clearly lettered segments: one radius, one diameter and one chord not through the centre. The drawings rotate by quarter turns and vary the answer among A, B and C. Endpoints are unambiguous and segment lines are heavier than the circle outline in grayscale. The SVG describes the geometry without identifying the radius by answer letter. One mark requires exactly one selected letter; a blank response prompts completion. The key explains the defining property. Practice offers a targeted hint, one retry and two distinct follow-ups. Automated release checks enumerate variants, verify segment endpoints and centre relations, bilingual rendering and marking, paper/PDF, keyboard controls, phone width and old links. Manual checks remain deferred.
