# P6 TSA 2026 question-variety release

Reviewed 19 November 2026. All eight supplied English/Chinese P6 papers were read in full. These are P6 papers, distinct from the earlier P3 release. The covers explain that P6 TSA in 2026 was suspended and these materials were offered for voluntary participation. Copyright and assessment provenance remain with their stated owners; MathQuiz uses original values, contexts and diagrams rather than reproducing the official questions.

EDB curriculum objectives remain the teaching framework. HKEAA develops and administers TSA on behalf of EDB. The reference papers describe assessment demands; they do not create new curriculum units. `6R1` is a local cumulative P4–P6 review group. No completion flags or official 85-unit inventory entries are changed.

## Teaching and marking boundaries

Easy provides a cue; Normal supplies a standalone practice task. Difficult adds an optional MathQuiz reasoning check and is labelled as an extension. These levels are MathQuiz teaching choices, not HKEAA difficulty classifications. A task may combine demands that appear separately in official papers; a reference BC does not certify every field or extension in the task.

Select-all questions use a Yes/No response for each candidate. Equation questions mark the selected model and numeric solution; composite-area and equation papers provide working space for teacher review. Graph completion is adapted to scale labels, numeric heights, missing categories and title choices. The online score cannot assess handwriting, construction quality or free-form working, and is not the official TSA marking scheme. Paper and worked-key views preserve diagrams and source notes. These practice sets are not timed official mock papers.

## Verification

`tests/p6-tsa-static.cjs` checks all 540 additions with independent arithmetic/geometry oracles, bilingual twelve-context levels, answer-format validation, source labels and catalogue metadata. `tests/p6-tsa-baseline.json` freezes the hash of all 1,872 original P6 question payloads. Existing P6, P3 source/extension and shared Smart tests are retained.

The public browser harness is [P6 TSA checks](https://kochiuyu.github.io/mathquiz/tests/p6-skill-levels.html?scope=tsa): 90 family/level/language cases plus directory, Smart progression, narrow-layout, partial-credit, language-transfer and shared P3 source regressions. Live results are recorded after deployment in `p6-tsa-live-evidence.json`. Physical printing and classroom trials are outside this release’s verification.
