## Teaching and verification limits

Easy, Normal and Difficult describe provisional support and reasoning demands, not empirically validated difficulty or lasting mastery. A completed Smart session records the session's first-try whole-task criterion, not permanent competence. Practical tasks retain blank observations; digital answers cannot establish that measurement, drawing, construction or real-world investigation occurred.

Physical printing, classroom trials, independent translation and screen-reader validation remain unverified. Official sources and unit-specific boundaries are recorded in the linked unit summaries.
