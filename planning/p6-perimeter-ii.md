# 6M3: Perimeter (II)

## Learning coverage

2 numbered objectives. Tasks explore circumference-to-diameter ratios, use C = πd = 2πr, recover radius or diameter from circumference, and total exposed curved and straight boundaries. Each calculation states π = 3.14 or 22/7. Semicircle and quarter-circle boundaries use fractions of a circle, not a general angle-to-arc formula.

Sources: [EDB 2020 KS2 notes](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS2_e.pdf#page=59) and the [2017 primary mathematics supplement](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf).

## Digital families

- `piratio6a`: 12 variants; numeric; circumference/diameter model, explicit approximate pi.
- `circlecircum6a`: 12 variants; numeric cm; circumference from radius/diameter.
- `inversecircle6a`: 12 variants; numeric cm; radius/diameter from circumference.
- `circleboundary6a`: 24 variants; numeric cm; full semicircle/quarter boundaries and stadium/semicircle-on-rectangle composites.


New scalar responses use explicit exact integer, decimal or lowest-term fraction contracts; earlier graders are unchanged. Easy offers a method cue, Normal preserves independent application, and Difficult adds a contextual check. These are provisional teaching demands, not empirically validated difficulty ratings.
