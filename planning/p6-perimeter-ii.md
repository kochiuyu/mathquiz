## Learning coverage

2 numbered objectives. Tasks explore circumference-to-diameter ratios, use C = πd = 2πr, recover radius or diameter from circumference, and total exposed curved and straight boundaries. Each calculation states π = 3.14 or 22/7. Semicircle and quarter-circle boundaries use fractions of a circle, not a general angle-to-arc formula.

Sources: [EDB 2020 KS2 notes](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS2_e.pdf#page=59) and the [2017 primary mathematics supplement](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf).
