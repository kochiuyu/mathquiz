## Chart tools and learner records

Charts include title, unit, zero, seven numbered ticks, equally spaced intervals and equal bar widths/gaps. Accessible descriptions identify orientation, unit, scale and endpoint intervals. Zero bars retain category labels. No colour recognition is needed. The model scale button compares different example datasets with the same bar-interval lengths; it explicitly does not pretend to rescale one fixed dataset.

The raw-card model contains 22 simulated responses (4 Walk, 8 Bus, 10 Train). Its table stays hidden until revealed; 正 represents a complete group of five, with | for remaining marks. These supplied cards never stand in for observed real collection.

Each builder orientation starts with blank title, unit, categories, counts and scale. Large samples 4/8/10 fit scales 2 and 5 on the six-interval grid; small samples 1/3/5 permit scale 1 too. Multiple valid scales are accepted. Too-small grids are refused without changing frequencies. Values between grid lines keep exact endpoints rather than rounding. A new sample explicitly clears that temporary chart; clearing or switching orientation preserves unrelated observation drafts. Models are temporary, unscored and not saved. Required labels and numerical matches are checked; adult review is still needed for the meaning of a title/unit and explanation.

Actual collection, writing, drawing and help are recorded in existing topic/learner observations and inquiry fields. First answers, one supported retry and initial inquiry predictions remain separate. Earlier practical notebooks, numeric/24-hour response rules and progress are unchanged. Damaged/future/oversized records are protected, stale writes refused, and blocked storage labelled temporary.
