# P5 cuboid volume · family brief

Status: live from 16 November 2026 as `volume5a`. Manual pupil and hands-on screen-reader checks remain deferred.

## Curriculum match

- **Primary 5, 5M2 Volume (I), objective 6.** The [2017 EDB learning-content supplement](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf#page=88) requires using formulae for the volume of cubes and cuboids. The [2020 Key Stage 2 explanatory notes](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS2_e.pdf#page=43) connect the formula to counting unit cubes and explicitly exclude finding a cube's side from its volume. This family covers labelled cuboids with whole-centimetre edges only.
- **Prerequisites and limit:** Pupils multiply three whole numbers and recognise `cm³`. No conversion between `cm³` and `m³`, missing dimension or composite solid is assessed. The drawing is schematic, not to scale.

## Bilingual item contract

| Element | English | 繁體中文 |
|---|---|---|
| Prompt | Find the volume of the cuboid. Length 4 cm, width 3 cm, height 2 cm. | 求長方體的體積。長4厘米、闊3厘米、高2厘米。 |
| Printed answer | `____ cm³` | `____ 立方厘米` |
| First hint | Count the cubes in one layer: length × width. Then multiply by the number of layers (height). | 先算一層有多少個小立方體：長乘闊；再乘層數（高）。 |
| Worked step | `4 × 3 × 2 = 24 cm³` | `4 × 3 × 2 = 24 立方厘米` |

Twelve permanent variants show a clean isometric cuboid with length, width and height labels. The accessible description names the three dimensions without giving the volume. One mark requires the exact whole-number numeral; accept full-width digits, reject decimals, calculations and typed units. Practice provides a one-layer hint, one retry, a worked multiplication and two distinct follow-ups. Check all variants against an independent product, correct/wrong/blank marking, bilingual builder/pupil/paper/key/practice, old links, 360-pixel width and direct PDF. Manual checks remain deferred.
