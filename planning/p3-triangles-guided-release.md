## Scope

[EDB notes, PDF pages 66–67 / printed 60–61](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS1_e.pdf#page=66) were rechecked. Four objectives cover five named categories, relationships, strict side-length feasibility, and drawing/making. Equal-side and right-angle properties are checked separately. Equilateral triangles are included among isosceles triangles; isosceles-right triangles retain both parent names. No acute/obtuse classification, degree measures, angle sums, Pythagoras or formal proof is required of pupils. [Existing activity sheets](../activities/p3-triangles.html) remain linked and unchanged.

Five lessons provide 45 practice tasks, ten guided examples and ten fresh sample checks. Questions specify a property or complete name set rather than demanding one exclusive name. Fresh checks use new prompts/length triples and visibly different orientations; static checks compare actual rendered cards, not only source scale values. This is a sample check, not an independent readiness certificate.

## Models and marking

Exact source models drive category membership. Equilateral geometry is represented analytically and converted to numeric SVG coordinates only for display. Right-isosceles and other templates use exact source geometry; rendered rotations do not redefine their names. Equal-side ticks and right-angle squares are independently checked against SVG coordinates. Text descriptions provide the same checked properties without relying on colour.

The builder starts empty and accepts three whole-number model lengths from 1 to 30, including full-width digits. This is an interface range, not a curriculum limit. It rejects missing/out-of-range inputs and displays no triangle for failed side sums, including equality. Valid lengths generate a proportional diagram with AB/AC/BC lengths stated. Actual drawing and making require tools/materials and separate observation; generating a diagram does not award practical competence.

All models are temporary and unscored. First answers and one supported retry remain distinct. Observation records and original prediction/plan are separate from scores. The investigation fixes two strips, predicts possible third lengths, records successful and unsuccessful trials, then changes the fixed pair. Damaged/future/oversized records are protected, stale tabs cannot overwrite saved work and unavailable storage is clearly temporary. Earlier learner records, question-bank IDs and scoring stay unchanged.
