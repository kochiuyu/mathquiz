## Exact source and existing evidence

Checked16November2026: [supplement PDF51, printed48](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/pmc2017_e.pdf#page=51) and [KS1 notes PDF58, printed52](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/ma/curr/EN_KS1_e.pdf#page=58). Title is **Time(III)**, not the24-hour-clock unit3M4. Three objectives: recognise second(s); measure/compare time intervals in seconds; solve simple time-interval problems.

Crucial separation: objective2 actual measurements have **no fixed interval limit**, but must be reasonable; encourage estimates. Objective3 word problems involve **whole hours≤12 or whole minutes≤60 only**, not seconds arithmetic. Given any two of start/end/duration, find the missing quantity. Inverse starting-time problems extend P2, which required finish/duration only.60seconds=1minute and actual one-second experience belong here. No24-hour notation conversion required.

Existing builder has no3M2family. P2 `timefinish2a`/`timeelapsed2a` cover minutes≤60 and remain prerequisite evidence, not evidence of new inverse solving or actual seconds measurement. Do not duplicate those families solely to increase counts.
