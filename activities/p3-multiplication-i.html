P3 · Multiplication and place value

Name ______ Date ______ · Use after P2 prerequisites. Prepare ten tens-rods, thirty ones, a place-value chart, paper and pencil. Rods/cards represent values.

1 · Explain the column method

Model two groups of 43. Separate tens and ones, then combine. Write 43 × 2 in column form, aligning places. Explain how the tens and ones products give the total, and why the tens digit of the product goes below the 4. Next model 27 × 3, exchanging ones for tens when needed. Explain the carried quantity, not just the small written digit.

2 · Calculate and show your thinking

Set out and calculate 126 × 4, 308 × 3, 467 × 3 and 3 × 126 in columns. Explain one carrying step and the role of zero in 308. Compare the method for two- and three-digit factors: what stays the same? Preserve working, not just answers.

Three factors and useful grouping

Use two trays, six cups and twenty-four counters. Keep small objects on the table.

3 · Build and regroup

Place three cups on each of two trays and four counters in each cup. Find the total in two ways: count cups first, or counters on each tray first. Record what the first product counts in each method. Why do both methods count the same collection? Write the three-factor multiplication; grouping may change without changing the factors.

4 · Choose a convenient method

Calculate 4 × 25 × 3 in two groupings: first combine 4 and 25, or first combine 25 and 3. Show both first products and totals. Which was easier for you, and why? You need not name a formal property. Explain why your grouping changes neither the objects counted nor the total.

Problems, then estimation

5 · Choose and explain the operation

Each box holds 24 pencils. How many pencils are in 6 boxes? Each album has 125 stickers. How many stickers are in 4 albums? A school has 3 shelves, each with 4 boxes, each holding 18 books. How many books altogether? Write number sentences and explain the units counted by each intermediate product.

6 · Estimate after practising exact methods

Before calculating exactly, estimate 198 × 4 and 302 × 3 using nearby convenient numbers. State your chosen numbers and whether you expect the exact answer to be above or below the estimate. Then calculate exactly and use the estimates to check whether the results are reasonable. Is an estimate an exact answer? Explain.

Teacher · Script and answers

Do not show this key before pupils attempt the tasks. Use the first tasks to observe reasoning, then offer teaching support if needed and record it. Material totals suffice for 43 × 2 and 27 × 3 separately; reuse/reset between trials. Thirty ones allow 21 ones before exchanging. Cards/rods model value; no huge physical collection is required.

1: 43 × 2 = 86: 40 × 2 = 80, 3 × 2 = 6. The 8 represents eight tens and belongs in the tens column under 4. 27 × 3 = 81: 21 ones become two tens and one one; six tens plus two tens make eight tens. 2: 504, 924, 1401, 378. In 308 × 3 the tens contribution is zero, but 24 ones exchange into two tens and four ones; do not omit the tens place. The same place-value and regrouping principles apply across lengths. No new multi-digit-by-multi-digit algorithm required.

3: two trays × three cups = six cups; six × four = 24 counters. Three cups × four = twelve counters per tray; two × twelve = 24. 4: 4 × 25 = 100, then 100 × 3 = 300; 25 × 3 = 75, then 4 × 75 = 300. The first route may be more convenient; a justified preference is acceptable. Do not require the term associative property.

Teacher · Problems and observation

5: 24 × 6 = 144 pencils; 125 × 4 = 500 stickers. For books, three shelves × four boxes = twelve boxes, then twelve × eighteen is mathematically valid but exceeds this unit’s simple two-factor algorithm. Prefer four boxes × eighteen = seventy-two books per shelf, then three × seventy-two = 216. Accept a valid independently reasoned alternative without requiring a later algorithm. 6: examples 200 × 4 = 800, exact 792 below; 300 × 3 = 900, exact 906 above. Other sensible approximations with reasoning are accepted. Teach estimation after exact multiplication skills, as the source specifies.

小三 · 乘法與位值

姓名 ______ 日期 ______ · 小二先備後使用。準備十條十粒棒、三十粒個位模型、位值表、紙筆。數棒／卡代表數值。

1 · 解釋直式原理

用模型表示兩組 43，分開十和個再合併。把 43 × 2 寫成直式，數位對齊。解釋十位及個位的積如何合成總數，為何積的十位數字寫在 4 下方。再用模型做 27 × 3，需要時把個換成十。說明進位代表甚麼數量，不只說小數字。

2 · 計算及展示思路

列直式計算 126 × 4、308 × 3、467 × 3、3 × 126。解釋一次進位，以及 308 中零的作用。兩位與三位數乘法有甚麼相同原理？保留過程，不只寫答案。

三個數相乘及方便分組

用兩個盤、六個杯及二十四粒圓片，小物件留在桌上。

3 · 實物分組與重組

每盤三杯，每杯四粒。用兩種方法找總數：先數杯數，或先數每盤圓片數。各寫第一次的積在數甚麼。為何兩法都數同一集合？寫三個數的乘法，可改分組但不改因數。

4 · 選方便方法

用兩種分組計算 4 × 25 × 3：先算 4 與 25，或先算 25 與 3。寫兩種第一次的積及最後總數。哪法較方便？解釋，不必說正式運算律名稱。說明改分組為何不改總數。

應用題及估算

5 · 選運算並解釋

每盒 24 枝鉛筆，6 盒共多少枝？每冊貼紙簿有 125 張貼紙，4 冊共多少張？學校有 3 層書架，每層 4 盒，每盒 18 本書，共多少本？寫算式，說明中間結果所數的單位。

6 · 熟習準確方法後估算

未精算前，用鄰近方便數估算 198 × 4 及 302 × 3。說選甚麼數，以及預期準確答案比估值大還是小，再精算，用估值檢查結果合理嗎。估值等於準確答案嗎？解釋。

教師 · 提問及答案

學生先嘗試，不預先展示答案。觀察思路後可教學支援，但記錄援助。模型分別足夠 43 × 2、27 × 3，做完重置；三十粒可先表示二十一個，再換十。數棒／卡代表值，不要求大量實物。

1：43 × 2＝86；40 × 2＝80，3 × 2＝6。8 是八個十，須在十位，對着 4。27 × 3＝81；二十一個換成二十及一個，六十加二十成八十。2：504、924、1401、378。308 × 3 十位本來零，但二十四個換成二十及四個，不能漏十位。各種長度的直式都用相同位值及進位原理，不要求多位乘多位新算法。

3：兩盤各三杯，共六杯，每杯四粒共 24；每盤三杯各四粒共十二粒，兩盤共 24。4：4 × 25＝100，再 × 3＝300；25 × 3＝75，再 4 × 75＝300。首法可能方便，接受有理由的選擇。不要求「結合性質」術語。

教師 · 應用及觀察

5：24 × 6＝144 枝；125 × 4＝500 張。書本可先 4 × 18＝72 本／層，再 3 × 72＝216 本。先 3 × 4＝12 盒再 12 × 18 在數學上正確，但兩位乘兩位算法非本單元要求；接受學生自發合理方法，不把後續算法設為必需。6：例如 200 × 4＝800，準確 792 較小；300 × 3＝900，準確 906 較大。接受合理鄰近估算及解釋。按來源先熟習乘法，再教估算。
