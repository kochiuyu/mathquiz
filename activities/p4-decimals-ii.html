Teacher key

1. 12.34 + 5.60 = 17.94. Align equal places; the decimal point is a separator, not a column to add. For 10.00 − 0.01, exchange one ten for ten ones, one of those ones for ten tenths, and one tenth for ten hundredths: 9 ones, 9 tenths and 10 hundredths before subtracting 1 hundredth. The result is 9.99. Require actual annotated exchange, not only a final answer.

2. First: 3.75 + 0.75 = 4.5. Second: 2 + 2.5 = 4.5. Third: 1.25 + 3.25 = 4.5. Equal-place quantities are combined: addition remains commutative and associative. But 4 − 1.25 + 0.75 = 2.75 + 0.75 = 3.5, whereas 4 − (1.25 + 0.75) = 4 − 2 = 2. The latter subtracts 0.75 instead of adding it, so the proposed regrouping is invalid.

3. 10 − 0.05 = 9.95; then 9.95 + 0.02 = 9.97. Write 10.00 if useful. Work left to right; the final addition restores two hundredths, not subtracts the combined amounts.

4. HK$20 − HK$6.80 − HK$4.25 = HK$13.20 − HK$4.25 = HK$8.95 change. Alternatively total the costs as HK$11.05, then subtract from HK$20. The answer is returned money, not cost. Equivalent decimal notation is valid; currency commonly shows two decimal places.

5. Ribbon: 3.25 m + 1.5 m − 0.75 m = 4.75 m − 0.75 m = 4 m. Jug: 2 L − 0.35 L + 0.2 L = 1.65 L + 0.2 L = 1.85 L. Each uses three numbers in one common unit, without conversion or decimal multiplication/division. Supplied quantities are not observed measurements.

6. One estimate replaces HK$8.65 with HK$9 and HK$5.40 with HK$5, giving about HK$16 change. Exact: HK$30 − HK$8.65 − HK$5.40 = HK$21.35 − HK$5.40 = HK$15.95, close to the estimate. Accept other defensible approximations and explanations; never require 16 or replace the original estimate after exact work. This new problem permits estimation before its exact answer.

教師解答

1. 12.34 + 5.60 = 17.94，相同數位對齊，小數點是分隔符號而非要相加的數位。10.00 − 0.01：一個十換成十個一，其中一個一換成十個十分之一，再把一個十分之一換成十個百分之一；減去一個百分之一前為9個一、9個十分之一及10個百分之一。結果9.99。須有實際退位標記，不能只看答案。

2. 第一式3.75 + 0.75 = 4.5；第二式2 + 2.5 = 4.5；第三式1.25 + 3.25 = 4.5。合併相同數位的量，加法交換性質及加法結合性質仍適用。但4 − 1.25 + 0.75 = 2.75 + 0.75 = 3.5，而4 − (1.25 + 0.75) = 4 − 2 = 2。後式把加0.75變成減0.75，故改組無效。

3. 10 − 0.05 = 9.95，再算9.95 + 0.02 = 9.97。可寫10.00。由左至右計算，最後加回兩個百分之一，不是減去兩量的和。

4. 港幣20元 − 6.80元 − 4.25元 = 13.20元 − 4.25元 = 8.95元找續。亦可先求合共11.05元，再從20元減去。答案是找回的錢而非售價。等值小數寫法有效；金額慣常寫兩個小數位。

5. 絲帶：3.25米 + 1.5米 − 0.75米 = 4.75米 − 0.75米 = 4米。量杯：2升 − 0.35升 + 0.2升 = 1.65升 + 0.2升 = 1.85升。每題三數且同一單位，不需換算或小數乘除。提供的量不是已實測。

6. 一個估計把8.65元看作9元，5.40元看作5元，找續約16元。準確計算30元 − 8.65元 − 5.40元 = 21.35元 − 5.40元 = 15.95元，與估計接近。接受其他有理由的近似值及解釋，不只接受16，不在準確計算後改寫原估計。此新題讓估算先於準確答案。
