P1 · Length and distance (I)

Pupil practical sheet · Name Date

Use two objects at a time. Point, speak or record your responses. Start with A before moving or measuring anything.

A · Look first

Look at strips A and B without moving them. Which looks longer? A / B. Which looks shorter? A / B.

Look from Start towards points C and D. Which looks nearer? C / D. Which looks farther? C / D.

Point along a strip to show its length. Point along the straight gap from Start to C to show a distance. The teacher puts a curved route between Start and C. Is that route the straight distance? Explain or show.

If two people use different-sized units, can their counts alone tell which object is longer? Show your reason.

Teacher setup and evidence

Prepare two strips A/B with clearly different lengths, e.g. 4 and 8 identical block lengths; lay them separately for A. Mark Start, C and D so the straight spans are clearly different, e.g. C at 4 and D at 7 identical large-card lengths. Use a clear desk or safe floor, three markers, a loose curved route string, two non-stretch measuring strings and removable clips to retain spans. Provide two loose cords E/F of clearly different actual lengths; choose E longer and coil it so its endpoint gap is smaller. Provide a choice of identical small blocks/paperclips and identical larger cards. Prepare whole-unit endpoints for the selected units where practical; do not force a fraction or rounding lesson.

A · Observe before tools

For the suggested setup, B is longer/A shorter; C nearer/D farther. Record these judgements before allowing alignment, string or counting. Pupil distinguishes extent of an object from straight separation of points and points to the straight span, not the curved route. Intuitive comparisons must be visually unambiguous.

B · Alignment and curves

Pupil aligns one end of both strips, identifies B. For each distance hold a string straight between the exact points and clip/mark both endpoints; preserve both spans before comparing with starting marks aligned. D is farther. Straighten E/F without stretching; E is longer even if its original endpoint gap looked smaller. Record the procedure, not just the answer. Do not let children tie strings around themselves.

C · Suitable, consistent units

Accept either supplied unit if the pupil can use it sensibly and consistently; larger cards may make long distances less laborious, while small blocks fit short strips. Record the actual chosen unit and teacher-checked counts: suggested block lengths A=4/B=8 and card lengths C=4/D=7, but alternatives need their own counts. Check end-to-end placement, repeated equal size, counting and both comparisons. A shorter unit gives more units for the same span; unequal unit sizes make bare counts incomparable. This final prompt supports understanding, not a separate official objective.

每次比較兩件物件，可指示、口述或記錄答案。先做A，不要先移動或量度。

A · 先觀察

不移動紙帶，看看A和B。哪條看來較長？A／B。哪條較短？A／B。

從起點看看C和D，哪點看來較近？C／D。哪點較遠？C／D。

沿紙帶指出它的長度，再指出起點至C的直線距離。老師在起點和C之間放一條彎曲路線，這條路線就是直線距離嗎？解釋或展示。

教師準備及評估證據

準備長度明顯不同的紙帶A／B，例如分別為4和8個相同方塊的長度，先分開放。標示起點、C、D，直線距離明顯不同，例如C為4、D為7張相同大卡片的長度。用安全而清空的桌面或地面、三個標記、彎曲路線繩、兩條不易伸長的量度繩及可移除夾子保留端點。另備長度明顯不同的活動繩E／F，E較長，但把E彎曲令端點間距較短。提供相同小方塊／回形針及相同大卡片供選擇。盡量準備可用所選單位完整量度的長度，不強加分數或進捨教學。

A · 用工具前觀察

按建議準備，B較長、A較短；C較近、D較遠。在對齊、用繩或點算前記錄判斷。學生能分辨物件的長度與兩點的直線距離，指出直線而非彎曲路線。直觀比較須有明顯差別。

B · 對齊及曲線

學生把紙帶一端對齊，指出B。用繩拉直連接各對準確的點，夾住或標示兩端，保留兩段長度再對齊起點比較，D較遠。把E／F拉直但不拉長；即使原先端點間距較短，E仍較長。記錄過程而不只答案。不可把繩綁在身上。

C · 合適而一致的單位

只要能合理而一致地使用，可接受任何所提供的單位。大卡片量較長距離可能較方便，小方塊適合較短紙帶。記錄實際單位及教師核對的數目：建議為A四個／B八個方塊，C四張／D七張卡片；改用其他單位須另核數目。核對首尾相接、單位相同、點算及兩次比較。同一長度用較短單位，數目會較多；單位大小不同，不能只看數目比較。最後一問為理解支援，並非獨立課程目標。
