5. Experience one hour.

Teacher key and observation rubric

Keep this page separate from pupils. Materials: working analogue clock, class calendar and this sheet. Read prompts aloud as needed; record mathematical understanding separately from reading/writing fluency.

TaskExpected evidence
1A: three o’clock; B: half past seven; C: twelve o’clock; D: half past eleven. Short hand = hour; long hand = minute. At half past, the hour hand is halfway between adjacent hour marks.
2a: five o’clock; b: 2 hours; c: one o’clock; d: Jo is quicker, Kim takes longer. Count-on explanations: 2→3→4→5; 9:30→10:30→11:30; 11→12→1. Do not require a.m./p.m. notation.
37 days: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday; accept any cyclic starting day. 12 months: January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December. About 4 weeks, not exactly 4 in every month.
4a: November 2026; b: Monday; c: 6, 13, 20, 27 November; d: Sunday. This is a dated Gregorian calendar, not a holiday calendar. Monday-first layout is not a claim that every week must start on Monday.
5–6Arrange genuine observations within ordinary supervised school time. Example schedule: A 9 o’clock→10 o’clock; B 10 o’clock→12 o’clock. These are setup examples, not observations to copy. Pupils read the actual clock endpoints and count 1 h or 2 h. “h” means hour. Let them describe activities within one hour. Compare B as longer and A as shorter only if observations match. If timing slips off whole hours, repeat a suitable observation; do not round or fabricate. Do not make pupils work continuously for two hours.

Record each criterion separately

I = independent; P = prompted; N = not yet shown; NA = not observed. No fixed pass score.

教師解答及觀察準則

請把本頁與學生工作紙分開。材料：正常運作的指針時鐘、班級月曆及本工作紙。按需要讀出題目，把數學理解與讀寫能力分開記錄。

題目預期證據
1A：三時；B：七時半；C：十二時；D：十一時半。短針是時針，長針是分針。半點時，時針在兩個相鄰整點刻度的中間。
2a：五時；b：兩小時；c：一時；d：小祖較快，小琴所需時間較長。順數解釋：2→3→4→5；9:30→10:30→11:30；11→12→1。不要求寫a.m.／p.m.。
3七天：星期一至星期日；可由任何一天開始，保持循環次序。十二個月：一月、二月、三月、四月、五月、六月、七月、八月、九月、十月、十一月、十二月。大約四星期，並非每個月恰好四星期。
4a：2026年11月；b：星期一；c：十一月六日、十三日、二十日、二十七日；d：星期日。這是指定年月的公曆，不是公眾假期表。星期一在首欄，不表示每星期必須由星期一開始。
5–6在正常、有教師照顧的上課時間安排真實觀察。安排例子：A九時至十時；B十時至十二時。這些只是安排例子，不是可抄寫的觀察結果。學生讀實際開始及結束鐘面，數出一或兩小時；h表示小時。讓學生描述一小時內的活動。只有實際觀察相符時，才判B較長、A較短。若未能在整小時結束，另作適合的觀察，不要取整或虛構記錄。不要求學生連續工作兩小時。

逐項記錄

獨＝獨立完成；助＝協助下完成；未＝尚未展示；無＝未觀察。不設固定合格分數。
