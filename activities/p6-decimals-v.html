Decimals V: compare representations

1 · Tenths, hundredths and thousandths

Use a grid or place-value model for 0.7,0.35 and 0.125. Write fractions first over 10,100 or 1000, then reduce. Explain why the quantity stays the same.

2 · Mixed quantities

Represent 5.024 using whole units and thousandths. Convert to a mixed number in lowest terms. Create a second measurement example larger than one.

Decimals V: compare representations

3 · Fraction to decimal

Find 3/25 by both expansion to hundredths and numerator division. Compare with 9/8; show whole units explicitly.

4 · Approximate carefully

Convert 2/3 to the nearest tenth and hundredth. Estimate first and state which reports are approximate. No special terminology for repeating digits is required.

Decimals V: compare representations

5 · A rounded tie

Compare 1/3 and 33/100. Both round to 0.33 at two decimal places. Refine the comparison using an extra digit or exact reasoning; explain why the tie does not prove equality.

6 · Own comparison

Choose two measured quantities and represent each as a fraction and a decimal. Predict their order, justify it by two methods and record how rounding could hide a difference.

小數（五）：比較不同表示

1 · 十分、百分及千分

用方格或位值模型表示0.7、0.35、0.125，先寫分母10、100或1000，再約簡，解釋量不變。

2 · 帶分數數量

用整個及千分之一表示5.024，化成最簡帶分數，再自擬大於一的量度例子。

小數（五）：比較不同表示

3 · 分數化小數

用擴分至百分及分子除分母兩法把3/25化小數，再比較9/8，清楚表示整個。

4 · 小心近似

先估2/3，再化成一及兩位小數，指出近似報告，毋須使用重複數字的專門術語。

小數（五）：比較不同表示

5 · 近似值相同

比較1/3及33/100，兩者保留兩位皆為0.33，用更多小數位或精確推理比較，解釋近似值相同不代表相等。

6 · 自擬比較

自選兩量度數，以分數及小數表示，先估大小，用兩法解釋並記錄四捨五入可能掩蓋的差異。
