P2 · Metres in the real world

Name ______ Date ______ · Work with an adult in a clear, safe space. Use real tools, not this printed page as a ruler.

1 · Build one metre

Use a centimetre ruler to mark a strip in ten consecutive lengths of 10 cm. Match each new start to the previous end, with no gaps or overlaps. Compare your strip with a metre ruler or tape. Say and write: 1 m = ____ cm. Explain why a metre is a useful unit for a long room.

2 · Choose, measure and compare

Measure the lengths of two long ribbons, and two straight distances marked between pairs of floor points. Before each measurement, choose a metre ruler, tape measure or trundle wheel and explain your choice. Say where the measurement starts and ends. Show how you keep the tool straight and start at zero; if repeating a ruler, mark each endpoint without gaps or overlaps.

For each of the four measurements record: item/endpoints; tool and reason; result with m and/or cm; partner’s check. Compare the two ribbon lengths, then the two distances: which is longer? Explain using your measurements.

Choose an appropriate unit for each result. If you measure a leftover part in centimetres, record, for example, 1 m 10 cm, not a decimal.

Record, check and estimate

3 · Record the same measured length

A ribbon measures 1 m 10 cm. Write its length in centimetres: ____ cm. Explain with 100 cm strips. Convert only in this direction: 2 m 5 cm = ____ cm; 3 m 40 cm = ____ cm; 4 m = ____ cm. Now select one of your own mixed-unit measurements and record it in centimetres. Do both records describe the same length? Explain.

4 · Calibrate an ever-ready ruler

After the real measurements in tasks 1–2, choose your arm span, arm length or a normal pace as an ever-ready ruler. Measure it twice using a real tool and record both results, with units. Agree an approximate reference length. Keep your arm position or pace consistent. Explain why your partner’s reference length need not match yours.

5 · Estimate first, then test

Choose a new long object and a new straight distance. Before using a measuring tool, estimate each using your calibrated body reference. Record your reference, how many approximate repeats, your estimated length and units. Then measure with a suitable real tool; record the measurement separately without erasing the estimate. Explain any difference and one improvement. Repeat with a second new target if time permits.

Record for each: target/endpoints; body reference and approximate repeats; estimate; tool; measured result; explanation. An estimate is approximate, not a secretly measured answer.

Teacher · Setup, script and feedback

Provide centimetre and metre rulers, tape measures, paper strips, removable floor markers and two ribbons longer than 1 m. Prepare at least one ribbon with a nonzero centimetre remainder (for example, exactly 1 m 10 cm), so task 3 has an actual mixed-unit measurement. A safely operated metric trundle wheel is useful for a longer clear route; demonstrate reset and its scale. Do not require ownership of every tool. Offer a real choice between available suitable tools. Set four clearly identified endpoints. Do not preprint lengths: actual measurements vary. Complete measurement experience before estimation.

Script: “Show your starting point. Why this tool? What will you do when it is too short? How can your partner check?” A tape is useful for a long ribbon; a metre ruler can be repeated on a straight route; a wheel can measure longer clear ground routes. Several choices are valid if the method works. Do not assess tool choice by a single fixed answer.

Task 1: ten lengths of 10 cm give 100 cm = 1 m. Watch joins and compare with the standard. Task 2: check zero, alignment, straightness, endpoints, repeated placements and labelled units. If results disagree, remeasure the same endpoints together. Do not accept a guessed number as measurement evidence.

Task 3 answers: 110 cm, 205 cm, 340 cm, 400 cm. Mixed units combine complete hundreds of centimetres and the remaining centimetres. A response 25 cm for 2 m 5 cm needs a physical two-metre demonstration. Do not ask pupils to convert a single centimetre amount into compound units; do not require decimal notation.

Tasks 4–5: personal references and estimates vary. Observe actual calibration and consistency, then the estimate recorded before tool measurement. Ask “Which reference did you use? What might change your estimate?” Avoid assuming every pace or arm span equals 1 m. Repeatability, a reasoned approximation and reflection matter; no fixed error tolerance is a curriculum mastery rule. Do not turn this into formal multiplication of arbitrary large numbers.

Teacher · Observable evidence

For each criterion record independent / prompted / not yet / not observed, plus actual words/actions. Record spoken reasoning separately from writing. Allow partner recording or a mobility-accessible arm reference, but do not attribute the partner’s measuring skill to the pupil.

小二 · 生活中的米

姓名 ______ 日期 ______ · 在成人指導下於安全、暢通的地方活動。用真正量具，不要把此工作紙當尺。

1 · 製作一米

用厘米尺在紙帶上連續標示十段 10 厘米。每段起點接着上一段終點，不能有空隙或重疊。與米尺或捲尺比較。說出並寫下：1 米＝____ 厘米。說明量度長課室時用米有甚麼好處。

2 · 選擇量具、量度及比較

量度兩條長絲帶的長度，以及地面兩對標記之間的兩段直線距離。每次先選擇米尺、捲尺或測距輪，解釋理由。說出起點和終點，展示量具保持直線及由零刻度開始。如重複移動尺，標記每次終點，不能留空隙或重疊。

每次記錄：物件／兩端位置、量具及選擇理由、以米和／或厘米表示的結果、同伴覆核。比較兩條絲帶，再比較兩段距離：哪一段較長？用量度結果解釋。

為各結果選擇合適單位。如餘下部分用厘米量度，可記作「1 米 10 厘米」，不要用小數。

記錄、核對及估計

3 · 同一長度的記錄

絲帶長 1 米 10 厘米，以厘米記錄：____ 厘米。用每段 100 厘米的紙帶解釋。只做以下方向的換算：2 米 5 厘米＝____ 厘米；3 米 40 厘米＝____ 厘米；4 米＝____ 厘米。再選自己量得的一個米和厘米的記錄，改以厘米表示。兩個記錄是否表示同一長度？解釋。

4 · 校準身體上的尺

完成活動 1–2 的實際量度後，選擇臂展、手臂長或正常步幅作身體上的尺。用真正量具量度兩次，記錄結果和單位，選定一個近似參考長度。保持手臂姿勢或步幅一致。解釋為甚麼同伴的參考長度可能不同。

5 · 先估計，後量度

選一件新的長物件及一段新的直線距離。使用量具前，先用已校準的身體上的尺估計。記錄參考長度、大約重複多少次、估計長度及單位。然後用合適量具實際量度，另外記錄，不要擦掉原先估計。解釋差異及一項改善方法。如有時間，再選新目標試一次。

每個目標記錄：物件／兩端位置、身體參考及大約重複次數、估計、量具、量度結果、解釋。估計是近似值，不是先偷偷量度才填答案。

教師 · 準備、提問及回饋

準備厘米尺、米尺、捲尺、紙帶、可移除地面標記及兩條長於 1 米的絲帶。至少一條絲帶須有非零厘米餘段（例如準確的 1 米 10 厘米），讓活動 3 有實際複名數量度記錄。較長而暢通路線可用公制測距輪，先示範歸零及刻度；不要求每人擁有所有量具。讓學生從現有合適量具中真正選擇。清楚標示各段兩端，不預填長度，結果按實物而異。先有量度經驗，才進行估計。

提問：「起點在哪裏？為何選這量具？量具不夠長怎麼辦？同伴如何覆核？」長絲帶可用捲尺；直線路線可重複用米尺；較長而暢通的地面可用測距輪。方法合適便可，不以唯一量具答案評分。

活動 1：十段 10 厘米共 100 厘米＝1 米。觀察接點及與標準尺比較。活動 2：觀察零刻度、對齊、直線、兩端、移尺及單位。結果不同時共同量度相同兩端。猜中數字不等於展示量度方法。

活動 3：110 厘米、205 厘米、340 厘米、400 厘米。把每米的 100 厘米與餘下厘米合起來。如把 2 米 5 厘米寫成 25 厘米，用兩米實物重新說明。不要求把單名數厘米換成複名數，也不要求小數表示。

活動 4–5：各人的參考長度和估計不同。觀察實際校準、穩定姿勢／步幅，以及量度前記下估計。問：「用甚麼參考？甚麼會影響估計？」不假定每人的步幅或臂展都是 1 米。重視合理近似、方法及反思，不設課程未規定的固定誤差合格線，不變成任意大數的乘法練習。

教師 · 可觀察證據

逐項記錄：獨立／經提示／尚未／未觀察，以及實際語句和動作。口頭解釋與書寫分開記錄。可由同伴代筆或選用適合活動能力的手臂參考，但不可把同伴的量度技能當作學生的表現。
