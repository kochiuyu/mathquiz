P5 · Bar charts (III)

1 · Read two series

Use the supplied example table: A: (30,000, 50,000), B: (60,000, 40,000), C: (20,000, 70,000), for X and Y visits. Mark legend, categories, common zero and units. These are fictional data, not actual observations.

2 · Choose and justify scale

For the supplied table choose a step of 1,000, 10,000 or 100,000 on an axis with ten intervals. Justify which fits and which gives useful detail. Do not compare charts with unequal unlabeled scales.

P5 · Bar charts (III)

3 · Draw vertical compound bars

On the blank grid make paired vertical bars with a key, common baseline, equal bar widths and categories. Preserve the raw table beside your chart. Have a partner read back every value.

4 · Draw horizontal compound bars

Represent the same table with paired horizontal bars. Compare both charts and explain what remains unchanged. Revise any category or legend mismatch.

P5 · Bar charts (III)

5 · Rounding and source

Use supplied raw values 34,500, 45,000, 65,100. Round to 10,000 and retain both columns. For a new chart choose a nonpersonal aggregate dataset, record source/date or collection method, and explain any information lost by rounding.

6 · Critique and improve

Create a deliberately misleading version by removing the zero or swapping the key. Ask a partner to identify the problem, then correct it. State two supported comparisons, not a claim of causation.

Teacher models, not observations

Example: a 10,000 step fits the maximum 70,000 within ten intervals; 1,000 does not; 100,000 fits but has less detail. X total 110,000, Y total 160,000; B difference 20,000. Rounded 34,500, 45,000, 65,100 → 30,000, 50,000, 70,000.

Observation record

Record date, original work, explanation, assistance and next step. Mark independent / prompted / not yet / not observed.

P5 · 棒形圖（三）

1 · 閱讀兩組數據

使用提供的示例表：A:(30000,50000)、B:(60000,40000)、C:(20000,70000)，表示X、Y到訪次數，標示圖例、類別、共同零點及單位。這是虛構資料，不是實際觀察。

2 · 選擇刻度

數軸分十格，為示例表從1000、10000、100000中選每格值，解釋是否容納所有數據及能否顯示細節，不用未標示的不等刻度比較。

P5 · 棒形圖（三）

3 · 畫直式複合圖

在空白方格畫成對直棒，附圖例、共同基線、相同棒闊及類別，保留原表，請同伴讀回每個數值。

4 · 畫橫式複合圖

用成對橫棒表示同一表，比較兩圖並解釋不變之處，修正類別或圖例錯配。

P5 · 棒形圖（三）

5 · 四捨五入與來源

把示例原數34500、45000、65100四捨五入至10000，保留原數及近似值兩欄。另選非個人整體資料製圖，記錄來源、日期或收集方法，解釋近似值損失的資料。

6 · 批判及改善

故意製作省略零點或交換圖例的誤導版本，請同伴找錯後改正。寫兩個資料支持的比較，不作因果推論。

教師示例，並非觀察

示例：每格10000可在十格容納最大70000，1000不足，100000雖可但細節較少。X合共110000，Y合共160000；B相差20000。34500、45000、65100四捨五入後為30000、50000、70000。

觀察記錄

記錄日期、原作、解釋、協助及下一步，註明獨立完成／提示後／尚未／未觀察。
