Teacher guide · keep separate

Do not show this page during dictation. Give only the pupil sheet. Use the English or Chinese route, not both as a memory test. Use the pupil's usual teaching language; distinguish mathematical evidence from pronunciation, hearing or writing-access needs.

Materials: pupil sheet, pencil, 60 identical counters and a tray. No calculator or multiplication sentence is needed. Work individually or in a small group where each pupil's responses can be recorded.

A · Reading numerals

Say: “Read the number I point to.” Point in this order: 21, 40, 56, 65, 90, 100. Expected: twenty-one; forty; fifty-six; sixty-five; ninety; one hundred. Accept a clear equivalent spoken number, not an accent standard. Do not model the answer first.

B · Counting, not reading a sequence

Cover the numeral cards. Say each prompt aloud. Do not display the expected sequence.

SayExpected response
“Count forwards by one from 27 to 33.”27, 28, 29, 30, 31, 32, 33
“Count backwards by one from 62 to 57.”62, 61, 60, 59, 58, 57
“Count forwards by one from 96 to 100.”96, 97, 98, 99, 100
“Count backwards by one from 100 to 96.”100, 99, 98, 97, 96

Set out 27 counters loosely. Say: “Count these objects. How many are there?” Then give 40 counters and say: “Put 36 counters in this tray.” Observe one-to-one counting or valid grouping, the final total, and whether the pupil stops at 36.

C · Writing a heard number

Cover all answer keys. Say: “Listen to the number. Write its numeral in box 1.” Read seventy-two; then use boxes 2–6 for thirty, forty-nine, ninety-four, eighty, one hundred. Read the whole number naturally, not separate digits. Repeat once if requested without extra clues. Expected numerals: 72, 30, 49, 94, 80, 100.

Record the first response before teaching or correction. A requested neutral repeat is not a mathematical error. Record self-correction and any number cue separately. Writing a printed number word is not evidence for this listening task.

Optional group-counting observation · objective 4

Prepare 24 counters in pairs, 35 in fives and 60 in tens, one collection at a time. Ask: “Count these objects in groups. Say each running total and then the total number.” No written calculation is required.

Expected totals: pairs 2, 4, …, 24; fives 5, 10, …, 35; tens 10, 20, …, 60. Check that each spoken total follows the next whole group and that the last total names the collection, rather than just the number of groups.

Use the evidence

Record difficulty such as reversing 56 and 65 or skipping 30 as an observed response, not a diagnosis. Teach the missed idea, then reassess with different numbers within 21–100. Keep assisted and independent attempts distinct. This sheet samples performance; it does not prove mastery of every number or replace the unit's other objectives.

教師指引 · 與學生頁分開

聽寫時不要展示本頁。只把學生頁交給學生。選用英文或中文版本，不要連做兩個版本來測試記憶。採用學生慣用的教學語言；把數學表現與發音、聽覺或書寫方面的需要分開記錄。

材料：學生頁、鉛筆、60個相同的數數物件和一個盤。不需計算機或乘法算式。個別進行，或在能分別記錄每位學生回答的小組進行。

A · 讀數字

說：「讀出我指着的數字。」依次指着 21、40、56、65、90、100。預期回答：二十一、四十、五十六、六十五、九十、一百。接納意思清楚的等值讀法，不以口音評分。不要先示範答案。

B · 數數，而非照讀數列

遮蓋數字卡，逐項口述以下指示，不展示預期數列。

教師說預期回答
「由二十七開始，一個一個順數至三十三。」27、28、29、30、31、32、33
「由六十二開始，一個一個倒數至五十七。」62、61、60、59、58、57
「由九十六開始，一個一個順數至一百。」96、97、98、99、100

散放 27個物件，說：「數一數這些物件，共有多少個？」然後提供40個物件，說：「請把三十六個物件放進盤裏。」觀察學生是否逐一點數或合理分組、說出總數，並在36停止。

C · 寫出聽到的數

遮蓋所有答案。說：「聽清楚這個數，在第一格寫出數字。」讀出七十二；然後依次在第2至6格讀出三十、四十九、九十四、八十、一百。自然地讀出整個數，不要逐個位的數字讀。學生要求時可重複一次，但不要加提示。預期數字：72、30、49、94、80、100。

先記錄首次回答，再教導或改正。要求中性地重複讀數，不算數學錯誤。分別記錄自行改正和數字提示。把印刷的中文數詞轉寫為數字，不能代替本項聽寫證據。

可選的分組點數觀察 · 目標4

分三次準備：24個物件，每2個一組；35個，每5個一組；60個，每10個一組。說：「一組一組地數，每次說出到目前為止共有多少個，最後說出總數。」不要求寫算式。

預期回答：每2個一組 2、4、……、24；每5個一組 5、10、……、35；每10個一組 10、20、……、60。觀察每個讀數是否對應下一整組，最後說的是物件總數，而非組數。

如何運用記錄

例如把56與65倒轉，或漏數30，應記作實際回答，不直接當作診斷。教導未掌握的概念後，以21至100範圍內的其他數再評估。分開記錄有提示和獨立完成的表現。本活動只是抽樣觀察，不能證明學生掌握了每一個數，亦不能代替本單元的其他目標。
