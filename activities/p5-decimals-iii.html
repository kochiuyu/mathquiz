5 · Exact and approximate results

Calculate 0.65 × 0.5 exactly, then round to tenths and hundredths. Repeat for 1.99 × 0.5. Use = for exact equality and ≈ for the requested approximation. Locate each exact result between two neighbouring tenths or hundredths and explain the halfway decision. Do not round the operands first.

6 · Write and solve a measurement problem

Choose a ribbon-length, liquid-amount or price-per-metre situation requiring multiplication of two numbers. Use one- or two-place decimal operands and integer coefficients no larger than a three-digit by two-digit product. State the reference amount, multiplier, units, whether an exact or rounded answer is required, and a useful estimate. Solve it and ask a partner to check the meaning and units.

Teacher examples and assessment

1

2.04 × 10 = 20.40; × 100 = 204.00; × 1000 = 2040.00. For 0.007 the results are 0.070, 0.700 and 7.000. Trailing zeros do not change numerical value.

2

30 × 0.1 = 3; × 0.01 = 0.30; × 0.001 = 0.030. Each successive value is one tenth of the previous value.

3

Three groups of 0.4 total 1.2. The overlap is 6 × 5 = 30 of 100 equal cells, hence 0.30. The reference whole must remain the same.

4

3.4 × 2.6 = 8.84; 1.25 × 8 = 10.00. Accept justified bounds such as 6 < 8.84 < 12; do not require one unique estimate. Check the actual original method.

5

0.65 × 0.5 = 0.325 ≈ 0.3 (tenths) or 0.33 (hundredths). 1.99 × 0.5 = 0.995 ≈ 1.0 or 1.00. Equivalent numerical forms are acceptable unless written precision is explicitly assessed.

6

For example: HK$2.05 per metre × 4 metres = HK$8.20. Confirm that units and reference amounts match. Teacher examples are not evidence that a pupil created or solved a problem.

教師示例及評核

1

2.04乘以10、100及1000的結果為20.40、204.00及2040.00；0.007的結果為0.070、0.700及7.000。末尾的零不改變數值。

2

30乘以0.1、0.01及0.001的結果為3、0.30及0.030；每次的值都是前一次的十分之一。

3

三組0.4共有1.2；重疊部分為6乘5，即100個相等方格中的30格，所以是0.30。須維持相同的整體。

4

準確積為8.84及10.00。接受有理據的範圍（例如6小於8.84小於12），估計沒有唯一答案。檢查原作及解釋。

5

0.65乘以0.5等於0.325，至十分位約為0.3，至百分位約為0.33。1.99乘以0.5等於0.995，約為1.0或1.00。除非明確評核書寫位數，否則接受數值相同的形式。

6

例如每米2.05港元，買4米共8.20港元。核對單位及基準量；教師例子不能當作學生已自擬或解題的證據。
