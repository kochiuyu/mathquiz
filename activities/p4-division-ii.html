Teacher key and bounded checks

1. 84 ÷ 12 = 7 remainder0:7groups of12counters, none left. 12 ×7 +0=84. Quotient counts groups; remainder counts counters. Require the pupil’s connection between groups and written operations.

2. 736 ÷23 =32 remainder0:73−69=4 (borrowing is needed), bring down6 to make46, then46−46=0. 825 ÷24 =34 remainder9:82−72=10; bring down5 to make105;105−96=9. 309 ÷15 =20 remainder9:30−30=0; bring down9; no full group of15fits, so write0in the units place of the quotient. The0is a trailing quotient digit, not an internal zero or a three-digit quotient.

3. A valid pair must satisfy dividend = divisor × quotient + remainder AND 0≤remainder<divisor. In33R33 another group of24can be removed: quotient34, remainder9. 24×34+9=825 and9<24. For309÷15,2R9reconstructs39, not309;20R9reconstructs309. Do not accept recomposition alone when the remainder is too large.

4. Use the final digit for2/5/10; use the digit sum for3. Divisible by2:0,12,102,120. By3:0,12,15,21,102,105,120. By5:0,15,105,120. By10:0,120. The number1is in none of these groups. Even:0,12,102,120; odd:1,15,21,105. For a positive divisor d,0=d×0so0÷d=0exactly. Division by0is not defined. Proofs of the tests and tests for4,6,8,9are not required here.

5. One compatible-number estimate is960÷24=40boxes. Exact947÷24=39 remainder11, so39FULL boxes and11pencils left. 24×39+11=947;0≤11<24. Other justified estimates are acceptable. Observe the estimate written before exact work. If the question instead asked enough boxes for every pencil,40would be needed; that is not the full-box question asked.

6. 125÷24=5R5:5full vehicles seat120people, leaving5; the minimum is6vehicles. Check5×24=120<125≤144=6×24. For120people,120÷24=5R0, so exactly5vehicles; a sixth is not the minimum. A nonzero remainder requires another vehicle; an exact case does not.

教師解答及範圍檢查

1. 84 ÷12 = 7餘0：7組各12粒，沒有剩餘。12×7+0=84。商表示組數，餘數表示剩餘計數物的粒數。須觀察學生連繫分組與直式運算。

2. 736 ÷23 =32餘0：73−69=4（須借位），移下6成46，再46−46=0。825 ÷24 =34餘9：82−72=10，移下5成105，105−96=9。309 ÷15 =20餘9：30−30=0，移下9，不足再分一組15，所以商的個位寫0。這是商末尾的0，不是中間的0或三位數商。

3. 有效答案須同時符合被除數＝除數×商＋餘數，以及0≤餘數<除數。33餘33還可再取一組24，變成34餘9。24×34+9=825且9<24。309÷15若寫2餘9，只能還原39，不是309；20餘9才還原309。餘數過大時，不可只因能還原就接受。

4. 2／5／10看個位，3看數字和。被2整除：0、12、102、120。被3整除：0、12、15、21、102、105、120。被5整除：0、15、105、120。被10整除：0、120。1不屬於任何一組。偶數：0、12、102、120；奇數：1、15、21、105。正除數d有0=d×0，所以0÷d=0且沒有餘數。除以0沒有定義。本單元不要求證明判別方法或學4、6、8、9的判別法。

5. 一個方便數估計是960÷24=40盒。準確947÷24=39餘11，所以39個滿盒，剩11枝鉛筆。24×39+11=947；0≤11<24。其他有理由的估計也接受，須觀察學生先估後算。如果問裝下所有鉛筆最少需幾盒，就需40盒，但本題問滿盒數。

6. 125÷24=5餘5：5輛滿車坐120人，餘5人；最少需6輛車。5×24=120<125≤144=6×24。120人則120÷24=5餘0，只需5輛車，第六輛不是最少。非零餘數才須另加一輛，整除時不用。
