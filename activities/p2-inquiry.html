P2 · Investigate equal packs

Name ______ Date ______ · Use 14 counters, seven trays or paper with clearly separated group spaces, paper and pencil. Work on a tray/table; counters are not food. These investigations belong inside number and measurement lessons, not a separate course.

A1 · Ask and predict

Start with 12 counters. Make full packs of the same size. Choose a pack size from 2, 3, 4 or 5 counters. Predict the number of full packs and leftovers before moving counters. What might “a useful packing” mean: no leftovers, fewer packs, or another clear purpose? Choose your criterion and explain it.

A2 · Plan a fair comparison

How will you compare the four permitted pack sizes without losing or double-counting counters? Design a record with pack size, full packs and leftovers. Decide an order for testing so you do not miss a size. Keep the total 12 each time. Show each trial using counters and record a multiplication/division check.

Test, challenge and transfer

A3 · Explain and challenge

Compare the trials with your chosen criterion. Is there one best choice or more than one? Ask a partner to check a trial from your record and offer a different criterion. Does changing the criterion change the recommendation? Explain with evidence. If a trial fails a check, keep the first record visible and add a correction.

A4 · Try a new total

Now use 14 counters with the same four pack sizes. Predict what will change, test and record. Does your earlier recommendation still work? Explain what you can conclude from these two totals and what you have NOT tested. Write a new question that could be investigated. No need to claim a rule for every number.

P2 · Investigate length estimates

Use a metre ruler/tape, paper and two safe targets longer than 1 m, selected by an adult in a clear indoor area. Do not climb or stretch into traffic. Work with a partner; use arm length or hand span, not any sensitive body information.

B1 · Choose a question and method

Can two people use different personal length references yet estimate the same target reasonably? Each person measures their own chosen reference twice in centimetres, records the results and chooses a working reference. Explain how you will handle disagreement between the two readings. Agree on one target, its two endpoints and a measurement method.

B2 · Predict before measuring

Using your own reference, estimate the target’s length before anyone reveals its ruler/tape length. Record the estimate and method without erasing. Then measure the target in metres and centimetres. Explain how you aligned zero, avoided gaps and agreed on endpoints. Compare estimates with the measurement; discuss why the personal-reference counts may differ.

Revise the method and communicate

B3 · Improve and transfer

Choose one change that might improve your estimating method, such as a better-calibrated reference or more careful placement. State why. For the SAME new target and agreed endpoints, record one estimate with your original method AND one with the revised method before anyone measures or reveals its length. Then measure once and compare which estimate is closer in this case. Accept a tie or a worse revised estimate; retain both records. This comparison does not prove the change always helps or caused any improvement.

Share the investigation

Show your question, predictions, trial records, checks, revisions and conclusion. Invite a partner to explain or challenge your method. What would you investigate next? You may speak, draw or use counters to communicate. Keep tool-use evidence separate from somebody else writing your words.

Teacher · Facilitation and bounded checks

Choose opportunities during 2N3/2N6 and 2M1 lessons once prerequisite concepts are available. Split sessions. The source allows investigation during motivation, development, consolidation or assessment; these examples do not replace ten hours of integrated opportunities across the year.

A materials: provide up to seven trays or allow clearly separated packs on paper, since 14 counters in twos makes seven packs. Ask “What stays the same?”, “Have you tested every permitted size?”, “Can you check another way?” rather than reveal the preferred arrangement. Pack identities/order are not additional methods: compare pack SIZE, full-pack count and leftovers. A complete finite record need only cover the four permitted sizes, not every partition of 12 or 14.

Teacher checks, sizes 2/3/4/5: total 12 gives full packs/remainders 6/0, 4/0, 3/0, 2/2; total 14 gives 7/0, 4/2, 3/2, 2/4. Check total = pack size × full packs + leftovers, with leftovers smaller than pack size. “No leftovers” at 12 has several solutions; “fewest full packs” and “fewest packs with no leftovers” are different criteria. Accept a clearly stated reasonable criterion and evidence, not an unexplained winner. No two-digit divisor or mixed-operation procedure required.

B: privately select two straight targets over 1 m, at least one with a nonzero centimetre remainder. No fixed measurements or ideal body reference are prescribed. Observe calibration, estimates written before measurement, zero/endpoint alignment and rechecking. Do not require decimal-metre recording, reverse compound conversion or numerical error formulae. Qualitative closer/farther comparisons with physical marks are acceptable. For B3 require both original-method and revised-method estimates of the same new target, recorded before measurement; compare each against that one measured length. Equal or worse revised estimates are valid findings. Do not infer universal benefit or causation from this small comparison.

Teacher · Process rubric and evidence

Record independently / with prompts / not yet / not observed, with date, pupil words, trial artefacts and assistance.

  1. Frames a question or criterion and makes a testable prediction.
  2. Plans a manageable comparison; states what stays fixed.
  3. Carries out trials using real counters or tools and records honestly.
  4. Checks arithmetic/measurement; notices conflict and revises.
  5. Communicates a reasoned conclusion with evidence; accepts challenge.
  6. Transfers to a new case; compares both estimation methods on the same new target before measurement; accepts equal/worse outcomes and distinguishes findings from untested causal/general claims.

Correct answers alone are insufficient process evidence. Supported communication is welcome; record transcription/manipulation assistance separately. No automatic score or completion claim. 小二 · 探究等量包裝

姓名 ______ 日期 ______ · 準備十四粒圓片、七個小盤或畫分組圈的紙、紙和筆。在桌上操作，圓片不是食物。探究應融入數與量度課，不是另設獨立課程。

A1 · 提問及預測

先用十二粒圓片，每包相同數量，滿包才算一包。每包數量可選 2、3、4、5 粒。未動手前預測滿包數及剩餘。「好用的包裝」指沒有剩餘、包數較少，還是另一個清楚目的？選你的準則並解釋。

A2 · 計劃公平比較

怎樣比較四種每包數量，而不漏數或重複計算？設計記錄，包含每包數量、滿包數及剩餘。安排測試次序以免遺漏；每次總數保持十二。用圓片展示各次試驗，寫乘法／除法核對。

測試、質疑及遷移

A3 · 解釋及接受質疑

用你的準則比較各次結果。最佳選擇只有一個還是不止一個？請同伴按記錄重做一次，提出另一準則。改準則會改建議嗎？用證據解釋。若核對失敗，保留原記錄並補寫更正。

A4 · 換新總數

改用十四粒圓片，每包仍選 2、3、4、5。先預測變化，再測試及記錄。先前建議還適用嗎？說明兩個總數支持甚麼結論，哪些情況尚未試過。寫一個可再研究的問題，不必聲稱適用所有數。

小二 · 探究長度估計

用米尺／捲尺、紙及成人在安全室內選的兩個超過一米的目標。不可攀爬或伸向車道。與同伴用手臂長或手掌跨度作參考，不涉及敏感身體資料。

B1 · 選問題及方法

兩人用不同身體參考，能否合理估計同一目標？每人用厘米量自己選的參考兩次，記錄後選工作參考值。兩次讀數不同，會怎樣處理？商定目標、兩端及量度方法。

B2 · 先估後量

未有人透露尺量長度前，用自己的參考估目標，寫估值和方法，不擦掉。再用米和厘米量目標，說明如何對零、避免間隙、統一端點。比較估值和實測；討論為何兩人的身體參考次數可能不同。

改進方法及溝通

B3 · 修訂及新目標

選一項可能改善估計的方法，例如重新校準參考或放置更仔細，說明原因。對同一個新目標及商定端點，先分別用原方法及修改後方法各作一次估計，兩個估值都要在任何人量度或透露長度前記錄。然後量一次，比較哪個估值在這次較接近。接受同樣接近或修改後更差的結果，保留兩份記錄。這次比較不能證明修改必定有用，也不能證明差異由修改造成。

分享探究

展示問題、預測、試驗記錄、覆核、修訂及結論。邀請同伴解釋或質疑方法。下一步想研究甚麼？可口述、畫圖或用圓片溝通；本人操作與他人代筆須分開記錄。

教師 · 引導及有限核對

在 2N3／2N6 及 2M1 有相關先備後安排，分次完成。來源允許在動機、發展、鞏固或評估階段探究；這兩例不代替全年十小時跨單元探究機會。

A 備七個盤，或紙上清楚分開的組，足夠十四粒每兩粒一包。問「甚麼保持不變？」「四種都試了嗎？」「可用另一方法覆核嗎？」不先透露偏好的排列。包的位置／個別圓片身份不算新方法；比較每包數量、滿包數及剩餘。只需完成四種有限選項，不要求找出十二或十四的所有分拆。

教師核對：每包 2／3／4／5，十二粒的滿包數／剩餘依次 6／0、4／0、3／0、2／2；十四粒依次 7／0、4／2、3／2、2／4。總數＝每包數×滿包數＋剩餘，剩餘小於每包數。十二粒「沒有剩餘」有多解；「滿包數最少」與「沒有剩餘且包數最少」是不同準則。接受準則清楚且有證據的建議，不接受只猜最佳。不要求兩位除數或混合運算程序。

B 私下選兩個超過一米的直線目標，至少一個有非零厘米餘量。不預設測量答案或最佳身體參考。觀察校準、先寫估值、對零／端點及覆核。不要求小數米、反向複名數換算或數值誤差公式；可用實物標記說較接近／較遠。B3 必須對同一新目標，在量度前記錄原方法及修改後方法兩個估值，再各與同一實測長度比較。同樣接近或修改後更差也是有效結果，不從這小型比較推斷普遍效益或因果。

教師 · 過程準則

逐項記獨立／提示後／尚未／未觀察，附日期、學生語句、實物記錄及援助。

  1. 提出問題或準則，作可測試預測。
  2. 安排可行比較，說明不變條件。
  3. 用實物或工具試驗，誠實記錄。
  4. 覆核運算或量度，發現矛盾並修訂。
  5. 以證據說結論，接受同伴質疑。
  6. 用新例測試；量度前對同一新目標用兩種方法估計，接受相同或更差結果，區分觀察與未證實因果／推廣。

只答對不算探究過程證據。可支援溝通，但代筆和操作援助分開記錄。不設自動分數或完成宣稱。本地草稿，待獨立、翻譯、瀏覽器／列印及課堂驗證。來源：補充文件 PDF 41（印頁 38）；說明 PDF 48（印頁 42）。
