Capacity and displacement

1 · Safety and prediction

Use plastic containers, a tray and clean water away from electrical items. Do not taste water. Choose safe non-floating, insoluble objects. Predict which object occupies more space; leave predictions and observations blank until tested.

2 · Match capacity and volume

Compare 1 cm³ unit cubes with 1 mL marked volume and a 10 cm×10 cm×10 cm internal box with 1 L. Check real internal dimensions and measuring equipment before claiming agreement. Explain 1 m³=1000 L using 1000 one-litre cubes conceptually.

Capacity and displacement

3 · Measuring cup displacement

Read an initial water level at eye height, fully submerge the object, then read again. Record both values with units and subtract. Repeat and explain any differences; do not replace real data with the model.

4 · Rectangular tank rise

Measure internal length and width. Record water depth before and after full submersion. Use base area×rise and compare with a cup result when feasible. Explain why final depth alone is wrong.

Capacity and displacement

5 · Overflow collection

Fill an overflow vessel to its spout; wait until dripping stops. Place an empty collecting cup, submerge the object fully and collect ALL displaced water. Record volume and spills. Explain why initial filling water must not be counted.

6 · Choose and critique a method

Compare a stone, a floating cork and a dissolving sugar cube without tasting materials. Explain which can be measured directly by displacement and why. Plan a safer/improved repeat with explicit limitations.

Teacher model and rubric

1. Check full submersion, no trapped air and no dissolution; use adult supervision where needed.

2. 10³=1000 cm³=1000 mL=1 L;1 m³=1000 L. Both capacity and liquid volume may use these units.

3. Model only:210 mL−150 mL=60 mL=60 cm³. This is not the pupil measurement.

4. Model:12 cm×8 cm×(7−5)cm=192 cm³.

Teacher model and rubric

5. Collected displacement volume equals submerged solid volume only if the set-up is ready and no displaced water is lost.

6. A fully submerged insoluble stone is suitable. Floating/dissolving objects violate the direct method; do not invent volumes for them.

容量與排水

1 · 安全與預測

使用膠容器、托盤及清水，遠離電器，不飲測試用水。選安全、不浮起、不溶解物件，先預測體積；未實測不可填觀察結果。

2 · 容量與體積配對

比較1cm³積木與1mL刻度，及內部10cm×10cm×10cm盒與1L。核對實物內尺寸及量具後才說相符；以1000個一升立方體說明1m³＝1000L。

容量與排水

3 · 量杯排水

視線水平讀初始水位，完全浸沒物件後再讀，記錄兩讀數及單位並相減。重做並解釋差異，不可用模型數據代替實測。

4 · 長方體水箱上升

量內長及內闊，記錄完全浸沒前後水深，用底面積×上升高度；可行時與量杯結果比較。解釋只用最終水深為何錯誤。

容量與排水

5 · 溢水收集

溢水杯加至出口，待滴水停止後放空收集杯，再完全浸沒物件，收集全部排水。記錄體積及灑水情況，解釋不能把原本加水計入。

6 · 選擇及評議方法

比較石塊、浮木及會溶解的方糖，不品嘗材料。解釋哪些可直接用排水法及原因，提出安全改良及限制。

教師模型及準則

1. 檢查完全浸沒、沒有困住氣泡、不溶解，需要時由成人監督。

2. 10³＝1000cm³＝1000mL＝1L；1m³＝1000L。容量及液體體積均可用這些單位。

3. 僅供模型：210mL−150mL＝60mL＝60cm³，不是學生實測結果。

4. 模型：12cm×8cm×(7−5)cm＝192cm³。

教師模型及準則

5. 裝置先準備妥當且排出水無損失，收集水體積才等於固體體積。

6. 完全浸沒而不溶解石塊適用；浮起或溶解物件不符合直接方法，不可編造體積。
