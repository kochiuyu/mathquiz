P2 · Line segments and quadrilaterals

Name ______ Date ______ · Use rulers, set squares, paper, string and safe geometric strips.

1 · Between two points

The three connections below join the same two points. Fit a string along each route, then straighten it to compare lengths without stretching. Identify the shortest connection and the line segment. Draw another curved connection and compare it with the segment. Explain that the segment is straight and shortest among these connections.

2 · Four sides; adjacent and opposite

Join four straight strips end to end to make a flat, non-crossing quadrilateral. Identify its four sides as line segments. On the labelled diagram, identify the side opposite AB and the two sides adjacent to AB. Explain that adjacent sides meet at a corner; opposite sides do not meet. Rotate the diagram and repeat.

Draw and make

5 · Draw different sizes

On grid, dot or plain paper draw two different-sized squares and two different-sized rectangles with unequal adjacent side lengths. Check every required side equality with a ruler and every corner with a set square; record your method. Orient at least one differently. A roughly similar freehand shape alone is not sufficient.

6 · Make different sizes from materials

Use string, strips or safe straws to make two different-sized squares and two different-sized rectangles. Lay each flat and check it. Keep the side lengths but push the corners out of square: does it still meet the intended properties? Repair it. Ask a partner to verify and retain models or records separately from the drawings.

7 · Explain and repair

Someone says “Four equal sides guarantee a square.” What else must be checked? Someone says “Two equal opposite-side pairs guarantee a rectangle.” What is missing? Show with models, then explain an example where rotation or enlargement preserves the relevant properties.

Teacher · Setup and key

Provide non-stretch string, rulers, set squares, grid/dot/plain paper, strips or safe straws and tape. Adults handle cutting. Models must be flat and non-crossing. Responsive/printed diagrams can scale: no fixed centimetre answers. Compare traced string lengths without stretching.

1: the straight connection is the line segment and is shortest; bent/curved routes are longer. Experiments establish the concept, not a formal general proof or required “endpoint” vocabulary. 2: CD is opposite AB; BC and DA are adjacent. The four sides are connected line segments.

3: S has four right angles and four equal sides: square. 4: R has four right angles and AB=CD, BC=DA: the rectangle example. T has equal opposite sides but not all right angles; side equality alone is insufficient. Do not require a later-class name for it.

5–6: require two sizes of each type, both drawings and material constructions. Verify four angles and required equal sides, not appearance alone. Pushing corners away from right angles breaks the intended properties. 7: both statements omit the four-right-angle condition. Rotation preserves sides/angles; uniform enlargement preserves required equalities and right angles.

Teacher · Evidence and teaching boundary

Record independent / prompted / not yet / not observed with actual methods; separate speech, measurement, drawing and making. Do not credit a partner’s actions as the pupil’s.

小二 · 線段及四邊形

姓名 ______ 日期 ______ · 準備尺、三角尺、紙、線和安全幾何紙條。

1 · 兩點之間

下面三條線連接相同兩點。用線貼沿每條路線，再拉直比較長度；不可把線拉長。指出最短的一條，說哪條是線段。再畫一條連接兩點的曲線，與線段比較。解釋線段是直的且在這些連線中最短。

2 · 四邊及相鄰／相對

用四條直紙條首尾相接，製作平面、不交叉的四邊形。指出四條邊都是線段。看下面 A、B、C、D 順序標示的圖，指出 AB 的相對邊及兩條相鄰邊，說明相鄰邊共用一個角而相對邊不相接。轉動圖再說一次。

3 · 正方形的檢查

先猜 S 的形狀，再用三角尺檢查四個角，用尺或紙帶比較四邊，逐項記錄。四角是否都是直角？四邊是否相等？轉向後再核對；不要只因為它「斜放」而改名字。

4 · 長方形的檢查

檢查 R 四個角，並分別比較兩對相對邊。記錄哪些邊相等，指出相對和相鄰的邊。再檢查 T：即使某些邊相等，角是否全是直角？用工具說明為何它不符合本題長方形的性質，不用替 T 學新類別名稱。

畫出並製作

5 · 畫不同大小

在方格紙、點紙或白紙上畫兩個不同大小的正方形及兩個不同大小的長方形。長方形的長邊和短邊不同。每個都用尺核對所需等邊關係，用三角尺檢查四角，記錄方法；至少一個轉向擺放。不只徒手畫「像」的圖。

6 · 用材料製作不同大小

用線、紙條或安全飲管製作兩個不同大小的正方形及兩個不同大小的長方形，逐個平放並檢查。先保留四邊長度但把角推斜，觀察名稱是否仍適用；再修正。讓同伴核對，保留模型或記錄，與畫圖證據分開。

7 · 解釋及修正

有人說「四邊相等就一定是正方形」，你會檢查甚麼？有人說「兩對相對邊相等就一定是長方形」，還缺甚麼？用模型示範，再說一個圖形不會因旋轉或放大而失去原來性質的例子。

教師 · 設備及解答

準備不易伸長的線、尺、三角尺、方格／點紙／白紙、紙條或安全飲管及黏貼物。成人處理剪裁；模型須平面且不交叉。圖像可縮放，不設固定厘米答案。線沿曲線量度後拉直比較，不拉伸。

1：直線連接是線段，最短；曲線或折線較長。這些實驗建立概念，不要求一般性形式證明或術語「端點」。2：AB 相對 CD，相鄰 BC 和 DA；四邊是首尾連接的線段。

3：S 四角直角、四邊相等，是正方形。4：R 四角直角、AB＝CD、BC＝DA，是本課長方形例子。T 雖有相對邊相等，但角不是全直角，不能只以邊長判斷。不要求替它命名為後續類別。

5–6：每類兩種大小，分別有畫圖及材料製作；核對四角和所需等邊，不以外觀代替量度。推斜後若失去直角，便不符合原定性質。7：兩種說法均欠四角直角的條件。旋轉不改邊長及角；整體等比例放大保留所需等邊及直角。

3

4
