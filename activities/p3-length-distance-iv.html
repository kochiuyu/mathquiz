P3 · Kilometres in everyday life

Name ______ Date ______ · Use after metre/centimetre experience. This sheet is not a scale ruler.

1 · Build a kilometre reference

Use ten cards, each representing a 100 m section of the same route. Join them end to end without overlaps or gaps. Count the represented metres and state the total in kilometres. Explain why a larger unit is convenient for a long route. These are model cards, not a real 1 km walk. Your teacher will also show a real local route whose length has been verified as about 1 km. Describe how its size compares with a familiar school distance.

2 · Compare lengths and distances

Two bridges have recorded lengths 2 km and 3 km. Which is longer? Two routes from the same school have distances 4 km and 6 km. Which is farther? Two tunnels are each 1 km long: compare them. Explain each comparison in words; these are fictional recorded measurements, not a map to scale.

Millimetres: measure, then estimate

Use a ruler with millimetre divisions, a tape measure if helpful, two card strips, and two pairs of marked dots on a separate card. Do not measure the printed response spaces as fixed-length specimens.

3 · Inspect one centimetre

Find two neighbouring centimetre marks on a real ruler. Count the equal small intervals between them, not just the tick marks. State the relation between millimetres and centimetres. Point to a 1 mm interval and compare it with 1 cm.

4 · Choose and use a tool

Choose a suitable tool. Measure the lengths of strips A and B and the distances between dot pairs C and D, each in millimetres. Align the zero mark with one endpoint, not necessarily the edge of the ruler. Record all four labelled results with units, compare A with B and C with D, and explain how you checked the readings. If support is needed, your teacher records it.

5 · Estimate a new target after experience

After measuring the four specimens, look at new strip E. Estimate its length in millimetres before touching it with a ruler. Record your estimate and explain your reference. Then measure it, record the result, and compare. Explain how your earlier measurements helped; an estimate need not equal the measurement.

Choose a useful unit and record clearly

6 · Use units that fit the context

Choose a convenient unit from mm, cm, m or km for recording: the thickness of a card; the length of a pencil; the width of a classroom; the distance between two towns. Explain each choice. More than one unit can be mathematically valid: explain why yours is practical. No numerical measurements are supplied for these examples.

7 · Combine centimetres and millimetres

A strip is recorded as 1 cm 3 mm; another is 4 cm 8 mm. Express each in millimetres and explain the ten-millimetre groups. Then write the length of a third strip, measured as 26 mm, with its unit. You are not asked to change 26 mm into compound units or use decimals.

Teacher · preparation and answers

Prepare ten 100 m model cards, an actual ruler with mm divisions, optional tape, card strips A/B/E, dot pairs C/D, pencil and paper. Cut/mark targets after checking them with a reliable ruler: suggested A 34 mm, B 57 mm, C 18 mm, D 42 mm, E 46 mm. Measure actual preparations rather than assuming these intended values were achieved. Supply longer writing space separately if needed. Select a familiar safe local route with independently checked length about 1 km; show its map/reference without implying the paper scale establishes length. An actual walk is optional and needs ordinary school supervision/accessibility arrangements.

1: ten groups of 100 m total 1000 m, or 1 km. 2: 3 km bridge is longer; 6 km route is farther; the 1 km tunnels have equal lengths. 3: ten equal 1 mm intervals make 1 cm. Do not count eleven boundary ticks as eleven intervals.

4: actual measurements govern the key; suggested preparation gives B longer than A, and D greater than C. Observe tool choice, zero alignment, interval reading, units and independent comparison, not only final numbers. 5: estimate must be written before measuring E. Compare with the actual E result; accept reasonable references and discuss discrepancies without inventing an accuracy pass threshold.

Teacher · recording and evidence

6: typical convenient choices are mm, cm, m, km respectively. Accept another unit with a sensible explanation; do not teach that other units are mathematically impossible. 7: 13 mm; 48 mm; 26 mm. The first two use 10 mm for each centimetre. Do not require reverse single-to-compound conversion or decimal measurement notation here.

Record independent / prompted / not yet / not observed with actual words and work: kilometre reference and 1000 m relation; comparisons of both object lengths and inter-place distances in km; millimetre/centimetre relation; suitable tool choice and actual measurements of both lengths and distances; estimation after practical experience; sensible unit selection and clear records. Oral or supported recording adaptations should be noted rather than equated with independent writing. Numerical screen answers cannot establish real tool use. No pupil-mastery claim.

小三 · 生活中的公里

姓名 ______ 日期 ______ · 先有米及厘米的量度經驗。本紙不是按實際比例的尺。

1 · 建立一公里的參照

用十張各代表同一路線 100 m 路段的卡，首尾相接，不重疊或留空。數出所代表的米數，再用公里說出總長。解釋為何長路程用較大單位較方便。卡只是模型，不代表真的走了 1 km。教師另展示已核實約 1 km 的熟悉本地路線；說說它與熟悉的校內距離相比有多長。

2 · 比較長度和距離

兩座橋記錄的長度為 2 km 和 3 km，哪座較長？從同一學校出發的兩條路線距離為 4 km 和 6 km，哪條較遠？兩條隧道各長 1 km，長度如何比較？用說話解釋。這些是假設的量度記錄，不是比例地圖。

毫米：先實測，再估計

用有毫米刻度的尺，必要時用捲尺，另備兩條卡紙及卡上的兩對標記點。不要把印出的作答空位當作固定長度的量度物。

3 · 觀察一厘米

在真尺上找相鄰的兩個厘米刻度，數其間相等的小間隔，不只是數刻線。說出毫米與厘米的關係。指出 1 mm 的間隔，與 1 cm 比較。

4 · 選工具並實際量度

選適當工具，分別量卡紙 A、B 的長度，以及 C、D 兩對點之間的距離，全部用毫米記錄。把零刻度對準起點，不一定是尺的邊緣。寫四個有標籤及單位的結果，比較 A 與 B、C 與 D，解釋如何核對讀數。如需協助，教師另作記錄。

5 · 有經驗後估計新目標

量完四個樣本後，觀察新卡紙 E。未用尺接觸它前先用毫米估計長度，記下估值並解釋參照。然後實測、記錄及比較。說明剛才的量度如何幫助估計；估值不必等於實測值。

選合適單位，清楚記錄

6 · 配合情境選單位

從 mm、cm、m、km 選方便的單位，記錄卡紙厚度、鉛筆長度、課室闊度及兩個城鎮間距離。逐一解釋。多於一個單位可以在數學上成立，要說明自己的選擇為何方便。這些例子沒有提供實測數值。

7 · 合併厘米和毫米

一條卡紙記為 1 cm 3 mm，另一條記為 4 cm 8 mm。各改用毫米表示，解釋每組十毫米。第三條實測為 26 mm，把數值連單位寫下。不要求把 26 mm 改成複名數，也不用小數。

教師 · 準備及答案

準備十張 100 m 模型卡、有毫米刻度的真尺、可選用的捲尺、卡紙 A／B／E、兩對點 C／D、紙筆。用可靠尺核對製作，建議 A 34 mm、B 57 mm、C 18 mm、D 42 mm、E 46 mm。必須量度實際製成物，不假設剪裁完全符合預定值。需要時另加作答紙。選已獨立核實約 1 km 的熟悉安全本地路線，展示地圖或資料，不以紙上圖的比例證明長度。實地步行非必要，如安排須按學校一般安全、監督及無障礙程序。

1：十個 100 m 為 1000 m，即 1 km。2：3 km 的橋較長；6 km 路線較遠；兩條 1 km 隧道等長。3：十個相等的 1 mm 間隔合為 1 cm，不把十一條邊界刻線當成十一個間隔。

4：答案以實際製成物為準；建議製作下 B 比 A 長，D 比 C 遠。觀察工具選擇、零點對齊、間隔讀數、單位及獨立比較，不只看答案。5：先寫估值，再量 E。與 E 的實測值比較，接受合理參照並討論差異，不自行訂出估計準確度合格線。

教師 · 記錄及觀察

6：一般方便的選擇依次為 mm、cm、m、km。有合理解釋可接受其他單位，不教成其他單位在數學上不可能。7：13 mm；48 mm；26 mm。首兩項每厘米合十毫米，不要求把單名數反向改成複名數，也不用小數量度記法。

逐項記獨立／提示後／尚未／未觀察，附說話和作品：公里參照及 1000 m 關係；用 km 比較物件長度及地方間距離；毫米厘米關係；選工具實測長度和距離；實測經驗後估計；選合理單位及清楚記錄。口述或協助記錄應註明，不當作獨立書寫。網上數值答案不能證明會用真工具。不宣稱學生掌握。
