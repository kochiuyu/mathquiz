P1 · Numbers have meaning

1N3 supplementary pupil sheet · Name Date

Use the objects your teacher gives you. You may speak, point, move objects or write your responses.

A · What does each digit mean?

Build 24 using groups of ten and single objects. Point to the 2 in 24. How many objects does that digit stand for?

Point to the 4. How many objects does it stand for?

Now build 42. What changed? What does its 2 stand for?

Build 60. How many tens? How many ones? What does the 0 tell you?

B · Odd and even, including zero

Start with 8 objects in pairs. Take away one pair at a time and say how many remain. Stop when the tray is empty.

Is 0 odd or even? Are any objects left without a partner?

Are 56 and 73 odd or even? Point to the digit that helps you decide.

Teacher preparation and rubric

Materials: 70 identical counters or sticks, six bundles of ten, spare singles, trays and a cloth. Make each bundle visibly contain ten. Reuse materials between activities. Keep this key from pupils until responses are recorded.

Task / setupExpected evidence
A · Give bundles and singles. Show 24, then 42, then 60.24: two tens and four ones; its 2 represents 20, its 4 represents 4. 42: four tens and two ones; its 2 represents 2. 60: six tens, zero singles; 0 records no extra ones. Record separately whether the pupil builds the quantity and identifies each digit's value. Do not award full meaning evidence merely for naming “tens” without showing or stating 20.
B · Use eight counters. Remove pairs until empty.8, 6, 4, 2, 0; zero is even, with no unmatched object. 56 is even and its units digit is 6; 73 is odd and its units digit is 3. The sequence continues to 100, which is even. Accept pointing, matching pairs or a correct spoken explanation. Do not require a formal definition of parity.
C · Show ten reference counters beside 24 target counters for A; then use 47 target counters for B. Use similar objects and spacing. Do not announce target totals.Record an estimate before counting and ask what reference groups the pupil visualised. Examples: A is a little more than two groups of ten; B is a little less than five. Estimates such as 20–30 for A or 40–50 for B can be supported, but these are examples, not compulsory intervals. Accept another reasonable estimate with a matching explanation. Then count exactly 24 and 47 and discuss any revision. An exact answer produced by counting first is counting evidence, not estimation-process evidence.

Record observations

Use I = independent, S = supported, N = not yet demonstrated, NA = not assessed. No total score or prescribed pass threshold is imposed.

Exact responses, prompts or access adjustments:

Use after pupils have counting experience. Do not require multiplication notation, inequality symbols, the term “place value” or a numerical estimation-error tolerance. Building quantities is our assessment method for making digit meaning visible, not a separately numbered curriculum requirement.

This resource supplements the oral sheet and eight digital families. Teacher observation is needed for the process; producing this sheet does not certify classroom mastery, independent translation or physical-print validation.

教師準備及評估準則

材料：70個相同的圓片或小棒、六捆十個一組的物件、單個物件、托盤和布。讓學生能看出每捆有十個，各題可重用材料。記錄回答前不要展示本解答。

任務／準備預期證據
A · 提供十個一捆及單個物件，依次展示24、42、60。24：兩個十和四個一；2表示20，4表示4。42：四個十和兩個一；2表示2。60：六個十，沒有單個物件；0表示沒有額外的個位數量。分開記錄能否表示數量，以及能否指出每個數字的值。只說「十位」而沒有表示或說出20，未能充分顯示數字的意義。
B · 用八個圓片，每次拿走一對，直到沒有。8、6、4、2、0；零是雙數，沒有未配對的物件。56是雙數，個位數字是6；73是單數，個位數字是3。數列繼續至100，100是雙數。接受指示、配對或正確的口頭解釋，不要求正式定義。
C · 用十個圓片作參考，旁邊放24個作A組；再用47個作B組。物件和間距相若，不要告訴學生目標總數。點算前記下估計，問學生想像了多少組參考物件。例如A略多於兩組十個，B略少於五組十個。A估20至30、B估40至50可有合理依據，但這只是例子，並非指定答案範圍。其他合理估計及相符解釋也接受。然後點算，核對24及47，討論是否修改。先點算而得出的正確總數，只顯示點算，並不顯示估計過程。
