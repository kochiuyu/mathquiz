P4 · Quadrilaterals (III)

Name ______ Date ______. Prepare paper, ruler, set square, grid paper and safe equal strips. Keep original drawings, models and tool records. Cards are schematics, not calibrated print measuring templates.

1 · Check properties

Inspect A–F. Use the given sides to identify quadrilaterals with four equal sides. A quadrilateral with four equal sides is a rhombus, including a square. Separately use tools on your own paper drawing or model to check all four sides and both opposite parallel pairs. Record actual results; do not guess from tilt.

2 · Draw different rhombuses

On grid, dot or plain paper, draw at least two non-square rhombuses of different sizes and shapes, then a square. Check each drawing’s four sides with a ruler and both opposite parallel pairs using suitable tools. Keep original drawings, tool methods and corrections. Explain why the square is also a rhombus.

P4 · Quadrilaterals (III)

3 · Separately make models

Join four equal safe strips or straws into a closed model; an adult prepares cutting materials. Move the joints to make different non-square rhombus shapes. Use another set of four equal strips of a different length to make a different size. Avoid flat or crossed configurations. Record each real model, its four sides and opposite parallel pairs. Making cannot be replaced by drawing or a digital answer.

P4 · Quadrilaterals (III)

4 · Every applicable name

For each card, list ALL applicable names from quadrilateral, parallelogram, rectangle, rhombus and square. Names overlap: do not force a square to be either a rectangle or a rhombus but not both. Explain the properties needed for each name. You need not construct classification diagrams or use set terminology.

5 · Relationships and counterexamples

Explain why every square is a rectangle and a rhombus, and why squares, rectangles and rhombuses are all parallelograms. Use specific cards to disprove: all rectangles are rhombuses; all rhombuses are rectangles; all parallelograms are rectangles or rhombuses. Identify the missing property and keep your own explanation.

P4 · Quadrilaterals (III)

6 · Does turning change its type?

Someone says a tilted square stops being a rectangle, or a rhombus cannot have right angles. Rotate a real square card, check its sides and right angles, and correct both statements using properties. Compare A and F. Orientation does not determine type. Attach original work and record recognition, drawing and making separately.

Teacher key and observation rubric

A and F: all five names. B: quadrilateral, parallelogram, rectangle. C: quadrilateral, parallelogram, rhombus. D: quadrilateral, parallelogram. E: quadrilateral only. A, C and F have four equal sides. B disproves rectangle implies rhombus; C disproves rhombus implies rectangle; D disproves parallelogram implies either. A square has four equal sides and four right angles, so is both a rhombus and a rectangle. Turning preserves these properties.

小四 · 四邊形（三）

姓名 ______ 日期 ______。準備紙、間尺、三角尺、方格紙及安全等長紙條。保留自己的圖畫、模型及工具紀錄。圖卡是示意圖，不是已校準的列印量度範本。

1 · 檢查性質

觀察A至F，按給定邊長辨認有四條等長邊的四邊形。四邊相等的四邊形是菱形，包括正方形。另在自己的紙圖或模型上，用工具檢查四邊及兩對對邊平行，記錄實際結果，不能只憑傾斜外觀猜測。

2 · 繪畫不同菱形

在方格紙、點紙或白紙上繪畫至少兩個不同大小、不同形狀而不是正方形的菱形，再畫一個正方形。用間尺檢查四邊，用合適工具檢查兩對對邊平行。保留原圖、工具用法及修正。解釋正方形為何也是菱形。

小四 · 四邊形（三）

3 · 另外製作模型

用四條等長的安全紙條或飲管連接成封閉模型，由成人準備剪裁材料。移動接點，製作不同形狀而非正方形的菱形；再用另一組不同長度的等長紙條製作不同大小的模型。不可攤平成直線或交叉。記錄實物、四邊及對邊平行檢查。製作不能用繪圖或選擇題代替。

小四 · 四邊形（三）

4 · 所有適用名稱

從四邊形、平行四邊形、長方形、菱形及正方形中，列出每張圖卡所有適用名稱。名稱可以重疊，不要強迫正方形只叫長方形或菱形其中一種。解釋各名稱所需性質。不要求繪畫分類圖或使用集合術語。

5 · 關係及反例

解釋每個正方形都是長方形及菱形，而正方形、長方形及菱形都是平行四邊形。用實際圖卡反駁：所有長方形都是菱形；所有菱形都是長方形；所有平行四邊形都是長方形或菱形。指出不符合的性質，保留自己的解釋。

小四 · 四邊形（三）

6 · 轉動後還是甚麼？

有人說傾斜的正方形不再是長方形，或菱形不可能有直角。轉動實際正方形圖卡，檢查四邊及直角，用性質修正說法。比較A與F，方向不能決定種類。附上原作，分開記錄辨認、繪畫和製作。

教師解答及觀察表

A及F：五個名稱全部適用。B：四邊形、平行四邊形、長方形。C：四邊形、平行四邊形、菱形。D：四邊形、平行四邊形。E：只有四邊形。A、C及F有四條等長邊。B反駁長方形必為菱形；C反駁菱形必為長方形；D反駁平行四邊形必為兩者之一。正方形四邊相等且有四個直角，所以同時是菱形及長方形。轉動不改變性質。
