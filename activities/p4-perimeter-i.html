P4 · Perimeter (I)

Name ______ Date ______. Prepare paper, a ruler and optional thread or paper strips. Measure real objects, preserve original work and hide the key. Observation records stay blank until the activity actually occurs.

1 · Find the boundary

Use a real closed paper shape. Trace its entire outside boundary ONCE and explain why the inside is not the perimeter. Separately draw a closed outline and an open broken path. Identify which has a complete boundary enclosing the shape; do not invent a perimeter for the incomplete path.

2 · Measure physical shapes

Draw and cut a square and a simple irregular polygon from paper. The outlines must be closed, without crossing, touching themselves, branches or holes. Use a ruler to measure every side; a thread or paper strip may help you follow the whole boundary. Record each side length, unit, tool and total. Screen pixels are NOT centimetres. Boundary meaning · Comparison · Formulae and composites

Teacher key and physical checks

1. Perimeter is the total length once around a complete boundary, measured in length units such as cm, not square cm. Check the pupil’s actual tracing. The open broken path has a gap and is not a complete boundary; no numeric perimeter is required for it.

2. There is no universal numerical key for pupil-made objects. Inspect the real shapes, ruler placement, side records and units. Add the recorded lengths and check against the objects within an appropriate tolerance. Thread must follow the boundary without slack or shortcuts. Do not use screen or uncalibrated printed sizes as known physical dimensions.

3. The comparison must follow the actual measured totals. Accept a physically justified result, including equality when supported within tool precision. Record repeats rather than pretending small differences are exact mathematical differences. A visually larger-looking interior need not have greater perimeter.

4. No prefilled estimate or measurement is correct for every object. Check that the estimate was recorded BEFORE measuring this fresh shape. Accept reasoned estimates, then check measured units, all edges and interpretation of any difference. Never backfill an estimate from the result.

5. A square with side s has s+s+s+s=4s. A rectangle with length l and width w has l+w+l+w=2(l+w). Opposite sides of a rectangle match; all four square sides match. Derive these from pupil records, not merely copied formulas. Transfer square: 4×5=20 cm; rectangle: 2×(8+2)=20 cm. Equal perimeters can belong to different shapes.

6. L: 6+2+4+3+2+5=22 cm. Joined: 6+2+2+1+4+3=18 cm; component perimeters 14+8 include the shared 2 cm segment twice, so 14+8−2−2=18 cm. U: 6+5+2+3+2+3+2+5=28 cm. The bounding 6-by-5 rectangle has perimeter 22 cm but misses the two 3 cm notch walls. The notch opens to the exterior, so it is not a hole. The dashed seam in Joined is internal and excluded. Inspect the pupil’s own assembly and tracing separately from these labelled calculations.

姓名 ______ 日期 ______。準備紙、尺及可選的線或紙條。用實物量度，保留原作，隱藏教師解答。所有觀察欄留待實際活動後填寫。

1 · 找出邊界

用實際封閉紙形，沿整個外邊界走一圈，解釋周界為何不是內部。另畫一條封閉輪廓及一條有缺口的折線，指出哪個有完整邊界圍成圖形。不要為不完整路徑虛構周界。

2 · 實際量度紙形

自己畫並剪出正方形及簡單不規則多邊形。輪廓須封閉，不交叉、不自相接觸、沒有分支或孔洞。用尺量每邊，可用線或紙條協助沿完整邊界量度。記錄各邊長、單位、工具及總長。屏幕像素不是厘米。本頁不是已校準模板，須量實物。

教師解答及實物核對

1. 周界是沿完整邊界一圈的總長，用厘米等長度單位，不用平方厘米。檢查學生實際描邊。開口折線有缺口，不是完整邊界，不要求其數值周界。

2. 學生自製實物沒有統一數值答案。檢查實物、尺的放置、各邊記錄及單位，把記錄邊長相加，按合適容差與實物核對。線須貼邊而不鬆弛或抄近路。不可把屏幕或未校準列印尺寸當成已知實際長度。

3. 比較須根據實測總長，接受有實物依據的結果，包括工具精細程度支持的相等。保留重複量度，不把細微差異假裝成精確數學差異。看似內部較大的圖形，周界未必較長。

4. 沒有對所有實物都正確的預填估計或實測值。核查是否在量新紙形前已記錄估計。接受有理由的估計，再檢查實測單位、所有邊及差異解釋，不可按結果補填估計。

5. 正方形邊長s，周界s+s+s+s=4s；長方形長l、闊w，周界l+w+l+w=2(l+w)。長方形對邊相等，正方形四邊相等。由學生記錄推導，不只抄公式。遷移題正方形4×5=20厘米，長方形2×(8+2)=20厘米。不同圖形可有相同周界。

6. L：6+2+4+3+2+5=22厘米。Joined：6+2+2+1+4+3=18厘米；各自周界14+8把共同2厘米邊計兩次，所以14+8−2−2=18厘米。U：6+5+2+3+2+3+2+5=28厘米。外接6×5長方形周界22厘米，遺漏兩條3厘米凹口側邊。凹口與外部相通，不是孔洞。Joined虛線是內部接縫，不計周界。學生自己的拼接及描邊須與這些標示計算分別觀察。
