P3 · Five-digit numbers

Name ______ Date ______ · Use a place-value chart and labelled value cards. Use after the related P2 prerequisites.

1 · Ten groups of one thousand

Lay out ten cards labelled 1000, count their value in thousands, then exchange them for one 10000 card. Explain why the total value stays the same despite the card count changing. Draw/fill a chart for ten thousands, thousands, hundreds, tens and ones to represent 30405 and 70008. Identify zero columns. State the value of 3 in 30405, not only the digit. Cards represent values, not 10000 physical objects.

2 · Read, listen, write and check

Read 10000, 10001, 30405, 70008 and 99999 aloud, pointing to places to explain internal zeros. Then cover this page, listen to three other numbers spoken by the teacher and write their numerals. Read them back for checking. Oral reading and numeral writing are recorded separately.

Counting, odd/even and comparison

3 · Cross the ten-thousand boundary

Count aloud and record each number from 9990 to 10000 in ones; then backwards from 10003 to 9998 in ones. Count backwards in thousands from 12400 to 8400. Explain each step rather than only stating the final number.

4 · Odd or even from the ones digit

Classify 10001, 30405, 70008, 99999 and 20000 as odd/even. Circle the ones digit and explain. What changes when 70008 becomes 70009?

5 · Explain, then use symbols

Explain each comparison aloud, then insert =, > or <: 30405 __ 34005; 70008 __ 69999; 20000 __ 20000; 9999 __ 10000. For two five-digit numbers, which column is compared first? What if those digits match? Choose another five-digit pair for a partner to compare and check.

Teacher · Prompts and key

Provide ten 1000 cards, one 10000 card, reusable place-value chart and digit cards. Explain that ten groups of one thousand make ten thousand. 30405 is 3 ten-thousands, 0 thousands, 4 hundreds, 0 tens, 5 ones; 70008 has 7 ten-thousands and 8 ones with three zero places. The value of 3 is 30000.

Accept conventional accurate English reading with or without optional “and”: ten thousand; ten thousand and one; thirty thousand four hundred and five; seventy thousand and eight; ninety-nine thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine. Dictate without displaying numerals: “forty thousand and twenty”, “sixty thousand three hundred and two”, “eighty-one thousand and six”. Answers: 40020, 60302, 81006. Ask pupils to explain zero columns without revealing the answers first.

Forward: 9990, 9991, 9992, 9993, 9994, 9995, 9996, 9997, 9998, 9999, 10000. Backward: 10003, 10002, 10001, 10000, 9999, 9998. Thousands: 12400, 11400, 10400, 9400, 8400. Returning to four digits is explicitly illustrated in the source, not an introduction to six digits.

Odd, odd, even, odd, even; 70009 is odd. Comparisons: <, >, =, <. Compare digit count, then equal-length numerals from highest place down. Verify pupil-generated examples by their actual values, not just whether a symbol appears.

Teacher · Observation record

小三 · 五位數

姓名 ______ 日期 ______ · 準備位值表及標有數值的數卡。完成小二相關先備後才使用。

1 · 十個千與萬

把十張「1000」卡排出，逐千數總值，再換成一張「10000」卡。說明卡數改變但總值不變。畫／填萬、千、百、十、個五欄位值表，表示 30405 及 70008；指出零佔哪幾欄。說出 30405 中 3 的值，不只說數字。數卡代表數值，不是實際一萬件物品。

2 · 讀數、聽寫、覆核

先讀 10000、10001、30405、70008、99999，指着數位解釋中間的零。再把這頁遮住，聽教師另讀三個數並寫成數字，讀回讓教師覆核。老師把口讀和書寫證據分開記錄。

數數、單雙數及比較

3 · 跨過萬位

由 9990 逐一順數至 10000，再由 10003 逐一倒數至 9998；逐個說及記錄。由 12400 每次減少一千，數至 8400。說明每次改變的值，不能只背最後一個答案。

4 · 個位決定單雙

判斷 10001、30405、70008、99999、20000 是單數還是雙數，圈出個位並解釋。把 70008 改成 70009 後答案怎樣改變？

5 · 先說後用符號

先口述比較理由，再填＝、＞或＜：30405 __ 34005；70008 __ 69999；20000 __ 20000；9999 __ 10000。同為五位數時，由哪一欄開始比較？兩數首欄相同時怎辦？自選一對五位數讓同伴比較並覆核。

教師 · 提問及答案

準備十張 1000 卡、一張 10000 卡、可重用位值表及各位數卡。學生說明 10 個千＝1 個萬；30405 是 3 萬、0 千、4 百、0 十、5 個，70008 是 7 萬、0 千、0 百、0 十、8 個。3 的值是 30000。

讀數：一萬、一萬零一、三萬零四百零五、七萬零八、九萬九千九百九十九。聽寫只由教師看這裏，依次讀「四萬零二十」「六萬零三百零二」「八萬一千零六」，答案 40020、60302、81006。必要時讓學生指位值說明，不預先展示答案。

順數 9990、9991、9992、9993、9994、9995、9996、9997、9998、9999、10000；倒數 10003、10002、10001、10000、9999、9998；減千序列 12400、11400、10400、9400、8400。跨回四位數是來源明示的例子，並非要求六位數。

單、單、雙、單、雙；70009 是單數。比較依次＜、＞、＝、＜。先比位數，同位數從最高位依次比較。自選題答案按實際數字覆核，不只檢查符號。
