5A2 Model, explain and check

1 · Balance

Build a balance model for x+3=7 with identical unknown bags and counters. Record both sides before and after removing 3 from each. Do not record a physical observation unless you performed it.

Original work, explanation and check; separate paper allowed.

2 · Eight forms

Solve the eight supplied equations on separate paper. For each show the same operation on both sides and substitute your answer into the original equation. Use a model when helpful.

  1. x+3=7
  2. x-3=1
  3. 2*x=8
  4. x/2=4
  5. 2*x+3=11
  6. 2*x-3=5
  7. x/2+3=7
  8. x/2-3=1

Original work, explanation and check; separate paper allowed.

5A2 Model, explain and check

3 · Detect an invalid step

A pupil changes 2x+3=11 into 2x=11 by deleting 3 only on the left. Explain the error, correct the step and check x. Invent another invalid one-sided change and explain why it fails.

Original work, explanation and check; separate paper allowed.

4 · Model a context directly

Three identical notebooks each cost x dollars; a bag costs 2 dollars and the total is 17 dollars. Define the unknown, write the direct relationship, solve, then check the total in words.

Original work, explanation and check; separate paper allowed.

5A2 Model, explain and check

5 · Sharing and checking

x counters are shared among 3 pupils. Each receives 2 more and finally has 6. Model the initial share and write the direct equation. Check the total original number, not merely the last arithmetic step.

Original work, explanation and check; separate paper allowed.

6 · Create, solve, revise

Choose one of the eight forms and invent a meaningful problem. Swap with a partner, solve and substitute to check. Record units, assumptions and any wording revisions.

Original work, explanation and check; separate paper allowed.

Supplied model answers

x+3=7 gives x=4; both original sides equal 7.

x+3=7; x-3=1; 2*x=8; x/2=4; 2*x+3=11; 2*x-3=5; x/2+3=7; x/2-3=1. Solutions: 4,4,4,8,4,4,8,8.

Subtract 3 on BOTH sides: 2x=8, so x=4; 2*4+3=11.

3x+2=17;3x=15;x=5. Check 3*5+2=17 dollars.

x/3+2=6;x/3=4;x=12. Check 12/3+2=6 counters each.

Accept only consistent direct models, a justified solution and substitution into the original sides. Do not prefill pupil evidence.

Teacher observation

Independent / prompted / not yet / not observed; record date, original work, pupil explanation, help and next step.

5A2 建模、解釋與驗算

1 · 平衡

用相同未知數袋及棋子建立x+3=7平衡模型，記錄兩邊同減3前後情況。未實作不可填寫觀察。

記錄原作、解釋及驗算，可另附紙張。

2 · 八類方程

另紙解八條示例方程，每條寫出兩邊同作的運算，並把解代入原式驗算，需要時建模。

  1. x+3=7
  2. x-3=1
  3. 2*x=8
  4. x/2=4
  5. 2*x+3=11
  6. 2*x-3=5
  7. x/2+3=7
  8. x/2-3=1

記錄原作、解釋及驗算，可另附紙張。

5A2 建模、解釋與驗算

3 · 辨認錯誤步驟

學生把2x+3=11只刪去左邊3，變成2x=11。解釋錯誤，改正並驗算x；另自擬單邊運算的錯誤例子。

記錄原作、解釋及驗算，可另附紙張。

4 · 直接列出情境方程

三本相同筆記簿每本售x元，袋售2元，共17元。確定未知數，直接列出數量關係，求解後用文字驗算總價。

記錄原作、解釋及驗算，可另附紙張。

5A2 建模、解釋與驗算

5 · 平均分配與驗算

x個棋子平均分給3人，每人再得2個，最後各有6個。建立最初份額模型並直接列方程，驗算原有總數而不只最後運算。

記錄原作、解釋及驗算，可另附紙張。

6 · 創作、求解與修訂

選八類方程之一自擬有意義的應用題，與同伴交換求解並代入驗算，記錄單位、假設及修改文字。

記錄原作、解釋及驗算，可另附紙張。

示例解答

x+3=7得x=4，原式兩邊均為7。

x+3=7；x-3=1；2*x=8；x/2=4；2*x+3=11；2*x-3=5；x/2+3=7；x/2-3=1。解依次為4、4、4、8、4、4、8、8。

兩邊同減3：2x=8，x=4；2*4+3=11。

3x+2=17；3x=15；x=5。驗算3*5+2=17元。

x/3+2=6；x/3=4；x=12。驗算12/3+2=6，每人6個。

須有符合情境的直接列式、有理由的解及代入原式驗算，不可預填學生證據。

教師觀察

獨立完成／提示後／尚未／未觀察；記錄日期、原作、解釋、協助及下一步。
