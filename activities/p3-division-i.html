P3 · Meaning behind column division

Name ______ Date ______ · Use hundreds/tens/ones place-value materials, six group circles, 24 large counters and paper/pencil. Reuse materials.

1 · Share place values and record columns

Model 68 with six tens and eight ones, and share into two equal groups. Record 60÷2 and 8÷2 separately and explain why their combined quotient is 34. Write the division in column form on paper: put quotient digit 3 above the tens digit 6, and 4 above the ones digit 8. Explain the value of every written digit, not just the final answer.

2 · Exchange when a place cannot share exactly

Use place-value materials for 72÷3. What remains after sharing seven tens among three groups? Exchange the remaining ten for ones and combine with the original ones. Then do 432÷4 and explain why a zero is needed in the quotient’s tens place. Write both calculations in column form, recording exchanges and subtraction of the amounts already shared.

Remainders and three-number division

3 · Remainders and checking

Use column division for 257÷6 and 305÷5. State quotient and remainder (write 0 when exact). Explain why the remainder is smaller than the divisor and check the original number using divisor × quotient + remainder. Explain each exchange and quotient alignment for one example to a partner.

4 · Left to right, not arbitrary regrouping

Calculate 24÷6÷2, 96÷4÷2 and 72÷3÷4 from left to right, recording each intermediate result. With 24 counters, first make six equal groups, then mark two spaces inside each group circle and share its four counters between those spaces, one for each person. How many per person? Separately, first calculate 6÷2 to get 3, then share 24 among three groups. How many per group? Are the outcomes the same? Explain concretely why changing the grouping changes the result; no formal associative-property term or bracket notation is required.

Problems and estimation

5 · What a remainder means

(a) Share 137 stickers equally among six people. What is the maximum each gets, and how many does the teacher retain? (b) Pack 125 pencils in full packs of four. How many full packs and unpacked pencils? (c) Seat 185 people in minibuses with eight seats each. How many minibuses are needed for everyone? For each, write an expression, column work, labelled answer and interpretation of the remainder.

6 · After mastering the method, use estimation

After the earlier division method is established, estimate the quotient of 248÷5 using a nearby easy-to-share number before calculating exactly. Record your estimate and reason, then divide in columns and judge reasonableness. You may consider 250, or explain another sensible choice. Repeat for 398÷4. Keep estimates distinct from exact answers; write whole-number quotient/remainder, not decimal quotients.

Teacher · Methods and answers

Provide exchangeable place-value pieces: for example four hundreds, forty tens and fifty ones, reused; six group circles and 24 large counters. Supervise safe handling. Observe pupils writing/explaining column steps; numerical answers cannot substitute.

1: 60÷2=30 and 8÷2=4, giving 34; quotient digit 3 means three tens. 2: 72÷3 gives two tens each, with one ten exchanged for ten ones; add the original two ones, then 12÷3=4, giving 24. For 432÷4, each group gets one hundred; three tens cannot give one ten to each group, so write 0 in the tens place; exchange to 30 ones, add two, then 32÷4=8. Quotient 108, not 18.

3: 257÷6 gives quotient 42, remainder 5; 305÷5 gives quotient 61, remainder 0. Checks: 6×42+5=257; 5×61=305. Explain exchanges and quotient alignment. Remainder is too small for another complete sharing round.

4: 24÷6=4 then 4÷2=2; 96÷4=24 then 24÷2=12; 72÷3=24 then 24÷4=6. First finding 6÷2=3 and then 24÷3 gives 8, not 2. Use concrete ordering rather than pre-teaching 3N4 bracket syntax.

5: (a) 22 stickers each, five retained; (b) 31 full packs, one pencil unpacked; (c) quotient 23, remainder 1, so 24 minibuses are needed. The last person needs a seat. 6: estimates may use 250÷5=50, exact quotient 49 remainder 3; 400÷4=100, exact quotient 99 remainder 2. Accept other justified nearby estimates, not only one prescribed method.

小三 · 直式除法的意義

姓名 ______ 日期 ______ · 使用百、十、個位值材料、六個分組圈、24 粒大圓片及紙筆；材料可重用。

1 · 分位值並寫直式

用 6 個十和 8 個一表示 68，平均分成 2 組。分開記錄 60÷2 和 8÷2 的結果，解釋為何合起來是 34。在紙上寫 68÷2 的除法直式，商的 3 對齊十位 6，4 對齊個位 8；逐步說明每個數的值。不是只填最後答案。

2 · 不夠分時換單位

用位值材料計算 72÷3。7 個十分給 3 組後剩甚麼？把剩餘的十換成一，連同原來的一再分。接着做 432÷4，解釋商中為何要保留十位的 0。兩題都寫直式，把交換和減去已分數量逐步記下。

餘數及三數連除

3 · 餘數與覆核

用直式計算 257÷6 及 305÷5，寫商和餘數（整除時寫 0）。說明餘數為何比除數小，再用「除數×商＋餘數」核對原數。選一題向同伴解釋每次交換及商的對位。

4 · 從左到右，不能任意換組合

依次計算 24÷6÷2、96÷4÷2、72÷3÷4，寫每步中間結果。用 24 粒圓片先分成 6 組，再在每個分組圈內畫兩個位置，每個代表一人，把每組 4 粒分到這兩個位置，說每人得多少。另試先算 6÷2 得 3，再把 24 分成 3 組，每組多少？結果相同嗎？用實物解釋改變先算哪一部分會改結果，不需用「結合性質」術語或正式括號。

解題及估計

5 · 餘數在故事中的意思

(a) 137 張貼紙平均分給 6 人，每人最多多少張？教師保留多少張？(b) 125 枝鉛筆每 4 枝裝滿一包，最多裝滿多少包，餘多少枝？(c) 185 人乘每車 8 座的小巴，全部人都要有座位，至少要多少架？每題寫算式、直式及有單位的答案，解釋餘數怎樣處理。

6 · 學會計算後，再用估計檢查

先完成前面的除法方法。現在未精算前，選附近方便平均分的數來估計 248÷5 的商，把估計和理由先記下；再用直式計算並核對估計是否合理。可考慮 250，但也可解釋其他合理選擇。另一題 398÷4 同樣先估後算。不要把估計當成精確答案，商和餘數分開寫，不用小數商。

教師 · 方法和答案

準備足夠可交換位值材料：例如 4 個百、40 個十、50 個一，可重用；另備六個分組圈和 24 粒大圓片。成人監督，不把圓片當食物。直式方法須學生親自寫及說明，數碼答案不能代替。

1：60÷2＝30、8÷2＝4，共 34；商 3 是 3 個十。2：72÷3：每組先得 2 個十，餘 1 個十換成 10 個一，加 2 個一共 12，每組再得 4，商 24。432÷4：每組 1 個百，3 個十不夠各給 1 個十，商十位寫 0；30 個一加 2 個一，32÷4＝8，商 108。0 不能略去成 18。

3：257÷6 商 42、餘 5；305÷5 商 61、餘 0。6×42＋5＝257；5×61＝305。先交換百／十，再分配，商位置要正確；餘數不足再完整分一輪。

4：24÷6＝4，4÷2＝2；96÷4＝24，24÷2＝12；72÷3＝24，24÷4＝6。另一分組先把 6÷2＝3，再 24÷3＝8，與 2 不同。只用具體順序，不先教 3N4 括號。

5：(a) 每人 22 張，教師留 5 張；(b) 31 包，餘 1 枝；(c) 185÷8 商 23、餘 1，需 24 架，最後一人不能留下。6：可用 250÷5＝50，精確商 49、餘 3；400÷4＝100，精確商 99、餘 2。接受有理據的近似估計，不把指定方法當唯一答案。
