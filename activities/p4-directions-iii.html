1 · Name all eight directions

Read the eight compass points and their abbreviations: N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW. Say which two main directions each intermediate direction lies between. Name opposite pairs. On the given diagram describe each point relative to O. These are supplied map facts, not your measured readings.

2 · Use an actual compass

In an adult-checked safe open space, follow your instrument’s instructions to identify its north-seeking end; do not assume a universal needle colour. Hold/place it level, wait for it to settle, find north, then locate all eight directions. Record your tool, place, date and actual observations. Stay away from magnetic interference. If unreliable, stop and change tool or location.

3 · Place objects and reverse the reference

Use measured compass directions to place two safe markers. Describe B relative to A, then A relative to B. Keep the reference explicit and record the two actual direction statements. Try a second pair using an intermediate direction. Explain why reversal gives the opposite direction.

4 · Turn yourself, not geographical north

Recheck one measured direction after turning your body. Separate geographical north/east from body left/right. Compare the two supplied maps: the north arrow rotates with the whole map. On the rotated map identify the north-east point from O and reverse that relation. Screen top is not automatically north. Record your own recheck separately.

5 · Plan and follow a safe route

With adult approval, choose a short unobstructed route using measured directions. Stop before EVERY compass reading; never look down at the compass while walking. No blindfolds. Avoid roads, stairs, water and vehicles. Record starting reference, readings, stops and any left/right right-angle turns. A partner may move while you give directions, but record assistance separately from your observed compass use.

6 · Make an original north-arrow sketch

Sketch your own measured arrangement and route with a north arrow and named reference points. Explain one faulty claim: “top of every map is north” or “B is north-east of A, so A is north-east of B”. Check your explanation using actual compass work and the supplied maps. Keep original drawings and readings; do not copy supplied labels as observations.

Teacher key and observation rubric

N North; NE North-east; E East; SE South-east; S South; SW South-west; W West; NW North-west

Intermediate directions: NE lies between N/E; SE between S/E; SW between S/W; NW between N/W. Opposites: N/S, NE/SW, E/W, SE/NW. In each given map A–H follow N,NE,E,SE,S,SW,W,NW from O. In the rotated map north points right; north-east is down-right on screen. B is north-east of O, while O is south-west of B. Actual readings depend on the pupil’s checked instrument and location; never prefill them. Observe compass use, reference statements and route safety separately.

小四 · 方向和位置（三）

姓名 ______ 日期 ______。實際儀器讀數須與給定地圖例子分開記錄。

1 · 認識八個方向

讀出八個方向及縮寫：N、NE、E、SE、S、SW、W、NW。說明各中間方向位於哪兩個主要方向之間，指出相反方向。按圖描述各點相對O的位置。這是給定地圖，不是你的實測紀錄。

2 · 使用真正指南針

在成人檢查過的安全空曠地方，按儀器說明辨認指北端，不可假定針尖必定是某種顏色。保持水平，待指針穩定，找出北方及其餘七個方向。記錄工具、地點、日期及實際觀察。遠離磁性干擾；若讀數不可靠，停止並更換工具或地點。

3 · 放置物件及交換參考點

按實測方向放置兩個安全標記，描述B相對A及A相對B的位置。明確指出參考點，記錄兩句實際方向描述。另用一個中間方向再試，解釋交換參考點後為何變成相反方向。

4 · 轉身不改變地理北方

轉身後重新檢查一個實測方向，分清地理上的北／東與身體的左／右。比較兩個給定地圖，北箭頭隨整幅圖轉動。在轉過的圖找出O的東北方，再交換參考點。畫面上方不一定是北方，另記錄自己的實測檢查。

5 · 設計及走一條安全路線

經成人同意，用實測方向設計短而無障礙的路線。每次讀指南針前必須停步，不可邊走邊低頭看；不可蒙眼。遠離道路、樓梯、水邊及車輛。記錄起點、讀數、停步點及向左／右轉直角。可由伙伴按指示移動，但協助須與本人使用指南針的觀察分開記錄。

6 · 畫自己的北箭頭示意圖

按實測畫自己的位置及路線示意圖，加北箭頭並命名參考點。解釋錯誤說法：「每張圖上方都是北方」或「B在A的東北方，所以A也在B的東北方」。用實際指南針工作及給定地圖檢查解釋。保留原圖及讀數，不可把圖上標記抄作實測。

教師解答及觀察表

N 北; NE 東北; E 東; SE 東南; S 南; SW 西南; W 西; NW 西北

中間方向：東北在北與東之間；東南在南與東之間；西南在南與西之間；西北在北與西之間。相反方向：北／南、東北／西南、東／西、東南／西北。給定地圖由O向外的A至H依次為北、東北、東、東南、南、西南、西、西北。轉過的圖北向右，東北在畫面右下方。B在O的東北方，O在B的西南方。實際讀數依學生檢查過的儀器及地點而定，不能預填。分開觀察使用指南針、參考點描述及路線安全。
