P3 · Triangle properties

Name ______ Date ______. Materials: ruler, set square, pencil, blank/grid paper, safe pre-cut strips and removable tape. An adult prepares strips of actual lengths. Printed diagrams and grids are not calibrated centimetre rulers. The stated lengths and right-angle marks specify the card properties.

A

Three sides: 4 cm, 4 cm, 4 cm.

B

Two sides meeting at the marked right angle are each 3 cm.

C

Three sides: 4 cm, 3 cm, 5 cm; right angle marked.

D

Three sides: 5 cm, 5 cm, 6 cm.

E

Three sides: 4 cm, 5 cm, 6 cm.

1 · Sort by properties

Using the card information, list ALL cards in each of these five groups: right-angled triangle; isosceles triangle; equilateral triangle; isosceles right-angled triangle; scalene triangle. A card may belong to more than one group. Explain using equal sides or a right angle. Turn the sheet: do the groups change?

2 · Keep every correct name

For A and B separately, copy every correct name from the five-name list above, then explain each choice. Does every equilateral triangle belong to the isosceles group? Does every isosceles triangle have three equal sides? Use D to check the second claim.

3 · Predict before testing

4 · Try, observe, explain

Try each actual strip set without bending, stretching, overlapping lengths or leaving a gap. Join endpoint to endpoint. Record what happens when the two shorter lengths have a sum equal to, smaller than or greater than the longest. For the successful set, check all three pairs of lengths. Explain why a straight line is not a triangle.

Draw and make

5 · Draw original triangles

On this grid or separate paper, draw two triangles of different sizes. Then draw a right-angled triangle and check its right angle with a set square. Any valid triangle is accepted for the first two drawings. A grid square is not necessarily 1 cm. Keep your drawings.

6 · Choose, make, check

Choose one of these sets of strips: (3, 4, 5), (2, 3, 5), (2, 3, 6) cm. Explain with the side-length rule why your chosen set can make a triangle. Build and fix a real triangle with the three strips joined at their endpoints. Show your teacher the closed model and your checks. Make a second, differently sized model using (6, 8, 10) cm strips. Record a sketch and any help received.

Practise triangle properties · Side-length practice · Screen answers do not certify drawing or making skill.

Teacher key

1. Right-angled: B, C. Isosceles: A, B, D. Equilateral: A. Isosceles right-angled: B. Scalene: C, E. Isosceles means at least two equal sides, so A belongs. Orientation does not change membership.

2. A: equilateral AND isosceles. B: isosceles right-angled AND right-angled AND isosceles. Every equilateral triangle is isosceles; the converse fails, for example D. Accept “right-angled isosceles” as an alternative name.

3–4. (3,4,5): yes, since 3+4>5, 3+5>4 and 4+5>3. (2,3,5): no; equality yields a straight arrangement, not an enclosed triangle. (2,3,6): no; the shorter sides cannot reach. Observe actual trials; a guessed yes/no is insufficient practical evidence.

5. Inspect original drawings for three straight sides, three distinct vertices, closure and nonzero enclosed area; verify the requested right angle separately. Accept varied sizes and orientations. These extra right-angle drawings are practice, not an assertion that EDB requires construction of every category.

6. Choose (3,4,5); require the pupil to apply the inequality before making. Observe both physical models, endpoint joins, closure and checking. (6,8,10) also works. Do not accept a photograph copied from the worksheet as evidence of the child constructing a model. Use teacher judgement for practical tolerances.

小三 · 三角形的性質

姓名 ______ 日期 ______。材料：尺、三角尺、鉛筆、白紙／方格紙、安全的預剪紙條及可移除膠帶。成人按實際長度準備紙條。印刷圖形和方格不是校準厘米尺。圖卡性質以所列邊長和直角記號為準。

A

三邊：4厘米、4厘米、4厘米。

B

直角記號旁的兩邊各長3厘米。

C

三邊：4厘米、3厘米、5厘米；有直角記號。

D

三邊：5厘米、5厘米、6厘米。

E

三邊：4厘米、5厘米、6厘米。

1 · 按性質分類

根據圖卡資料，把所有符合條件的圖卡編號分別填入以下五組：直角三角形、等腰三角形、等邊三角形、等腰直角三角形、不等邊三角形。一張卡可以屬於多組。用等長邊或直角解釋。轉動紙張後，分組會改變嗎？

2 · 保留每個正確名稱

對A及B，分別從上方五個名稱中抄下全部正確名稱，逐一解釋。每個等邊三角形都屬於等腰三角形嗎？每個等腰三角形都有三條等長邊嗎？用D檢查第二句。

4 · 實試、觀察及解釋

每組都用實物紙條試做，不可彎曲、拉長、重疊邊長或留下空隙；端點須相接。記錄較短兩邊之和等於、小於或大於最長邊時的結果。對成功一組，檢查三組兩邊之和。解釋直線為何不是三角形。

繪畫及製作

5 · 自己畫三角形

在方格或另一張紙上，畫兩個不同大小的三角形，再畫一個直角三角形，用三角尺檢查直角。首兩個只要是有效的三角形都接受。一格不一定是1厘米。保留繪圖。

6 · 選擇、製作及檢查

從(3、4、5)、(2、3、5)、(2、3、6)厘米三組紙條中選一組，用邊長關係說明選擇理由。把三條紙條端點相接，製作及固定實物三角形。向老師展示封閉模型及檢查結果。再用(6、8、10)厘米紙條製作不同大小的模型。畫草圖及記下所獲協助。

練習三角形性質 · 邊長練習 · 屏幕答案不證明會繪畫或製作。

教師解答

1. 直角：B、C。等腰：A、B、D。等邊：A。等腰直角：B。不等邊：C、E。等腰指至少兩邊等長，故包括A。方向不改變分類。

2. A：等邊及等腰。B：等腰直角、直角及等腰。每個等邊三角形都是等腰三角形；反過來不一定成立，如D。英文另一名稱right-angled isosceles也接受。

3至4. (3、4、5)：可以，因3+4>5、3+5>4及4+5>3。(2、3、5)：不可以，等號只排成直線，不能圍成三角形。(2、3、6)：不可以，較短兩邊不能接到。須觀察實試，只猜對答案不足以證明實作。

5. 檢查原創繪圖有三條直邊、三個不同頂點、封閉及非零面積，另檢查指定直角。接受不同大小及方向。額外直角繪圖是練習，並非聲稱課程要求製作所有類別。

6. 選(3、4、5)，製作前須應用兩邊之和的關係。觀察兩個實物模型、端點相接、封閉及檢查。(6、8、10)也可。只抄工作紙圖片不能證明學生親自製作。實物容差由教師判斷。
