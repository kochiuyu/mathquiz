Volume (I): build, compare and measure

1 · Occupied space

Handle two safe solid objects. Predict which occupies more space, explain the evidence and test using matching unit-cube models where possible. Record when shape alone is insufficient. Distinguish occupied space from surface covering or height.

2 · A cubic centimetre

Make or obtain a cube with each edge 1 cm. Check all three edge directions with a ruler. Trace the model, describe 1 cm³ and compare one edge with one face and the whole occupied space. Printed diagrams are not certified physical sizes; verify before measuring.

Volume (I): build, compare and measure

3 · Estimate then count layers

Build complete blocks 3×2×2 and 4×2×2 with identical cubic-centimetre blocks. Estimate each volume, count every layer including hidden cubes and compare. Rebuild one arrangement with equal volume but a different shape, keeping a record of your original prediction.

4 · Experience a cubic metre

With adult supervision, use metre lengths of string or light rods to model the edges of a 1 m cube. Estimate what fits inside without climbing into an unstable frame. Explain why the enclosed space defines 1 m³. No conversion to cubic centimetres is required.

Volume (I): build, compare and measure

5 · Derive volume formulas

Use unit cubes to build a 4×3×2 cuboid and a 3×3×3 cube. Record cubes per layer and number of layers, derive length×width×height and edge×edge×edge, and check a new example. Find volume from dimensions, not cube side from volume.

6 · Join without overlap

Join filled cuboids 4×2×3 and 2×2×1 along a face. Predict total volume, show the join, count or calculate both parts and explain why addition works only without overlap. Design a different two-part model, label dimensions and verify by layers.

Supplied models, not pupil observations

Volume is occupied three-dimensional space; comparison must use evidence, not height or visible faces alone.

A cube 1 cm on each edge defines 1 cm³. Length, face area and volume use different units.

3×2×2=12 cm³;4×2×2=16 cm³. Rearrangement of the same filled cubes preserves volume.

A cube 1 m on each edge defines 1 m³. A physical experience and safety observation must actually occur.

4×3×2=24 unit cubes;3×3×3=27. Count complete layers, including hidden cubes.

4×2×3+2×2×1=28 unit cubes. Verify no cube belongs to both components.

體積（一）：建構、比較與量度

1 · 所佔空間

觸摸兩件安全實物，預測哪件佔較多空間並說明根據，盡可能用相同單位立方體模型測試。記錄何時只看外形不足判斷，分清所佔空間、表面覆蓋及高度。

2 · 一立方厘米

製作或取一個每邊1厘米的立方體，用尺核對三方向邊長。畫模型，說明1立方厘米，分別比較一條邊、一個面及整個所佔空間。印圖不保證實際大小，量度前須核對。

體積（一）：建構、比較與量度

3 · 先估再分層數

用相同1立方厘米積木砌完整3×2×2及4×2×2方塊，先估體積，再逐層數包括遮擋方塊並比較。把其中一組重砌成等體積不同外形，保留原來預測。

4 · 體驗一立方米

在成人指導下，用1米長繩或輕杆做每邊1米的立方框，估計可容納甚麼，不要爬入不穩固框架。解釋所圍空間定義1立方米，毋須換成立方厘米。

5 · 推導體積公式

用單位積木砌4×3×2長方體及3×3×3立方體，記錄每層粒數和層數，推導長×闊×高及邊×邊×邊，再驗新例。由尺寸求體積，不從體積反求立方體邊長。

6 · 沒有重疊的接合

沿一個面接合實心4×2×3及2×2×1長方體，先預測總體積，標接合處，數或計兩部分並解釋沒有重疊才可直接相加。另設計兩部分模型、標尺寸及逐層驗算。

提供的模型，不是學生觀察

體積是三維所佔空間，須有根據，不能只比較高度或可見面。

每邊1厘米的立方體定義1立方厘米；長度、面積、體積用不同單位。

3×2×2＝12立方厘米；4×2×2＝16立方厘米，同樣積木重新排列體積不變。

每邊1米的立方體定義1立方米，實物體驗與安全觀察須實際進行。

4×3×2＝24粒；3×3×3＝27粒，須包括遮擋方塊的完整層。

4×2×3＋2×2×1＝28粒，核對兩部分沒有共用任何方塊。
