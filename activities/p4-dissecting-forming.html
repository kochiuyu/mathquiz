1 · Trace polygon boundaries

Trace the closed straight boundary of each example. Count outside sides, not internal seams. Identify triangles, quadrilaterals and the pentagon. Draw an open path and a curved outline: explain why these are not polygons. This warm-up is not a separate objective.

2 · Actually cut a non-square rectangle

Prepare a real non-square rectangle. Predict the pieces, draw a cut from one corner to the opposite corner, then safely cut it. Keep BOTH pieces. Check right angles and compare actual side lengths using tools. Do not claim all these triangles have two equal sides. An adult prepares safe materials and supervises blunt scissors as appropriate.

4 · Two different dissections

Draw two copies of a pentagon or another simple polygon. Plan TWO different valid cuts, predict the components, then actually cut and label all pieces. Keep original work and explain one property inferred from the source shape. The roof example is not a right triangle. Multiple verified constructions are valid.

5 · Actually form polygons

Prepare the two matching right-angled trapeziums shown by cutting your own checked rectangle along the indicated sloping line. Physically move and turn the two pieces to reconstruct a rectangle. Mark the shared seam and the outside boundary; check no gap or overlap. Then make another simple polygon from suitable pieces. Do not stretch or make extra cuts. Turning over is allowed only when you explicitly record it.

6 · Repair an incorrect arrangement

Inspect the incorrect arrangement. The pieces retain their original sizes, but do they exactly cover the target rectangle? Build and physically repair it without changing either piece. Explain why equal total piece area does not prove exact fitting. Keep your own outline and repaired arrangement, rather than copying a teacher solution.

Teacher key and observation rubric

Rectangle: two right triangles, not necessarily isosceles. The 6 by 4 example gives squared sides 16,36,52. Square: two isosceles right triangles; the example gives 16,16,32. Pentagon: rectangle and isosceles roof triangle (squared sides 13,13,36), not a right triangle. The two shown trapeziums each have exactly one parallel pair and right angles. Joining their sloping sides reconstructs the rectangle. In the incorrect placement, piece B is translated right, creating a gap and protruding outside the target. Move it left to restore the shared seam. Accept alternative constructions only after checking every piece, source partition, outside boundary and no positive-area gap/overlap. Observe actual cutting and forming separately.

小四 · 分割和拼砌圖形

姓名 ______ 日期 ______。保留原拼塊、預測、工具及檢查紀錄。所有圖都是示意圖，不是已校準的實物範本。

1 · 描出多邊形邊界

描出各例子的封閉直線邊界，數外邊而非內部接縫。辨認三角形、四邊形及五邊形。畫一條不封閉的路徑及曲線輪廓，解釋為何不是多邊形。此熱身不是另一個學習目標。

2 · 實際分割非正方形的長方形

準備一張非正方形的長方形。先預測拼塊，畫由一角到對角的切線，再安全剪開，保留兩塊。用工具檢查直角及實際邊長，不可聲稱這些三角形都有兩條等長邊。成人準備安全材料，按需要監督使用鈍頭剪刀。

5 · 實際拼砌多邊形

按圖示斜線剪開自己已檢查的長方形，製作兩塊配對的直角梯形。實際移動及轉動兩塊，重新拼成長方形。標記接縫與外邊界，檢查沒有空隙或重疊。再用合適拼塊拼成另一多邊形。不可拉伸或另外剪開；翻面須明確記錄。

6 · 修正錯誤拼砌

觀察錯誤擺放：拼塊大小沒有改變，但是否恰好覆蓋目標長方形？實際拼出並修正，不改變拼塊。解釋為何拼塊總面積相等不能證明恰好吻合。保留自己的輪廓及修正結果，不只是抄教師答案。

教師解答及觀察表

長方形分成兩個直角三角形，不一定等腰；6乘4例子的邊長平方是16、36、52。正方形分成兩個等腰直角三角形，例子為16、16、32。五邊形分成長方形及等腰屋頂三角形，邊長平方13、13、36，不是直角三角形。兩個梯形各有恰好一對平行邊及直角，沿斜邊接合可還原長方形。錯誤例子把B向右移，形成空隙並超出目標，向左移可還原接縫。其他方法須檢查全部拼塊、原圖分割、外邊界及沒有正面積的空隙或重疊。分開觀察實際分割和拼砌。
