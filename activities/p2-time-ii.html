2 · Experience and measure minutes

An adult times one minute while you quietly trace a pattern. Then use a clock or stopwatch yourself to measure two activities: listening to music and sorting cards, starting and stopping on an adult’s whole-minute signals. The adult keeps the target durations private. Read the start/end or elapsed display, record minutes and compare which lasted longer. Explain 1 hour = ____ minutes. Do not just copy a time told by the adult.

Time problems and a day

3 · Time problems

All are in the same afternoon. A meeting was at 3:50; Ming arrives 20 minutes late. When does he arrive? Reading starts at 4:35 and ends at 5:10: how many minutes? Music starts at 2:15 and lasts 60 minutes: when does it finish? Explain by counting minutes or using a clock.

4 · Time in context

A day has ____ hours. Match and read: school in the morning at 8:05, home in the afternoon at 3:20, lunch at 12:00 noon, and the boundary between days at 12:00 midnight. Which uses a.m.? Which uses p.m.? Label noon and midnight in words. Can an 8:05 clock face alone tell you morning or evening? Explain. Which day begins just after Monday ends at midnight?

6 · Compare three consecutive years

Compare February 2026, 2027 and 2028 using these calendars and the previous sheet. Which differs? Use the month totals to explain 365 days in a common year and 366 in a leap year; other month lengths are unchanged. No divisibility rule for leap years is needed.

7 · Find the missing calendar information

Attend every day, including BOTH the first and last dates. Mark the 2027 calendars: (a) January 30 to February 2: how many days? (b) Starting April 29 for 4 consecutive days: what is the last date? (c) Ending March 2 after 3 consecutive days: what is the first date? Explain why the starting date is counted.

Teacher · Key and prompts

Prerequisites are hours and half-hours; new evidence includes minute reading, real timed observation and calendar reasoning, not hand drawing. Provide a real clock/stopwatch, music/cards and pencils. Measured intervals must be whole minutes no greater than 60. In task 2 privately time music for 2 minutes and card sorting for 3 minutes, without announcing these durations beforehand. An adult signals exact whole-minute starts and stops; observe pupils reading their own clock/stopwatch. Repeat if a stop is missed rather than relabelling a non-integer duration.

1: A 3:07; B 8:42; C 11:58; D 12:01. The hour hand moves continuously; 11:58 is not yet 12. Digital order: 09:06, 09:11, 09:16. 2: actual one-minute experience; measured 2 and 3 minutes, with 3 longer; 1 hour = 60 minutes. 3: 4:10 p.m.; 35 minutes (25 + 10); 3:15 p.m.

4: Analogue E 8:05 a.m.; F 3:20 p.m.; G 12:00 noon; H 12:00 midnight, inferred from the scenarios. Digital contexts: 24 hours; 8:05 a.m., 3:20 p.m.; 12:00 noon and 12:00 midnight. A clock alone cannot establish morning/evening. Midnight at the end of Monday begins Tuesday; time continues. Do not require conversion to 24-hour clock notation or the potentially confusing 12 a.m./p.m. labels.

5: 31,28,31,30,31,30,31,31,30,31,30,31 = 365. 6: February 2026/2027 has 28 days; 2028 has 29; the additional day makes 366 in a leap year. 7: (a) 4 days: Jan 30,31 and Feb 1,2; (b) May 2; (c) February 28. Count both first and last days, not merely date subtraction.

Teacher · Observation record

Record independent / prompted / not yet / not observed, plus actual pupil words, methods and date. Ask “How do you know? Can you show me?” Elicit reasoning before feedback. Distinguish spoken, written and tool-use evidence.

6 · 比較連續三年

比較 2026、2027、2028 年二月月曆。哪年二月不同？用十二個月日數說明平年 365 日、閏年 366 日的差別；其餘月份日數不變。不用背判定閏年的除法規則。

7 · 用月曆找未知數

以下每天都參加活動，開始和結束日都包括在內。用 2027 年月曆逐格標記：(a) 1 月 30 日至 2 月 2 日，共多少日？(b) 4 月 29 日開始，連續 4 日，何日結束？(c) 3 月 2 日結束，連續 3 日，何日開始？解釋為何開始日也要數。

教師 · 解答及提問

先備是整點及半點；本活動新增逐分鐘讀時、真實分鐘量度及日期推理，不要求畫鐘針。準備真鐘／秒錶、音樂／圖卡、筆和月曆。量度活動只用不超過 60 的整數分鐘。活動 2 由成人私下安排音樂 2 分鐘、圖卡 3 分鐘，不預先透露時長，準確提示整分鐘開始及停止，觀察學生親自讀鐘／秒錶；若錯過提示，重新做，不把非整分鐘硬改作整分鐘。

1：A 3:07、B 8:42、C 11:58、D 12:01。時針隨分鐘連續移動，11:58 未到 12 點。電子時刻依次 09:06、09:11、09:16。2：一分鐘體驗；2 和 3 分鐘的真實量度，3 分鐘較長；1 小時＝60 分鐘。3：4:10 下午；35 分鐘（25＋10）；3:15 下午。

4：模擬鐘 E 8:05 a.m.、F 3:20 p.m.、G 正午 12:00、H 午夜 12:00，根據情境判斷。電子時刻情境：24 小時；8:05 a.m.、3:20 p.m.；12:00 noon／正午、12:00 midnight／午夜。單靠鐘面不能判斷早晚。星期一完結的午夜也是星期二開始，時間持續不斷。不要求把時刻轉成 24 小時制，也不要求用 12 a.m./p.m. 表示正午／午夜。

5：依次 31、28、31、30、31、30、31、31、30、31、30、31，共 365。6：2026 和 2027 二月 28 日，2028 二月 29 日；多一日，所以閏年 366 日。7：(a) 4 日：1 月 30、31 日及 2 月 1、2 日；(b) 5 月 2 日；(c) 2 月 28 日。開始和結束均計入，不能只相減。
