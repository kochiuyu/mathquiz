5 · Estimate a genuinely large quantity

Choose a book or a large arrangement of objects that is practical and safe to inspect. Plan how to estimate its words or items before counting everything. Choose and count actual sample pages, rows or groups; record how you selected them, units, variation and any excluded pages. Use a justified representative count or range with manageable arithmetic. Record your assumptions and estimate before checking. You do not need a formal mean formula, personal attendance information or an exact total. 6 · Compare, check and revise

Try a second sampling or grouping method on the same quantity. Keep both sets of actual sampled counts. Compare the estimates and explain possible bias, such as choosing only crowded pages. Validate a manageable part or use a trustworthy total if one exists; label what was actually checked. Which method seems more accurate, and why? Revise your estimate or range and state what remains unknown. If the total cannot be checked, do not claim a measured error.

Teacher · Prompts and key

Ten-for-one exchanges give 100000, 1000000, 10000000, 100000000. Read 100010001 as “one hundred million, ten thousand and one”; 203004050 as “two hundred and three million, four thousand and fifty”. Dictate different numbers without showing them: “four hundred million, twenty thousand and six” (400020006), “seventy million and three hundred” (70000300). Accept accurate conventional readings, including optional “and”; ask for place-value explanations, not exact-string oral scoring.

Fictional worked model only, not a pupil observation or exact total: a book has 80 comparable text pages. The model sample counts are 240, 250 and 260 words. If 250 represents each page, estimate 80 × 250 = 20000 words; using 240 to 260 gives an estimated range 19200 to 20800. Three sampled pages do not prove that every other page lies in this range. Another method uses eight groups of ten pages; if each group has about 2400 words, estimate 19200. The estimates differ by 800, but without the true total neither is proved more accurate. 5 · 實際估計一個大數量

選一本書或可安全觀察的一大批物品。先計劃如何估計字數或件數，不先數盡全部。實際選取並點算樣本頁、行或組，記錄選樣方法、單位、差異及不計的頁。用有理由的代表數或範圍，配合可掌握的運算。在核實前記下假設及估計。毋須使用正式平均數公式、個人出席資料或取得精確總數。不可把教師的虛構例子當作自己的觀察。

教師 · 提問及答案

十換一依次為100000、1000000、10000000、100000000。100010001讀作「一億零一萬零一」；203004050讀作「二億零三百萬四千零五十」。聽寫另讀「四億零二萬零六」（400020006）及「七千萬零三百」（70000300），不先展示數字。接受準確的慣常讀法，請學生用位值解釋；不要以單一字串評分。
