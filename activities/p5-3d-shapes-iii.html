P5 · 3-D shapes (III)

1 · Parallel slices

Use safe soft models or virtual models of a prism, cylinder, pyramid and cone. With adult handling of cutting tools, compare sections parallel to the base at two interior heights. Record shape and size separately; do not generalise to sloping cuts.

2 · Build frameworks

Make cube and non-cube cuboid frameworks with straws and connectors. Count each vertex and edge once, including hidden ones. Label your counting route and compare models.

P5 · 3-D shapes (III)

3 · Fold a cube

Cut the supplied six-square pattern, add your own tabs, predict which faces become opposite, then fold. Record predictions, the actual fold and any overlap. Explain why a 2 by 3 rectangle of six squares fails.

4 · Construct a cuboid net

Plan a 2 by 3 by 1 cm cuboid. Draw and label all six rectangular faces, match adjoining edges, add tabs and fold. Use the separate net sheet; verify print calibration with a ruler before using stated lengths.

P5 · 3-D shapes (III)

5 · Wrap a cylinder

Measure a round model boundary with string. Draw a rectangle whose wrapping side equals that measured length and whose other side equals height. Add two equal circles. Test a rectangle 2 cm too long and explain overlap; no circumference formula is needed.

6 · Investigate a sphere

Compare a ball, cylinder and cube by touch and rolling. Use a sphere model with a marked centre or software to compare centre-to-surface distances. With adult handling of tools, slice a soft sphere model through its interior at two different positions, or use a virtual model. Trace and compare the circular sections; exclude a plane merely touching the sphere. Record surfaces, edges, vertices and section observations without inventing results.

Cube net and your cuboid design

Check one square side against 2 cm with a ruler. Reproduce on squared paper if print scaling differs. Add tabs yourself. Use remaining space to construct a labelled 2 × 3 × 1 cm cuboid net.

Teacher models, not observations

Cube/cuboid frameworks: 8 vertices and 12 edges. The supplied cross folds to six distinct faces; a 2 by 3 grid overlaps. A 2 × 3 × 1 cuboid needs two 2 × 3, two 2 × 1 and two 3 × 1 faces.

Base-parallel interior sections of prisms/cylinders retain shape and size; those of pyramids/cones get smaller towards the top. A sphere has no straight edges or vertices; each interior plane section is a circle. Student model observations stay blank.

Record date, original work, explanation, assistance and next step. Mark independent / prompted / not yet / not observed.

P5 · 立體圖形（三）

1 · 平行切片

用安全軟模型或虛擬柱體、圓柱、錐體及圓錐，由成人操作切割工具，比較內部兩高度平行於底的截面，分開記錄形狀及大小，不推論斜切。

2 · 製作骨架

用飲管及連接物製作正方體及非正方體的長方體，逐一數頂點及棱，包括看不到的部分，標示點數次序並比較。

P5 · 立體圖形（三）

3 · 摺正方體

剪下六正方形圖樣，自加黏貼邊，預測相對面再摺，記錄預測、實際摺合及重疊。解釋為何六格2乘3長方形不能摺成正方體。

4 · 製作長方體圖樣

設計2乘3乘1厘米長方體，畫及標示六個長方形面，配合相鄰邊，加黏貼邊再摺。另用圖樣紙，先以尺核對列印比例。

P5 · 立體圖形（三）

5 · 圍成圓柱

用線量圓形模型圓周，畫長方形，一邊等於實測圓周，另一邊等於高，配上兩個相同圓。試比圓周長2厘米的長方形並解釋重疊，不用圓周公式。

6 · 探究球

觸摸及滾動球、圓柱和正方體作比較，用有球心的模型或軟件比較球心至球面距離，由成人操作工具，把軟球模型在內部兩位置切開，或使用虛擬模型，描畫並比較圓形截面，不包括僅相切的平面。如實記錄面、棱、頂點及截面觀察。

正方體圖樣與自製長方體圖樣

用尺核對正方形一邊是否2厘米；列印比例不同須另用方格紙重畫，自加黏貼邊。餘下位置設計並標示2×3×1厘米長方體圖樣。

教師示例，並非觀察

正方體及長方體有8頂點、12棱。十字圖樣摺成六個不同面；2乘3格會重疊。2×3×1長方體需兩個2×3、兩個2×1及兩個3×1面。

柱體及圓柱內部平行截面保持形狀及大小；錐體及圓錐向頂點的截面變小。球沒有直棱或頂點，平面切過球內部的截面是圓，學生模型觀察不可預填。

觀察記錄

記錄日期、原作、解釋、協助及下一步，註明獨立完成／提示後／尚未／未觀察。
