Products · Strategies · Problems

Teacher key and reasoning

1. 23 × 4 = 92; 23 × 10 = 230; 92 + 230 = 322. Four groups of 23 and ten groups of 23 combine to fourteen groups. The tens-row shift represents multiplying by ten, not an unexplained instruction to “add a zero”. Accept a correct model and pupil wording; no formal distributive-property terminology is required.

2. 48 × 27: 48 × 7 = 336, 48 × 20 = 960; total 1296. 243 × 26: 243 × 6 = 1458, 243 × 20 = 4860; total 6318. 105 × 24: 105 × 4 = 420, 105 × 20 = 2100; total 2520. The 0 in 105 denotes zero tens while retaining hundreds and units positions. Inspect actual carrying and alignment, not merely final answers.

3. 486 is 243 × 2, but the digit2 in26 represents20. Replace486 by4860; 1458 + 4860 = 6318. The addition1458 + 486 =1944 is correct, but it sums the wrong partial products.

4. One useful route: 25 × 36 × 4 = 36 × (25 × 4) = 36 × 100 = 3600. A second route: (25 × 36) × 4 = 900 × 4 =3600, or25 × (36 × 4)=25 ×144=3600. Check that all original factors are preserved. Observe the pupil changing both order and grouping and explaining the unchanged product; do not award reasoning solely for selecting a supplied expression.

5. Exact: 38 × 62 = 2356 pencils; partial products76 and2280. One sensible prior estimate is40 ×60=2400 pencils. Other justified estimates are possible; no single estimate is compulsory. Verify that the estimate was recorded before the exact calculation, and ask how the comparison supports plausibility.

6. 24 groups of126 seats: 126 × 24 = 3024 seats, using504 +2520. For example,120 ×25=3000 is a useful compatible-number estimate. Accept another explained check. A missing tens-place shift can produce an implausibly small result, but do not claim that every such mistake is exactly a factor of ten.

教師解答及推理

1. 23 × 4 = 92；23 × 10 = 230；92 + 230 = 322。4組各23與10組各23合成14組各23。第二行移位表示乘十，不只是沒有理由地「加一個零」。接受正確模型及學生自己的說法，不要求分配性質的專有名稱。

2. 48 × 27：48 × 7 = 336，48 × 20 = 960，合共1296。243 × 26：243 × 6 = 1458，243 × 20 = 4860，合共6318。105 × 24：105 × 4 = 420，105 × 20 = 2100，合共2520。105中的0表示沒有十，同時保留百位及個位的位置。須檢查實際進位及對齊，不只看最終答案。

3. 486是243 × 2，但26中的2表示20。把486改成4860；1458 + 4860 = 6318。1458 + 486 =1944的加法沒有錯，錯在所加的部分積。

4. 一個方便方法：25 × 36 × 4 = 36 × (25 × 4) = 36 × 100 = 3600。另一法：(25 × 36) × 4 = 900 × 4 =3600，或25 × (36 × 4)=25 ×144=3600。檢查所有原來因數都保留。觀察學生改變次序及分組並解釋積不變，不可只因選中提供的算式便判定能推理。

5. 準確答案：38 × 62 = 2356枝鉛筆，部分積為76及2280。一個合理的預先估計是40 ×60=2400枝。其他有理由的估計也可接受，沒有唯一指定估計。核實學生在準確計算前記下估計，再問比較如何支持答案合理。

6. 24組各126個座位：126 × 24 = 3024個座位，用504 +2520。例如120 ×25=3000可作方便的估計，亦接受其他有解釋的檢查。漏了十位移位可令答案過小，但不可聲稱每次這類錯誤都恰好相差十倍。
