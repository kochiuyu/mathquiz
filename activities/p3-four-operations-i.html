P3 · Order and brackets

Name ______ Date ______ · Keep intermediate working. Use paper, pencil, three trays and eighteen counters of two distinguishable types. No nested brackets or division exercises here.

1 · What do brackets change?

Calculate 12 + 8 × 3 and (12 + 8) × 3. Circle the first operation in each and explain why the answers differ. A class has 3 red boxes and 4 blue boxes, with 2 pencils in every box. Add brackets to 3 + 4 × 2 to express all the pencils; explain what the bracketed sum counts. Then calculate (2 + 3) × (5 + 3), keeping both bracket results.

2 · Three-number addition/subtraction

Calculate 2345 + 1876 − 958; 6000 − 2487 + 1365; 4321 + 1234 + 2100; 9000 − 2345 − 1678. Show first and final results, and explain order when there are no brackets. Compare 6000 − (2487 + 1365) with the second expression: do brackets change what happens?

Addition/subtraction with multiplication

3 · Explain the first action

Calculate 6 × 8 + 3 × 4; 90 − 4 × 12; 100 − (18 + 7) × 3; (15 − 7) × (9 − 4). Mark brackets and multiplication that must be completed before the remaining addition/subtraction. A pupil says 90 − 4 × 12 starts with 90 − 4. Explain and correct the method, not just the final answer.

4 · Recognise two counts of one collection

Put 4 counters of one type and 2 of another in each of 3 trays. Explain with the collection why 3 × (4 + 2) and 3 × 4 + 3 × 2 give the same total. Also examine the supplied pair (4 + 2) × 3 and 4 × 3 + 2 × 3. Next start with 6 per tray and remove 2 from EACH tray. Explain both supplied pairs: 3 × (6 − 2) and 3 × 6 − 3 × 2; (6 − 2) × 3 and 6 × 3 − 2 × 3. Point to what each product counts. You are recognising supplied relationships, not learning a rule for converting expressions.

Comparison stories and totals

5 · More/fewer AND altogether

Andy has 10 stickers. He has 2 MORE stickers than Betty. How many does Betty have, and how many do they have altogether? In a NEW situation Andy has 10 stickers and 2 FEWER than Betty. Answer both questions again. Model or draw both situations before choosing signs. Explain why the word “more” does not automatically mean add to Andy’s amount.

6 · Choose expressions from events

A school has 4 boxes of 18 pencils and 15 loose pencils. How many altogether? It then gives away 27 pencils: how many remain? Solve as two linked questions, preserving the intermediate meaning. Separately, there are 3 trays each containing 12 red counters and 8 blue counters. Write one expression with brackets for the total and explain the bracketed quantity.

Estimate and check after exact skills

7 · Use convenient numbers

After practising exact methods, estimate before calculating: 3980 + 2015 − 995; 198 × 4 + 103; 1000 − 98 × 3. Record your chosen convenient numbers and approximate results, then compute exactly. Are the exact answers reasonable? Explain why an approximate answer should not replace an exact answer requested in a problem.

8 · Explain the boundaries

Choose one earlier calculation and explain its order to a partner, including what brackets do. Use one concrete tray example to explain why its two supplied expressions agree. No formal property name, algebraic rewriting or nested brackets are needed.

Teacher · Order, models and answers

Keep key hidden before attempts. Teach/check prerequisite multiplication before mixed operations, then estimation after exact skills. For Task 4 supply 12 counters of one type and 6 of another, three trays; reuse the same 18 counters for removal. Record help separately.

1: 36 versus 60; (3 + 4) × 2 = 14 pencils, sum counts boxes; (2 + 3) × (5 + 3) = 5 × 8 = 40. Brackets first, then multiplication, then addition/subtraction in order. 2 first/final: 4221/3263; 3513/4878; 5555/7655; 6655/4977. The bracketed comparison is 6000 − 3852 = 2148, not 4878. 3: 60;42;25;40. In 90 − 4 × 12, multiply first to obtain 48, then subtract from90.

4: addition pairs each give18; subtraction pairs each give12. Point to red/blue collections and removed counters in all four orientations. This recognises the four supplied equality patterns. Do not require expanding/factoring expressions, the formal distributive property, or using this as a compulsory shortcut: those applications belong to4N5.

Teacher · Stories, estimation and rubric

5: “Andy has2more”: Betty8, altogether18. “Andy has2fewer”: Betty12, altogether22. Observe reference reversal rather than keyword rules. 6: 4×18+15=87 pencils, then87−27=60. The linked questions avoid a single five-number expression. Counter trays:3×(12+8)=60; bracket counts counters per tray. 7 example estimates:4000+2000−1000≈5000 (exact5000);200×4+100≈900 (exact895);1000−100×3≈700 (exact706). Other sensible estimates accepted with reasoning; coincidence with an exact answer in the first example does not make estimation generally exact.

Record independent/prompted/not yet/not observed with actual working: (1) chooses/uses brackets and explains their purpose; (2) three-number addition/subtraction up to four digits; (3) multiplication before addition/subtraction, with no more than four numbers; (4) recognises all four concrete supplied equality patterns without imposed conversion; (5) models more/fewer plus altogether and explains intermediates; (6) estimates and judges reasonableness. 小三 · 次序及括號

姓名 ______ 日期 ______ · 保留中間步驟。用紙筆、三個盤及十八粒可分兩類的圓片。不用多重層次括號或除法題。

1 · 括號改了甚麼？

計算 12 + 8 × 3 及 (12 + 8) × 3，圈出各自先做的運算，解釋答案為何不同。班上有 3 盒紅盒及 4 盒藍盒，每盒 2 枝鉛筆；在 3 + 4 × 2 加括號表示全部鉛筆，說括號內在數甚麼。再算 (2 + 3) × (5 + 3)，保留兩個括號結果。

2 · 三數加減

算 2345 + 1876 − 958；6000 − 2487 + 1365；4321 + 1234 + 2100；9000 − 2345 − 1678。寫第一步及最後結果，說明沒有括號時的次序。另算 6000 − (2487 + 1365)，與第二題比較，括號有改意思嗎？

加減與乘法混合

3 · 解釋先做甚麼

算 6 × 8 + 3 × 4；90 − 4 × 12；100 − (18 + 7) × 3；(15 − 7) × (9 − 4)。標示須先算的括號及乘法。有同學把 90 − 4 × 12 先算 90 − 4，解釋並改正方法，不只給答案。

4 · 同一集合兩種數法

每盤放一類 4 粒、另一類 2 粒，共 3 盤。用實物解釋已提供的 3 × (4 + 2) 與 3 × 4 + 3 × 2 為何相等；也觀察 (4 + 2) × 3 與 4 × 3 + 2 × 3。再每盤由 6 粒各拿走 2 粒，解釋兩對：3 × (6 − 2) 與 3 × 6 − 3 × 2；(6 − 2) × 3 與 6 × 3 − 2 × 3。指出各積在數甚麼。這是認識已提供的關係，不是學把算式互相轉換的規則。

比較情境及合共

5 · 較多／較少及合共

安迪有 10 張貼紙，比貝蒂多 2 張。貝蒂有多少？兩人合共多少？另一個新情境：安迪有 10 張，比貝蒂少 2 張，再答兩問。先畫圖或用模型，再選正負號。為何看到「多」不一定把安迪的數量加上 2？

6 · 按事件選算式

學校有 4 盒鉛筆，每盒 18 枝，另有散裝 15 枝，合共多少？其後送出 27 枝，剩多少？作兩條相連問題，說中間結果意思。另題：3 盤各有 12 粒紅及 8 粒藍圓片，寫一條有括號算式求總數，解釋括號內數量。

熟習精算後估算覆核

7 · 用方便數

熟習準確方法後，先估再精算：3980 + 2015 − 995；198 × 4 + 103；1000 − 98 × 3。寫所選方便數及估值，再算準確結果。答案合理嗎？為何題目要準確答案時不能只交估值？

8 · 解釋及範圍

選前題向同伴說運算次序及括號作用。選一盤實物例子解釋已提供的一對算式為何相等。不要求運算律術語、代數改寫或多重層次括號。

教師 · 次序、模型及答案

先讓學生嘗試，解答保持隱藏。先掌握乘法再混合運算，熟習精算後才估算。活動4用一類12粒、另一類6粒和三盤，拿走情境重用十八粒。援助另記。

1：36及60；(3+4)×2＝14枝，括號數盒；(2+3)×(5+3)＝5×8＝40。先括號，再乘，最後按次序加減。2第一步／最後：4221／3263、3513／4878、5555／7655、6655／4977。另括號題6000−3852＝2148，不是4878。3：60、42、25、40。90−4×12先乘得48，再由90減去。

4加法各18，減法各12。四種方向均指紅藍集合或被移去的圓片。只認識所提供的四種等式關係，不要求展開／提取因數、分配性質術語或把它設為必用捷徑；應用屬4N5。

教師 · 情境、估算及準則

5安迪多2：貝蒂8，合共18；安迪少2：貝蒂12，合共22。觀察參考關係，不用關鍵字猜加減。6：4×18+15＝87，再87−27＝60。分成兩問，避免單一五數算式。圓片3×(12+8)＝60，括號是每盤數量。7例如4000+2000−1000約5000（準確5000）；200×4+100約900（準確895）；1000−100×3約700（準確706）。接受其他合理估算及理由，首題恰巧等於準確值不代表估算必定準確。
