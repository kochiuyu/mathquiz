P1 · Early numbers and operations

1N1 / 1N2 supplementary pupil tasks · Name Date

Work in short sessions with your teacher. Say your answer before writing where asked. Use large counters, cards or drawings. This is not a timed test.

A · Read, count and write (1N1)

Read these numbers aloud: 1 · 6 · 10 · 14 · 17 · 20.

Count the objects in the teacher’s tray. Say and write how many:

From a pile of 20 objects, take out exactly 13. Count to check.

Write the three numbers the teacher says:

Count aloud by ones from 7 to 12; then from 16 back to 10. Now count from 17 to 20 and back from 20 to 17.

B · Which one? How many? (1N1)

In the teacher’s row, start at the arrow. Point to the first, third and fifth objects. How many objects are in the whole row?

Compare trays A and B by matching objects or counting. Tell the teacher which has more and which has fewer. Which number is greater?

C · Partners (1N1)

Make pairs with 7 objects. Is any object without a partner? Is 7 odd or even?

Add one object and pair again. Is 8 odd or even?

Try 19 and then 20 objects. Say odd or even and show why.

D · Parts and a whole (1N1)

Make one group of 4 and one of 8. Join them. Say what whole they make, then record: = +

Separate the same whole into groups of 4 and 8 again. Cover the 8. Say how many you can still see; then record: − =

Point to +, − and = and name each sign. Show 18 as two one-digit parts. Show 2 as two one-digit parts.

E · Pictures tell a story (1N2)

Use the pictures and move counters. For each question, say the answer first, then write a horizontal number sentence.

Join these groups: ●●●●   ●●●●●   How many altogether?

Start with ●●●●●●. Add ●●●●●●●. How many now?

Start with ●●●●●●●●●●●●●. Take away 7. How many remain?

A: ●●●●●●●●   B: ●●●●●   How many more does A have than B? Match the pictures to help.

In one addition sentence, point to the sum. In one subtraction sentence, point to the difference.

F · Nothing left (1N2)

Put 4 counters on a mat. Take all 4 away. How many remain? Say the answer, then complete 4 − 4 =

Use the mat and counters to show each action. Say and write each answer.

Start with none and add 4: 0 + 4 =

Start with 4 and add none: 4 + 0 =

Start with 4 and take away none: 4 − 0 =

What does 0 mean here?

G · Related actions (1N2)

Join 3 counters and 5 counters. Keep the two parts visible. Say and record the total. Take away the 3; what remains? Put them back, then take away the 5; what remains?

3 + 5 =   8 − 3 =   8 − 5 =

Now join 5 then 3. Compare with 3 then 5. Did swapping the groups change the total? Show why.

Start with 5 counters and take away 2. Can you swap these actions by starting with only 2 counters and taking away 5? Show or explain. Do not use negative numbers.

Teacher preparation and rubric

Use 20 large counters, two mats, six distinct objects, an arrow and paper. Reuse materials between tasks. Read prompts aloud without supplying the method or answer. Keep this key hidden until responses are recorded. These are samples of each objective, not an exhaustive test of every fact.

TaskSetup and expected evidence
APut 16 counters in an unlabelled tray. Dictate 3, 11, 18 without showing numerals. Expected count/write: 16; selection: 13; dictation: 3, 11, 18. Check oral reading of all six printed numbers separately. Counting: 7,8,9,10,11,12; 16,15,14,13,12,11,10; 17,18,19,20; 20,19,18,17. Observe one-to-one counting and endpoint inclusion.
BArrange six distinct objects in a row and place an arrow at its starting end. First, third and fifth must follow that direction; total 6. Make tray A with 8 and B with 5 counters. A has more; B has fewer; 8 is greater than 5. No inequality signs or numerical difference required in this 1N1 task.
C7: three pairs plus one, odd. 8: four pairs, even. 19: nine pairs plus one, odd. 20: ten pairs, even. Accept pairing or a correct verbal explanation; record both the classification and its evidence. Zero parity belongs to the later 1N3 supplement, not this 1N1 requirement.
DRequire actual joining/splitting and oral statements before recording 12 = 4 + 8 and 12 − 8 = 4. Accept equivalent correct horizontal statements for the same action. Name addition sign, subtraction sign and equal sign. Endpoint parts: 18 = 9 + 9; 2 = 1 + 1. This task records a decomposition/composition, not timed calculation. Check both one-digit parts.
EJoin: 4 + 5 = 9. Add on: 6 + 7 = 13. Take away: 13 − 7 = 6. Compare: 8 − 5 = 3; A has 3 more. Observe answer spoken before horizontal recording. Sum denotes an addition result; difference a subtraction result. All facts come from two one-digit parts with total at most 18. No column form.
FResults in order: 0, 4, 4, 4. Zero describes no counters remaining. Observe four separate concrete actions, not answers alone: remove all, add to none, add none, remove none. Do not require the term “whole number”.
G3 + 5 = 8; 8 − 3 = 5; 8 − 5 = 3. Observe restoration of the whole and both inverse actions; a correct equation alone does not demonstrate the exploration. 5 + 3 and 3 + 5 both total 8. With only 2 counters one cannot take away 5 in this context, whereas 5 − 2 leaves 3. Do not teach negative numbers or demand the term “commutative property”. Exploration stays within 10.

Observation record

Record I = independent, S = supported, N = not yet shown, U = unassessed for each item. No invented pass percentage. Record actual responses, prompts and accommodations.

小一 · 數與加減的初步認識

1N1／1N2 補充學生任務 · 姓名 日期

與老師分段完成。題目要求時，先說答案，再寫下來。可用大圓片、卡片或圖畫。本活動不計時。

A · 讀、數、寫（1N1）

讀出這些數：1 · 6 · 10 · 14 · 17 · 20。

點算老師盤內的物件，說出並寫下數量：

從20個物件中取出剛好13個，再點算核對。

寫出老師讀出的三個數：

每次數一，從7順數至12，再從16倒數至10。然後從17順數至20，再從20倒數至17。

B · 第幾個？多少個？（1N1）

從箭嘴所示的起點開始，指出老師排列的物件中，第一、第三和第五個。整排共有多少個物件？

把A盤和B盤的物件逐一配對或點算，比較數量。告訴老師哪盤較多、哪盤較少。哪個數較大？

C · 找朋友（1N1）

把7個物件兩個兩個配對。有沒有物件未能配對？7是單數還是雙數？

加一個物件再配對。8是單數還是雙數？

再試19個和20個。說出是單數還是雙數，並展示原因。

D · 部分與整體（1N1）

把4個和8個物件分成兩組，再合起來。先說出整體有多少，再記錄： = +

把同一整體再分成4個和8個。蓋着8個，說出還看得見多少，再記錄： − =

指出並說出 +、−、= 三個符號的名稱。把18分成兩個一位數的部分，再把2分成兩個一位數的部分。

E · 圖畫說故事（1N2）

看圖並移動圓片。每題先說出答案，再寫橫式。

合起來：●●●●   ●●●●●   共有多少？

原有 ●●●●●●，加上 ●●●●●●●，現在共有多少？

原有 ●●●●●●●●●●●●●，拿走7個，還剩多少？

A：●●●●●●●●   B：●●●●●   A比B多多少個？可逐一配對幫助你。

在一道加法算式中指出「和」，在一道減法算式中指出「差」。

F · 一個也沒有（1N2）

在墊上放4個圓片，把4個全拿走。還剩多少？先說出答案，再填 4 − 4 =

用墊和圓片展示每個動作，說出並寫下答案。

原來沒有，加4個：0 + 4 =

原有4個，沒有加任何圓片：4 + 0 =

原有4個，沒有拿走任何圓片：4 − 0 =

這裏的0表示甚麼？

G · 有關連的動作（1N2）

把3個和5個圓片合起來，保留兩部分的分別。說出並記下總數。拿走3個，還剩多少？放回那3個，再拿走5個，還剩多少？

3 + 5 =   8 − 3 =   8 − 5 =

現在先放5個，再加3個。與先放3個再加5個比較。交換兩組的次序，總數有沒有改變？展示原因。

原有5個，拿走2個。可以交換成原有2個，拿走5個嗎？展示或解釋，不用負數。

教師準備及評估準則

準備20個大圓片、兩塊墊、六件不同物件、箭嘴和紙，可重用材料。可讀出題目，但不要提供方法或答案。記錄回答前隱藏本解答。各題是目標的評估樣本，並非每個數或算式的全面測驗。

任務準備及預期證據
A在沒有數字標示的盤內放16個圓片。口述3、11、18，不展示數字。點算及書寫：16；取出：13；聽寫：3、11、18。分開記錄能否讀出題上六個數。數列：7、8、9、10、11、12；16、15、14、13、12、11、10；17、18、19、20；20、19、18、17。觀察逐一點算及有否包括起點和終點。
B把六件不同物件排成一行，在起點放箭嘴。第一、第三、第五須按該方向判斷；總數6。A盤放8個，B盤放5個。A較多、B較少、8比5大。此1N1題不要求不等號或相差的數量。
C7：三對及一個未配對，是單數。8：四對，是雙數。19：九對及一個未配對，是單數。20：十對，是雙數。接受配對展示或正確口述，分開記錄分類及理由。零是雙數在後續1N3補充活動處理，並非此1N1要求。
D須實際合併、分拆及先口述，再記錄12 = 4 + 8、12 − 8 = 4。接受表示相同動作的正確橫式。符號名稱：加號、減號、等號。端點例子：18 = 9 + 9；2 = 1 + 1。本題記錄分拆及組合，不是限時計算。核對兩部分都是一位數。
E合併：4 + 5 = 9；增加：6 + 7 = 13；取去：13 − 7 = 6；比較：8 − 5 = 3，A多3個。觀察學生先口答再寫橫式。「和」是加法的結果，「差」是減法的結果。全部來自兩個一位數的組合，總數不超過18。不用直式。
F依次為0、4、4、4。零表示沒有剩下的圓片。分開觀察四個實際動作：全取去、從沒有開始增加、增加零個、取去零個，而不只核對答案。不要求「整數」術語。
G3 + 5 = 8；8 − 3 = 5；8 − 5 = 3。觀察放回物件及兩種逆向動作，只有正確算式不足以顯示探索過程。5 + 3和3 + 5同是8。在只有2個圓片的情況不能拿走5個，而5 − 2剩3個。不教負數，不要求「交換性質」術語。探索的數在10以內。
