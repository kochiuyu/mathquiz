Teacher guidance and supplied-example checks

The three supplied charts show (20,40,60), (100,200,300) and (200,400,600), with scales10,50,100. Both orientations show the same rule: value = intervals × scale. These are examples, not observed pupil work.

Classification: Art5, Music4, Sport3, total12. Check the actual response list, not just a guessed total.

For genuine collection, inspect source, date, tally and category rule. There is no predetermined numerical answer. An absent observation remains not observed. Consider improving an overlarge “others” category; do not force an arbitrary percentage.

With six intervals AND whole-tick endpoints: 20/40/60 permits10 only; 100/200/300 permits50 or100, so50 is smallest; 200/400/600 permits100 only. A larger grid or subdivisions changes admissibility. Do not reject a defensible scale for unconstrained genuine-data charts.

Rounding:44→40,45→50,46→50;249→200,250→300,251→300. For145+145+145, exact total435 rounds to440; rounded categories150+150+150 total450. Preserve both exact and approximate values. Do not teach rounding to the nearest50 as a P4 requirement.

Construction is demonstrated by the pupil’s actual chart, not by selecting or copying a model. Check title, axes, units, zero, regular numerical intervals, equal bar widths/gaps, and each endpoint against the chosen exact/approximate table. Accept justified alternatives. Peer comments and teacher decisions must be actual records.

教師指引及提供例子的核對

三幅提供的圖分別表示20、40、60；100、200、300；200、400、600，刻度為10、50、100。兩種方向均按「格數乘刻度」表示數量。這些是例子，不是已觀察的學生作品。

分類結果：美術5、音樂4、體育3，共12。須核對原始回應卡，不只猜總數。

真實收集資料須核對來源、日期、畫記及分類準則，沒有預定數值答案。未觀察仍記未觀察。若「其他」太廣，可改善分類，不強訂任意百分比。

六格且數量落在完整格線上：20／40／60只能用10；100／200／300可用50或100，最小為50；200／400／600只能用100。增加格數或細分格線會改變可用刻度。真實資料若沒有此限制，不應否定有理由的刻度。

四捨五入：44→40、45→50、46→50；249→200、250→300、251→300。145＋145＋145的確切總數435取至十位為440；各自取值後150＋150＋150為450。保留確切及近似值。不把取至最接近50的倍數列為小四要求。

須以學生實際繪圖證明製作能力，選圖或抄示例並不等同。檢查標題、數軸、單位、零、等距刻度、棒形等闊及間距，逐一把末端與確切或近似統計表核對。接受有理由的其他方法。同伴回應及教師決定必須是真實記錄。
