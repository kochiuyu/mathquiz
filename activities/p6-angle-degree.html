Angle measurement and construction

1 · Degree and turn

Fold a paper circle in halves, quarters and eighths. Record the angle at its centre each time. Name the vertex in ∠ABC and compare clockwise and anticlockwise turns. Discuss a full round turn, but do not use 0° or 360° as a measurement/drawing target.

2 · Estimate then measure

Draw two differently oriented angles without labelling their sizes. Exchange sheets. Estimate first, then align a real protractor centre/zero ray and measure. Record the selected scale and explain why ray length is irrelevant.

Angle measurement and construction

3 · Construct acute and obtuse angles

Using a ruler and protractor, construct ∠ABC=35° and ∠PQR=125°. Label the middle-letter vertex, starting ray and degree mark. A partner measures each without seeing your written target.

4 · Reflex construction

Construct a 220° clockwise turn and a 305° anticlockwise turn. Mark the intended long path with an arrow. Explain the smaller angle used to construct each; verify with a full protractor or 360° minus the measured smaller turn.

Angle measurement and construction

5 · Orientation and comparison

Rotate the paper; lengthen one ray. Measure again and record whether the angle changed. Draw and identify a straight angle; contrast it with a reflex and a round angle.

6 · Explain a scale error

A pupil starts from the right-hand zero but reads the opposite scale as 140° instead of 40°. Explain the error with a drawing. Record a new example and a checking strategy.

Teacher model and rubric

1. Half 180°, quarter 90°, eighth 45°; the middle letter B is the vertex.

2. No invented measurement is supplied. Check centre, baseline alignment, chosen zero scale and whether the pupil compares the actual opening.

3. Overlay target 35°/125° and record actual deviation; do not claim a printout is physically calibrated.

4. Smaller angles 140° and 55°. Marking the long path is essential; a small arc alone is not a reflex answer.

Teacher model and rubric

5. Rotation and ray extension do not change angle size. Straight 180°; reflex greater than 180° but less than 360°; round 360°.

6. The two semicircular scales sum to 180° at the same tick. Start from the zero on the selected starting ray.

Teacher observations

量角及畫角

1 · 度與轉動

把紙圓對摺成二、四、八等份，記錄每次圓心角。指出∠ABC頂點，分清順時針及逆時針。討論周角，但不把0°或360°作量角或畫角題。

2 · 先估後量

畫兩個方向不同而不標示度數的角，交換紙張。先估，再把真實量角器頂點、零度線對準量度，記錄使用的刻度及射線長短為何無關。

量角及畫角

3 · 畫銳角及鈍角

用直尺及量角器畫∠ABC＝35°及∠PQR＝125°，標頂點、起始射線及度數。伙伴先不看目標度數作量度。

4 · 畫反角

畫順時針220°及逆時針305°反角，箭咀標明較長轉動路徑。說明用來作圖的較小角，用全圓量角器或360°減小角驗證。

量角及畫角

5 · 方向與比較

旋轉紙張及延長一條射線，再量度並記錄角是否改變。畫平角，與反角及周角比較。

6 · 解釋刻度錯誤

學生從右方零度起卻讀相反刻度，讀成140°而非40°。畫圖解釋錯誤，再自創例子及核對策略。

教師模型及準則

1. 半圓180°、四分之一90°、八分之一45°；中間字母B是頂點。

2. 不預填量度結果；檢查頂點、底線、零度刻度及實際張口大小。

3. 以35°／125°目標疊合核對，記錄實際偏差；不要假設列印圖已校準。

4. 較小角分別140°及55°；必須標示較長路徑，只畫小弧不算反角。

教師模型及準則

5. 旋轉及延長射線不改變角；平角180°，反角大於180°小於360°，周角360°。

6. 同一刻度的兩讀數相加180°，必須從起始射線所在的零度開始。
