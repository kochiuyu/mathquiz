Teacher preparation, script and rubric

Use up to 600 large counters, reusable trays/bags, paper and base-ten materials. Reuse between stations; do not put all collections out at once. Hundreds/ten groups must represent actual quantities (visible unit grids or countable bundles), not arbitrary tokens merely labelled 100/10. Keep small pieces away from mouths. Hide this key and target quantities until responses are recorded.

TaskSetup / expected evidence
APrepare an unlabelled collection of 134 counters, with trays for organising. Observe grouping/counting, then oral total and written 134. Ask the pupil to read the six printed numbers individually. Dictate 307, 590, 816 without showing numerals; allow natural local number-reading conventions. Record numeral writing separately from supported transcription.
BUse ten groups of ten objects to establish 100; pupil joins or matches them to one hundred representation. 242: two hundreds, four tens, two ones; digit values 200, 40, 2. 304: three hundreds, zero tens, four ones; 3 means 300 and 0 records no tens. Observe construction and digit meaning separately. No multiplication statement required.
C197,198,199,200,201,202,203; 302,301,300,299,298,297,296; 995,996,997,998,999; 104,103,102,101,100. 105 odd (5); 240 even (0); 402 even (2); 999 odd (9). Accept a clear ones-digit explanation; do not infer it from the classification alone.
D305 < 350; 628 = 628; 700 > 699; 340 < 403 < 430; 223 > 124 > 56. Listen to the spoken relationships before symbolic recording. Record magnitude understanding and the direction/meaning of the symbol separately. An accurate spoken comparison with a reversed symbol needs targeted symbol support.
EPrepare six groups for each station, reusing counters: 20 each, 25 each, 50 each, 100 each. Pupils check one group, then point and say running totals: 20,40,60,80,100,120; 25,50,75,100,125,150; 50,100,150,200,250,300; 100,200,300,400,500,600. All final totals are three-digit. Assess counting, not recalling a multiplication fact or writing an expression. If physical preparation uses visible grouped unit grids, each group must have the stated count.
FAfter A/E counting experience, prepare reference 50 and target A=138, then reference 100 and target B=275. Use similar objects and spacing, avoid revealing targets. Record estimate and reference reasoning first, then count to check 138/275. For example, roughly three reference groups may inform either estimate, but do not give that clue before the response or require a single answer. Judge a plausible reference-based estimate and checking process, not a fixed percentage tolerance. Exact counting before estimating is not estimation evidence. Both targets are below 1000.

Use I independent / S supported / N not yet / U unassessed. Record actual response and prompts for each: A counting __ reading __ dictation __; B ten tens __ hundreds meaning __ tens/ones meaning __ zero placeholder __; C forwards __ backwards __ parity __ ones reasoning __; D oral comparison __ = __ > __ < __ ordering __; E groups20 __ 25 __ 50 __ 100 __; F prior estimate A __ reference A __ prior estimate B __ reference B __ check/revise __.

This is representative assessment, not every possible three-digit number. No fixed pass score or speed requirement. Screen-capable items such as order and parity can later supplement these tasks, but cannot certify oral reading or real counting processes. Classroom, independent translation, physical-printer and screen-reader checks remain separate from material authoring. Browser/print/release QA pending.

教師準備、口述及評估準則

準備最多600個大圓片、可重用托盤／袋、紙及十進制材料。各站重用，不同時展示所有組。百／十的模型須表示實際數量（可見單位格或可點算的組），不能只是隨意貼100／10標籤的物件。小物件不要放近口部。記錄回答前隱藏本解答及目標數量。

任務準備／預期證據
A準備沒有數字標示的134個圓片及整理用托盤。觀察整理及點算，然後口述總數及寫134。請學生逐一讀出題上的六個數。口述307、590、816，不展示數字，接受自然的本地讀數方式。把獨立寫數與協助代寫分開記錄。
B用十組十個物件建立100，讓學生合併或配對一個百的表示。242：兩個百、四個十、兩個一；各數字的值為200、40、2。304：三個百、零個十、四個一；3表示300，0表示沒有十。分開觀察表示數量及數字意義，不要求乘法算式。
C197、198、199、200、201、202、203；302、301、300、299、298、297、296；995、996、997、998、999；104、103、102、101、100。105單數（5），240雙數（0），402雙數（2），999單數（9）。須有清楚的個位數字理由，不能只憑分類推斷已理解。
D305 < 350；628 = 628；700 > 699；340 < 403 < 430；223 > 124 > 56。先聽口頭比較再記符號。把大小理解與符號方向／意思分開記錄。口述正確但符號倒轉，須針對符號支援。
E每站準備六組，重用材料：每組20、25、50或100。學生核對一組，再逐組指出及口述：20、40、60、80、100、120；25、50、75、100、125、150；50、100、150、200、250、300；100、200、300、400、500、600。總數均為三位數。評估分組點算，不是背乘法或寫算式。如用可見單位格作組，每組須有相符數量。
F先有A／E點算經驗，再準備參考50個及A組138個，然後參考100個及B組275個。用相似物件和間距，不透露目標。先記估計和參考理由，再點算核對138／275。想像約三組參考物件可幫助這兩題，但不可預先提示或限定唯一答案。評估有參考依據的合理估計及核對過程，不設固定誤差百分比。先逐個點算不算估計證據。兩組均少於1000。
