Area (II): measure, cut and explain

1 · Matching heights

Draw an acute triangle, an obtuse triangle and a sloping parallelogram. Choose two different bases in each. Use a set square to draw the corresponding perpendicular heights; extend a base line when needed. Rotate your paper and explain why the same pair remains valid. Record your own lengths, not measurements from an uncalibrated screen.

2 · Rearrange a parallelogram

Make a paper parallelogram with base8cm and perpendicular height3cm. Mark the height before cutting a triangular end and moving it to make a rectangle. Trace before/after outlines, account for every piece and derive area=base×height. Create a second example with a different slant.

3 · Pair triangles

Make two matching triangles with base6cm and perpendicular height4cm. Join them to form a parallelogram, then explain why one triangle has half its area. Repeat with an obtuse triangle. Do not infer a missing side from a given triangle area.

4 · Double a trapezium

Make two matching trapeziums with parallel sides4cm and8cm and height3cm. Rotate one to form a parallelogram. Label its base and height, then derive the trapezium formula and test a different example.

5 · Two polygon methods

Draw a10cm by7cm rectangle and remove a4cm by3cm corner. Estimate the remaining area, then calculate by subtraction and by two nonoverlapping rectangles. Label all dimensions and explain why both decompositions cover the same region exactly.

6 · Design and investigate

Choose your own polygon design using rectangles, triangles or trapeziums. Write a question, estimate, select dimensions, calculate by two methods and revise any gap/overlap. Exchange with a partner who checks the base-height matches. Preserve your first attempt and explanation.

面積（二）：量度、剪拼與解釋

1 · 配對高

畫銳角三角形、鈍角三角形及傾斜的平行四邊形，各選兩條不同底邊。用三角尺畫對應垂直高，必要時延長底邊。轉動紙張解釋底高配對仍然成立，記錄實測長度，不可直接量未校準屏幕。

2 · 重組平行四邊形

製作底8厘米、垂直高3厘米的紙平行四邊形。先標高，再剪下一端三角形移至另一端拼長方形。描出前後輪廓，說明沒有缺口或重疊，推導面積＝底×高。另作斜度不同的例子。

面積（二）：量度、剪拼與解釋

3 · 拼合三角形

製作兩個底6厘米、垂直高4厘米的全等三角形，拼成平行四邊形，解釋每個三角形的面積為一半，再用鈍角三角形重做。毋須由三角形面積倒求邊長。

4 · 把梯形加倍

製作兩個兩底4厘米及8厘米、高3厘米的全等梯形，把其中一個轉動後拼成平行四邊形，標示新底與高，推導梯形面積公式，再試不同例子。

5 · 多邊形兩種方法

畫10厘米乘7厘米長方形，從角切去4厘米乘3厘米小長方形。先估餘下面積，再用相減及兩個不重疊長方形相加計算，標齊尺寸，解釋兩方法如何剛好覆蓋同一區域。

6 · 設計與探究

自選長方形、三角形或梯形組成多邊形設計。自擬問題、估算、選尺寸，用兩方法計算並修正缺口或重疊。與同伴交換檢查底高配對，保留初稿與解釋。
