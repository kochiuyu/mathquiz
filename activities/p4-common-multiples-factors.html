Teacher key and bounded checks

1. Factors of 4:1,2,4; factors of 6:1,2,3,6; common factors:1,2. First positive common multiples:12,24,36. For example, lists 4,8,12,…,36 and 6,12,18,…,36 show these. Only finitely many positive factors are possible for these numbers, but adding another 12 gives another common multiple. Do not require a formal infinitude proof.

2. Factors of 12:1,2,3,4,6,12; factors of 18:1,2,3,6,9,18. Common factors:1,2,3,6; H.C.F.=6. Positive multiples first coincide at 36, so L.C.M.=36. The value 3 is common but smaller than 6;72 is common but later than 36. Require the pupil’s actual separate lists.

3. Canonical shared-divisor ladder: (12,18) ÷2 → (6,9); ÷3 → (2,3), then stop. Left product 2×3=6 gives H.C.F.; 2×3×2×3=36 gives L.C.M. Bottom 2 and 3 belong only in the L.C.M. extraction. A single shared division by 6 is also valid if the pupil correctly reaches (2,3).

4. (8,12) ÷2 → (4,6); ÷2 → (2,3), then stop. H.C.F.=2×2=4; L.C.M.=2×2×2×3=24. Stopping at (4,6) is premature because both still divide by 2. Including bottom 2×3 in the H.C.F. incorrectly yields 24, which does not even divide 8 or 12.

5. Correct pairs (H.C.F.,L.C.M.): for (4,12)→(4,12); (1,8)→(1,8); (6,6)→(6,6); (7,9)→(1,63). Example (4,12) shared divisions 2,2 leave (1,3). For (6,6), shared divisions 2,3 leave (1,1), so H.C.F.=6 and L.C.M.=6×1×1=6. For (1,8) and (7,9), no shared divisor greater than 1 is available; H.C.F.=1 and L.C.M. is 1×8 or 7×9. Do not insist that input numbers differ.

6. Common multiples 12,24,36 are followed by 48, which is 4×12;48÷4=12 and 48÷6=8. The ribbon answer is 6 cm, the H.C.F. of 12 and 18; this gives 2 and 3 pieces. The bells next coincide after 12 minutes, the least POSITIVE common multiple of 4 and 6. Zero is the starting coincidence, not the requested next one. These contexts illustrate the four objectives; they are not an additional objective.

教師解答及範圍檢查

1. 4的因數：1、2、4；6的：1、2、3、6；公因數：1、2。首三個正公倍數：12、24、36。可用4、8、12、……、36及6、12、18、……、36兩表核對。這兩數的正因數有限，但再加12又得到另一公倍數，不要求正式無限性證明。

2. 12的因數：1、2、3、4、6、12；18的：1、2、3、6、9、18；公因數：1、2、3、6，最大公因數=6。正倍數首次同為36，故最小公倍數=36。3雖是公因數卻比6小；72雖是公倍數卻在36之後。須檢查學生自己的兩個表。

3. 共同除數短除法：(12,18) ÷2 → (6,9)；÷3 → (2,3)，然後停止。左邊2×3=6是最大公因數；2×3×2×3=36是最小公倍數。底部2及3只乘入最小公倍數。直接共同除以6而正確到(2,3)也可接受。

4. (8,12) ÷2 → (4,6)；÷2 → (2,3)，然後停止。最大公因數=2×2=4；最小公倍數=2×2×2×3=24。停在(4,6)太早，因兩數仍可被2整除。把底部2×3乘進最大公因數會錯得24，24甚至不能整除8或12。

5. 正確的(最大公因數,最小公倍數)：(4,12)→(4,12)；(1,8)→(1,8)；(6,6)→(6,6)；(7,9)→(1,63)。(4,12)可共同除以2、2至(1,3)。(6,6)可共同除以2、3至(1,1)，故最大公因數=6，最小公倍數=6×1×1=6。(1,8)及(7,9)開始就沒有大於1的共同除數，最大公因數=1，最小公倍數為1×8或7×9。不可要求輸入兩數必不同。

6. 公倍數12、24、36之後是48，即4×12；48÷4=12、48÷6=8。絲帶答案6厘米，是12及18的最大公因數，分別剪成2及3段。鐘在12分鐘後再同響，是4及6的最小正公倍數。0是開始同響，不是問的下一次。這些情境用來說明四個目標，不另算一個目標。
