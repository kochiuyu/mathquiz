5A1 Model, explain and check

1 · Letters for changing values

Choose a number of counters x. Make a table for x=2,4,7 and show x+3 with objects. Explain why x is a number, not the name of an object. Choose another value yourself.

Original work, explanation and check; separate paper allowed.

2 · Repeated groups

Use three identical bags containing x counters each. Write repeated addition and multiplication for the total. Open one bag only to check a chosen example, not to assume every bag question has that fixed x.

Original work, explanation and check; separate paper allowed.

3 · Sharing

Share a total x equally among four. Draw and write x/4. Choose totals 8 and 12, then write another suitable total. Compare with 4x and explain the difference.

Original work, explanation and check; separate paper allowed.

4 · Word order matters

Write expressions for three less than x and x subtracted from nine. Choose x=5 and compare. Explain which expression could represent a remaining physical quantity in your chosen context.

Original work, explanation and check; separate paper allowed.

5A1 Model, explain and check

5 · Brackets

Compare twice the sum of x and 3 with three more than twice x. Build both for x=4. Write a context for each and explain the grouping.

Original work, explanation and check; separate paper allowed.

6 · Create and critique

Create two different stories represented by 2x+7. Ask a partner to write the expression without seeing yours. Record disagreements and justify the final model using two chosen values.

Original work, explanation and check; separate paper allowed.

Supplied model answers

x+3 at 2,4,7 gives 5,7,10.

3x=x+x+x; at x=4 both give 12.

x/4 at 8,12 gives 2,3; 4x gives 32,48.

x-3 and 9-x differ; at x=5 they give 2 and 4. Keep the reference quantity clear.

2*(x+3) at 4 is 14; 2*x+3 is 11.

2x+7: two equal notebook prices plus a 7-dollar bag, or two equal ribbon lengths plus 7 cm. Pupil stories require independent review.

Teacher observation

Independent / prompted / not yet / not observed; record date, original work, pupil explanation, help and next step.

5A1 建模、解釋與驗算

1 · 字母表示可變值

選x個棋子，分別用x=2、4、7，以實物表示x+3並列出數表。解釋x是數而非物件名稱，另自選一個數值。

記錄原作、解釋及驗算，可另附紙張。

2 · 重複組合

三個袋各有x個棋子，寫出總數的連加及乘法表示。只選一個例子打開袋驗算，不可認定所有題目的x相同。

記錄原作、解釋及驗算，可另附紙張。

3 · 平均分配

把總數x平均分成4份，畫圖並寫x/4。試總數8及12，再自選總數，比較4x並解釋差異。

記錄原作、解釋及驗算，可另附紙張。

4 · 文字次序

寫出比x少3及從9減去x的代數式，代入x=5比較，解釋哪一個可表示自選情境的實際餘量。

記錄原作、解釋及驗算，可另附紙張。

5 · 括號

比較x與3的和的2倍，以及x的2倍多3。用x=4建模，為兩式分別寫情境並解釋組合。

記錄原作、解釋及驗算，可另附紙張。

6 · 創作與評議

自擬兩個可用2x+7表示的不同情境，請同伴在不看答案下列式，記錄分歧並用兩個自選數值解釋。

記錄原作、解釋及驗算，可另附紙張。

示例解答

x+3在x=2、4、7時為5、7、10。

3x=x+x+x；x=4時都等於12。

x/4在x=8、12時為2、3；4x則為32、48。

x-3及9-x不同；x=5時為2及4。須分清參考數量。

x=4時2*(x+3)=14；2*x+3=11。

2x+7可表示兩本同價筆記簿加7元的袋，或兩段等長絲帶加7厘米。須獨立檢視學生故事。
