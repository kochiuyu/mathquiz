P2 · Sharing and grouping

2N6 · Name ________ Date ________ · Work in short sessions with objects and extra paper.

1. Share equally: how many in each?

Share 12 counters equally among 3 trays. Tell the result first. Draw the trays and counters, write a sentence, then write a division statement.

Now share 14 counters among the same 3 trays. Keep counters whole. Give everyone as many as possible, equally. Say how many each gets and how many remain. Draw and record the new result.

2. Make equal groups: how many groups?

Start with 12 counters. Make groups of exactly 3. How many full groups? Say, draw and record. Now do the same with 14 counters. How many full groups and loose counters?

Both tasks use division by 3, but what does the 3 mean in task 1 and in task 2? What does each quotient count?

3. Why must the remainder be smaller?

Someone records 14 ÷ 3 as quotient 3, remainder 5. Build this with counters. Can you make another full group, or give one more to each of 3 trays? Improve the result and explain.

In your correct statement, point to the dividend, division sign, divisor, quotient and remainder. Say what each means in this situation.

Calculate, check and interpret

4. Division facts

Write the quotient and remainder for each. Put 0 for no remainder. Use a table fact or counters, not a written long-division algorithm.

24 ÷ 6: ________ / ________   35 ÷ 5: ________ / ________

17 ÷ 5: ________ / ________   3 ÷ 5: ________ / ________

0 ÷ 4: ________ / ________

Show why zero counters shared among 4 trays gives zero each. None of these questions divides by zero.

5. Multiply to check

Build 3 groups of 4 counters. Record the multiplication total. Use the same counters to show 12 ÷ 3 and 12 ÷ 4. Explain the connection.

Check 17 ÷ 5: multiply the divisor by the quotient, then count the remainder back in. Does this recover 17? Why is checking only the multiplication not enough when there is a remainder?

6. Can you swap the division numbers?

Make groups of 2 from 8 counters. Then start again: make groups of 8 from 2 counters, without breaking counters. Compare full groups and remainders. Does swapping the two numbers keep the result? Demonstrate rather than just naming a rule.

Use division in stories

7. Decide what happens to the remainder

a. Share 17 stickers equally among 5 children. Keep stickers whole. How many does each get, and how many stay with the teacher? Show a model, write division and explain the answer with units.

b. Pack 17 pencils, exactly 5 in each full pack. How many full packs can be sold, and how many pencils remain unpacked? Explain why the quotient counts packs, not pencils.

c. All 17 children need seats. Each small table seats at most 5 children. How many tables are needed? Use counters to show where the remaining children sit. A partly filled last table is allowed.

For each story: calculation ________ meaning of quotient/remainder ________ answer and reason ________. Use extra paper or tell the teacher.

Teacher worked key and feedback

Prepare 35 large counters, 8 trays or drawn circles, paper and pencils. Reuse materials. Keep counters away from mouths. Pupils first describe orally, then draw, use words and record symbols. Adult transcription/tactile adaptations are acceptable when recorded; do not infer independent writing.

1–2

12 ÷ 3 = 4 remainder0;14 ÷ 3 = 4 remainder2. Sharing:3 is the number of trays and4 counters per tray. Grouping:3 counters per group and4 full groups. Same numeric answers, different meanings. Models must match the interpretation, with leftovers separate. If unequal sharing occurs, ask whether all trays received the same number.

3

3 groups of3 use9 and leave5, but another group of3 is possible. Final4 groups with2 left; or distribute one more to each of3 trays. Therefore remainder2 is smaller than divisor3. Vocabulary:14 dividend,÷division sign,3 divisor,4 quotient,2 remainder. Rebuilding is the feedback, not just replacing a digit.

4

In order: quotient 4, remainder 0; quotient 7, remainder 0; quotient 3, remainder 2; quotient 0, remainder 3; quotient 0, remainder 0. For3÷5 there are not enough to make even one full group; all3 remain. Zero divided by nonzero4 is zero. Do not introduce fractional quotients or any zero-divisor case.

5

3×4=12 or4×3=12.12÷3=4 and12÷4=3. For17÷5:5×3=15;15 plus2 recovers17 and2 is smaller than5. Accept either factor order. If product alone gives15, ask where the two leftover counters went.

6

8÷2 gives4 groups, no remainder.2÷8 gives0 full groups and2 remaining. Results differ, so division order cannot generally be swapped. Concrete whole-group interpretation avoids needing fractions; no formal property name required.

7

All use17÷5 with quotient3/remainder2. a each child3 stickers, teacher keeps2. b3 full packs,2 pencils unpacked. c4 tables:3 full tables seat15, the remaining2 need one more table. Do not mark3 tables correct merely because the quotient is3. Conversely,4 full packs is wrong for b. Require contextual units and a reason; a numeric quotient alone does not assess remainder handling.

小二 · 平均分配與分組

2N6 · 姓名 ________ 日期 ________ · 分短節用實物操作，可另用白紙。

1. 平均分配：每組有多少？

把12個圓片平均分到3個盤。先口述結果，再畫出盤及圓片，寫一句說明，然後寫除法算式。

再把14個圓片平均分到同樣3個盤，不切開圓片。盡量公平分完，口述每盤數量和餘數，再畫圖並記錄。

2. 等量分組：有多少組？

用12個圓片，每3個一組，有多少完整組？先口述，再畫圖記錄。改用14個再做，有多少完整組和散片？

兩題都除以3，但第1和第2題的3各表示甚麼？兩個商各表示甚麼？

3. 為甚麼餘數必須較小？

有人把14÷3記成商3、餘5。用圓片擺出來，還能多組一個完整組，或給3個盤各多一個嗎？改正並解釋。

在正確算式中指出被除數、除號、除數、商及餘數，說出在本題的意思。

計算、驗算及解釋

4. 基本除法

每題寫商及餘數，沒有餘數寫0。可用乘法表或圓片，不要求長除法。

24 ÷ 6: ________ / ________   35 ÷ 5: ________ / ________

17 ÷ 5: ________ / ________   3 ÷ 5: ________ / ________

0 ÷ 4: ________ / ________

展示零個圓片平均分到4個盤，為甚麼每盤是零。本活動沒有除以零的題目。

5. 用乘法驗算

擺出3組，每組4個，寫乘法總數。用同一批圓片展示12÷3和12÷4，解釋關係。

驗算17÷5：把除數乘商，再加回餘下圓片，是否得到17？有餘數時，為甚麼只核對乘法還不夠？

6. 除法的兩個數可交換嗎？

把8個圓片每2個一組。重新開始，用2個圓片每8個一組，不切開圓片。比較完整組數和餘數，交換兩數後結果相同嗎？用實物示範，不只說規則名稱。

在情境中運用除法

7. 決定怎樣處理餘數

a. 把17張貼紙平均分給5個小朋友，不剪開貼紙。每人得多少張？多少張由教師保留？展示模型，寫除法，並用語句及單位解釋。

b. 把17枝鉛筆每5枝裝成一個完整包裝。可出售多少完整包？多少枝未裝？解釋為甚麼商表示包數，不是枝數。

c. 17個小朋友都要有座位，每張小桌最多坐5人。需要多少張桌？用圓片展示餘下小朋友坐哪裏，最後一張桌可以不坐滿。

每題記錄：算式 ________ 商／餘數的意思 ________ 答案及理由 ________。可加紙或向教師口述。

教師解答及回饋

準備35個大圓片、8個盤或畫出的圈、紙筆，材料可重用。圓片不要放近口部。學生先口述，再畫圖、用文字及符號記錄。可記錄成人代寫／觸感調整，但不當作獨立書寫證據。

1–2

12÷3＝4，餘0；14÷3＝4，餘2。平均分配：3是盤數，4是每盤圓片數。分組：3是每組數，4是完整組數。數字答案相同，但意義不同。模型須符合解釋，餘片另放。若分配不均，問各盤是否相同。

3

3組每組3用去9，餘5，仍可多組一組3。最後4組餘2；或給3盤各多一個。因此餘2比除數3小。14被除數、÷除號、3除數、4商、2餘數。回饋應讓學生重擺，不只改數字。

4

商／餘數依次：4／0、7／0、3／2、0／3、0／0。3÷5不夠組成一個完整組，全部3個餘下。零除以非零的4是零。不引入分數商或除數為零。

5

3×4＝12或4×3＝12；12÷3＝4及12÷4＝3。17÷5：5×3＝15，15再加2得17，而且2比5小。乘法次序均可。若只算15，問餘下兩個去了哪裏。

6

8÷2得4組，無餘數；2÷8得0完整組，餘2。結果不同，所以除法不能隨意交換次序。用完整組解釋，不需分數或規則名稱。

7

三題均17÷5，商3餘2。a每人3張，教師保留2張；b裝滿3包，餘2枝；c需要4張桌：3張坐15人，餘2人需多一張。不可只因商是3而接受3張桌；b也不能說4個完整包。須有情境單位及理由，單有商不足以顯示餘數處理。

觀察記錄及範圍

獨：獨立／助：協助／未：尚未展示／無：未觀察。記錄動作及說話，不只記錄參與。不自訂合格分數。
