P3 · Tell time without a.m./p.m.

Name ______ Date ______ · Use paper, pencil and printed time cards. No duration calculations in this unit.

1 · Why change the notation?

Two invitations say “Meet at 7:30”, one meaning morning and the other evening. Explain the possible confusion. Rewrite both using 24-hour time. Say which one comes before noon; do not calculate the interval between them.

2 · Read the pairs

On 06:05, circle the pair showing hours and underline the pair showing minutes. Read it aloud. Explain why 06:05 is not 6 hours 50 minutes. Write 9:07 a.m. using two digits for each pair.

Noon and the day boundary

3 · One day has two boundaries

On a line labelled Monday, write 00:00 at its START, 12:00 at noon, and 24:00 at its END. Explain why Monday 24:00 and Tuesday 00:00 meet at the same boundary but refer to different named-day labels. Is Monday 00:00 the same boundary as Monday 24:00? Explain without subtracting times.

4 · Sort the cards

Label each as morning, noon, afternoon/evening, start of day or end of day: 00:00, 00:05, 11:59, 12:00, 12:15, 13:05, 20:08, 23:59, 24:00. Match 12 noon to its card. Distinguish 12:15 p.m. from 12:15 a.m. Explain why 24:30 is not used within the stated day.

Read, write and explain

5 · Write 24-hour times

Write these times using HH:MM: 6:30 a.m.; 9:07 a.m.; 12 noon; 12:15 p.m.; 1:05 p.m.; 4:45 p.m.; 8:08 p.m.; 11:59 p.m.; midnight at the START of Wednesday; midnight at the END of Wednesday. Explain the rule, including the exceptions around twelve.

6 · Tell the time in context

A library notice lists opening at 09:07, a story at 13:05 and an evening event at 20:08. Tell each in 12-hour words with morning/afternoon/evening. Then a partner reads a different card from task 4; write its 24-hour time and explain both pairs. Correct: “12 noon is 00:00” and “00:05 is five minutes after noon”. Do not calculate opening hours or event durations.

Try writing and reading digital practice.

Teacher key

  1. 07:30 and 19:30; first is before noon. Omitting morning/evening creates ambiguity.
  2. 06 hours, 05 minutes: 6:05 a.m. The minute pair is 05, not 50. 09:07.
  3. Monday 00:00 starts Monday; Monday 24:00 ends it and coincides with Tuesday 00:00. Monday 00:00 and Monday 24:00 are different boundaries.
  4. 00:00 start; 00:05 shortly after the start (12:05 a.m.); 11:59 morning; 12:00 noon; 12:15 and 13:05 afternoon; 20:08 and 23:59 evening/night; 24:00 end. 12:15 p.m. is 12:15, while 12:15 a.m. is 00:15. Within the named day, hour 24 is used only at 24:00 for its ending boundary.
  5. 06:30; 09:07; 12:00; 12:15; 13:05; 16:45; 20:08; 23:59; 00:00; 24:00. Minutes stay unchanged. Morning 1–11 stay; afternoon/evening 1–11 add 12; noon stays 12 and midnight at the start is 00.
  6. 9:07 a.m.; 1:05 p.m.; 8:08 p.m. Noon is 12:00; 00:05 is 12:05 a.m., after midnight, not noon.

Teacher evidence and limits

Record independently / prompted / not yet / not observed for: (1) recognising HH:MM and 00:00/24:00 with a named day; (2) telling and writing times and relating 12-hour and 24-hour forms. Collect written cards and oral explanations. Accept clear spoken equivalents; require both hour and minute pairs in written HH:MM. A correct choice alone does not demonstrate oral explanation. Review midnight/noon separately.

小三 · 不用上午／下午也能報時

姓名 ______ 日期 ______ · 使用紙、筆及時間卡。本單元不計算時段長短。

1 · 為何改用另一種寫法？

兩張邀請都寫「7:30見面」，一張指早上，一張指晚上。說明可能產生的混淆，用二十四小時制分別重寫。指出哪個在中午前，不用計算兩者相隔多久。

2 · 讀出兩組數字

在06:05圈出表示時的兩位數字，並在表示分的兩位數字下面畫線。讀出時間，解釋為何不是6時50分。把上午9時7分寫成每組各兩位數字。

中午及一天的分界

3 · 一天的兩個邊界

在標示星期一的線段，開始處寫00:00，中午寫12:00，結束處寫24:00。解釋星期一24:00和星期二00:00為何是同一分界，卻用了不同日子的標示。星期一00:00和星期一24:00是同一分界嗎？不用相減，說明原因。

4 · 分類時間卡

把各卡標示為上午、中午、下午／晚上、一天開始或一天結束：00:00、00:05、11:59、12:00、12:15、13:05、20:08、23:59、24:00。配對中午12時的卡。分清下午12時15分和凌晨12時15分。解釋為何所述日子內不用24:30。

讀、寫及解釋

5 · 寫二十四小時制時間

用HH:MM寫出：上午6時30分、上午9時7分、中午12時、下午12時15分、下午1時5分、下午4時45分、晚上8時8分、晚上11時59分、星期三開始時的午夜、星期三結束時的午夜。解釋規律，包括十二時附近的特別情況。

6 · 在情境中報時

圖書館告示列出09:07開門、13:05講故事、20:08舉行晚間活動。用十二小時制讀出各時間，說明上午、下午或晚上。再請同伴讀另一張任務4時間卡，你寫二十四小時制並解釋兩組數字。改正：「中午12時是00:00」和「00:05是中午後五分鐘」。不要計算開放時間或活動時長。

試做數碼寫時間及讀時間練習。

教師解答

  1. 07:30及19:30；前者在中午前。省略早上／晚上可能造成誤會。
  2. 06表示時，05表示分，即上午6時5分；分鐘不是50。上午9時7分寫09:07。
  3. 星期一00:00是星期一開始；星期一24:00是結束，與星期二00:00重合。星期一00:00和星期一24:00是不同分界。
  4. 00:00是開始；00:05是開始後不久，即凌晨12時5分；11:59上午；12:00中午；12:15和13:05下午；20:08和23:59晚上；24:00結束。下午12時15分是12:15，凌晨12時15分是00:15。在所述日子內，時數24只用於24:00這個結束分界。
  5. 06:30；09:07；12:00；12:15；13:05；16:45；20:08；23:59；00:00；24:00。分鐘不變；上午1至11時不變；下午／晚上1至11時加12；中午保留12；開始時的午夜用00。
  6. 上午9時7分、下午1時5分、晚上8時8分。中午是12:00；00:05是凌晨12時5分，在午夜之後而非中午之後。

教師觀察及限制

分別記錄獨立完成／提示後／尚未／未觀察：(1)辨認HH:MM、指定日子的00:00及24:00；(2)報時、寫時間及連繫十二和二十四小時制。保留時間卡及口述解釋。接受清楚的同義說法；書寫HH:MM須有時、分各兩位。選擇題答對並不證明能口述解釋，須另查午夜及中午。
