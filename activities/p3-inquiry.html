Teacher · Facilitation and bounded checks

Embed these opportunities in3S2/3D1 lessons after prerequisites. 3F1 has one integrated inquiry statement, not six numbered objectives. The curriculum allocates ten hours across varied opportunities; this resource does not certify delivery of those hours. Use neutral prompts; do not reveal the answer list before exploration. A failed prediction can support strong inquiry when revised from evidence.

A: For positive integer lengths ordered a≤b≤c, closure requires a+b>c. For total12 the successful unordered triples are (2,5,5), (3,4,5), (4,4,4). Equality cases(2,4,6) and(1,5,6) fail; (1,4,7) is too short. For total15 they are (1,7,7), (2,6,7), (3,5,7), (3,6,6), (4,4,7), (4,5,6), (5,5,5). Enumeration is an optional teacher check, not a pupil combinatorics requirement. Observe actual joins and discuss practical tolerance; mathematical lists do not supply pupil observations.

B: Under the stated constraints, data2/4/6 fit scales1 and2 (steps2/4/6 and1/2/3); scale5 fails the grid-line condition. For10/20/30, scale5 fits (steps2/4/6); scales1/2 need more than six intervals. Do not assume one of the first two charts must win: accept a criterion-based recommendation, tie, error or revised conclusion. A partner reading is a recorded trial, not proof of causal improvement.

Keep predictions, records and corrections visible. Provide more paper, accessible materials or a scribe if needed; distinguish pupil decisions/manipulation from the helper’s contribution. Never fill observation boxes from these keys.

教師 · 引導及有界限的檢查

學生具備先備知識後，在3S2／3D1課堂安排探究。3F1是一項綜合探究陳述，不是六個編號目標。課程分配十小時作多元機會；本資源不證明已教足時數。用中性提示，不要先揭示答案列表。學生根據證據修訂錯誤預測，也可展現良好探究。

A：把正整數邊長排列為a≤b≤c，須a+b>c才能圍成三角形。總長12成功的不重複組合為(2,5,5), (3,4,5), (4,4,4)。(2,4,6)及(1,5,6)因相等而失敗；(1,4,7)較短兩邊不足。總長15成功組合為(1,7,7), (2,6,7), (3,5,7), (3,6,6), (4,4,7), (4,5,6), (5,5,5)。列盡組合是教師可選檢查，不是要求學生掌握組合數學。觀察實際端點相接並討論實物容差；數學答案列表不是學生觀察記錄。

B：依本題條件，2／4／6用每格1或2人均合適（棒長分別2／4／6格及1／2／3格）；每格5人不符合格線末端條件。10／20／30用每格5人合適（2／4／6格）；每格1或2人超過六格。不假定首兩幅圖必有勝負：接受按準則的建議、相同表現、錯誤及修訂結論。同伴讀圖是一次記錄，不是改善成因的證明。

保留預測、記錄及修正。需要時提供更多紙、無障礙材料或代筆；分清學生的決定／操作和協助者的貢獻。不可按解答預填觀察欄。
