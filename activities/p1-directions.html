P1 · Directions and positions

1S3 · Name ________ Date ________

You are the observer. Keep looking from your own place. Say your answers or point while an adult records your words.

1. Look from where you are.

Which object is on the left? Which is on the right? Use the words “left” and “right”. ________

Complete: B is between ___ and ___.

2. Look above and below the table.

Describe D and E using “over” and “under”: D is ___ the table. E is ___ the table.

3. Look at the box and ball.

From your view, G covers part of F. Complete: G is ___ F. F is ___ G. Use “in front of” and “behind”.

Use real objects

Materials from your teacher: a ball, three blocks, a box and a safe low table. Stay at the observer mark, facing the table. Your teacher stays beside you, not opposite you.

4. Describe what you see.

a. The teacher places one block on each side. Say which is on the left and which is on the right.

b. The teacher holds the ball over the table, then puts it under the table. Describe both positions in a sentence.

c. The teacher places a ball and box in a line away from you. Describe which is in front of the other, and which is behind.

d. The teacher places three blocks in a row. Say which block is between the other two, naming both neighbours.

5. Place, then describe.

Listen to one instruction at a time from your teacher. Move one object, then say where it is. Keep your viewpoint unchanged.

Use these words as appropriate: over · under · left · right · in front of · behind · between.

Do not climb, crawl under furniture or reach above your safe height. The teacher handles anything out of reach.

Teacher key, setup and rubric

Use labelled blocks A/B/C, a ball, box, clear low table and floor observer mark. Stand beside the pupil and share their viewing direction. Do not use the viewpoint of a facing doll/person or require left/right reversal. Reset each scene; no compound-position puzzle.

TaskExpected evidence
1–3A is on the left; C is on the right; B is between A and C (either neighbour order accepted). D is over the table; E is under it. G is in front of F; F is behind G. The overlap indicates depth, not simply higher/lower on the page. Accept equivalent positional wording; record the seven target terms separately.
4a: place A on the pupil’s left and C on their right. b: hold the ball visibly above the tabletop without resting it, then place below it. c: ball nearer pupil, box farther away on the same viewing line, both visible: ball in front of box, box behind ball. d: arrange A–B–C left to right: B between A and C. Record descriptions, not just pointing.
5Give these seven requests separately, resetting as needed: “Put A on the left”; “Put A on the right”; “Hold the ball over the table”; “Put the ball under the table”; “Put the ball in front of the box”; “Put the ball behind the box”; “Put B between A and C”. Ask “Where is it?” after each. Use a low tabletop/floor arrangement so behind remains visible and under is safe to reach; otherwise the pupil directs the adult. Mark placement and description separately. Do not coach with pointing before the first response.

Record for each word

I independent / P prompted / N not yet / NA not observed. For every term record: picture description, real-object description, placement response.

Over __/__ /__ · Under __/__ /__ · Left __/__ /__ · Right __/__ /__ · In front of __/__ /__ · Behind __/__ /__ · Between __/__ /__

Support / next step: __________________

For motor/access needs, let the pupil direct an adult or use tactile objects, keeping the same observer viewpoint. Record adapted assessment; a tactile substitute is not evidence of visual picture reading. No pass mark, classroom-use or complete-unit claim. Compass directions, turns and another observer’s viewpoint are not assessed.

小一 · 方向和位置

1S3 · 姓名 ________ 日期 ________

你是觀察者，保持從你的位置觀看。可口述或指示，由成人記錄你的說法。

1. 從你的位置看。

哪件物件在左邊？哪件在右邊？用「左邊」和「右邊」回答。 ________

完成：B在___和___之間。

2. 看看桌子的上方和下方。

用「上方」和「下方」描述D及E：D在桌子的___。E在桌子的___。

3. 看看盒子和球。

從你的視角看，G遮住F的一部分。完成：G在F的___。F在G的___。用「前面」和「後面」回答。

運用實物

教師提供球、三塊積木、盒子及安全的矮桌。留在觀察者標記位置，面向桌子。教師站在你旁邊，不站在對面。

4. 描述你所看見的。

a. 教師在兩旁各放一塊積木。說出哪塊在左邊，哪塊在右邊。

b. 教師把球拿在桌子上方，再放到桌子下方。用句子描述兩個位置。

c. 教師把球和盒子沿着離開你的方向前後排好。說出哪件在另一件的前面，哪件在後面。

d. 教師把三塊積木排成一行。說出哪塊在另外兩塊之間，並說出兩旁積木的標記。

5. 擺放，再描述。

逐一聆聽教師指令。每次移動一件物件，再說出它的位置。保持觀看位置不變。

按情況使用：上方、下方、左邊、右邊、前面、後面、之間。

不可攀爬、鑽進家具下方或伸手拿取超過安全高度的物件。碰不到的物件由教師處理。

教師解答、準備及準則

準備標示A／B／C的積木、球、盒子、清空的矮桌及地面觀察位置標記。站在學生旁邊，觀看方向一致。不要採用對面玩偶／人物的視角，不要求左右反轉。每題重新擺放，不出多重位置推理題。

題目預期證據
1–3A在左邊；C在右邊；B在A和C之間（兩旁物件次序可互換）。D在桌子上方；E在下方。G在F前面；F在G後面。遮擋表達前後深度，不只是紙面上下位置。接受意思相同的方位說法，七個目標詞分開記錄。
4a：A放學生左邊，C放右邊。b：把球拿在桌面上方而不接觸桌面，再放桌下。c：沿同一觀看方向，球較接近學生，盒子較遠，兩件都看得見：球在盒前，盒在球後。d：由左至右A–B–C：B在A和C之間。記錄描述，不只記錄指示。
5按需要重新擺放，逐一下達七項指令：「把A放左邊」、「把A放右邊」、「把球拿在桌子上方」、「把球放桌子下方」、「把球放盒子前面」、「把球放盒子後面」、「把B放A和C之間」。每次問「它在哪裏？」使用矮桌／地面安排，確保後面的物件仍可看見，下方可安全伸手，否則讓學生指示成人操作。擺放與描述分開記錄。首次回答前不以手勢暗示答案。

逐詞記錄

獨：獨立／助：協助／未：尚未展示／無：未觀察。每個詞記錄：圖像描述、實物描述、擺放反應。
