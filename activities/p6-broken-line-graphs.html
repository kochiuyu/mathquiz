P6 · Broken-line graphs

1 · Choose continuous data

Compare daily example temperatures with favourite-colour category counts. Explain why joining time-ordered temperatures is meaningful but joining arbitrary colours may mislead.

P6 · Broken-line graphs

2 · Construct a graph

Supplied temperatures for days 1–5 are 18, 22, 20, 24, 23 degrees Celsius. On a blank grid choose a scale, label time and temperature with units, plot every point and join successive points.

P6 · Broken-line graphs

3 · Read and explain

Use your graph to locate the largest rise and a fall. Explain what a connecting line represents and why it is not a physical travel route.

P6 · Broken-line graphs

4 · Compare two series

Add a second supplied series 16, 19, 19, 21, 20 for the same days. Use a distinct line style and legend. Compare values at the same time.

P6 · Broken-line graphs

5 · Change the scale honestly

Redraw the first series using a different labelled vertical scale. Keep values unchanged and explain how the apparent steepness changes.

P6 · Broken-line graphs

6 · Use observed data

Record a safe time-ordered dataset or cite a teacher-supplied source. Retain raw data, construct the graph and have a partner read every point back.

Teacher models and observation record

For the first example series the greatest rise is 4 degrees, tied from day 1 to 2 and day 3 to 4. Falls are 2 and 1 degrees. A valid graph must retain all five values and equal time intervals; scales may differ if accurately labelled.

P6 · 折線圖

1 · 探究任務

比較每日示例溫度與喜愛顏色的類別數量，解釋為何連接時間次序的溫度有意義，而隨意連接顏色可誤導。

P6 · 折線圖

2 · 探究任務

第1至5天示例溫度為18、22、20、24、23攝氏度，在空白方格選刻度，標時間、溫度及單位，標所有點並依次連接。

P6 · 折線圖

3 · 探究任務

按圖找最大升幅和一次下降，解釋連線意思及為何不是實際行走路線。
