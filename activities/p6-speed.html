Safe speed investigation

1 · Single-unit conversion

Convert 1.5 h to minutes,90 min to hours,2.5 min to seconds and 180 s to minutes. Explain larger/smaller unit reasoning. Do not mix hours, minutes and seconds in one complicated calculation.

2 · Intervals in a timetable

A walk begins 09:35 and ends 10:50. Find duration; then use the duration and end to recover the start. Create another same-day start/end/duration example.

Safe speed investigation

3 · Intuitive comparison

Safely watch a toy car and a slowly moving clock hand or other clearly different motions. Describe which seems faster and what distance/time information is missing. Do not race near roads, stairs or other hazards.

4 · Direct fair comparison

On a clear level tabletop or safe marked walking space, compare two model vehicles for the same time or the same distance. Keep starting conditions controlled. Record method, distances/times and repeated trials.

Safe speed investigation

5 · Improvised units

Use identical floor tiles and equally timed claps, or equal blocks and a consistent sand timer. Compare a model before/after a change. Discuss why different tile sizes or clap rhythms invalidate comparison.

6 · Metres per second and kilometres per hour

Explain what 3 m/s and 40 km/h mean using distance/time diagrams. Measure a safe toy journey in metres and seconds if equipment allows. No conversion between m/s and km/h is required.

Safe speed investigation

7 · Travel and rest graph

Plot time(s)/distance(m):(0,0),(2,20),(4,20),(6,50). Use equal axis intervals, connect in time order, tell the journey and identify rest. Compare speed of the two moving segments.

8 · Speed distance time problems

For uniform travel at 6 m/s over 42 m, find time and check using multiplication. For 24 km in 2 h find average speed. Explain units at every step.

Safe speed investigation

9 · Plan and report a safe journey

Plan a safe toy-car journey with a pause. Collect actual time/distance data, draw a travel graph, estimate one segment speed and explain limitations. Label measured data clearly and distinguish them from your predictions.

Teacher model and rubric

1. 90 min,1.5 h,150 s,3 min.

2. 75 min=1 h 15 min;10:50−1 h 15 min=09:35.

3. Record actual observations, not a preassigned result; explain the limits of impression alone.

4. Same-time farther is faster; same-distance shorter time is faster. No invented trial results.

Teacher model and rubric

5. The length and time unit must both be consistent; this motivates standard units.

6. 3 m in each second and 40 km in each hour for uniform motion; average speeds summarise total distance/time.

7. Rest 2–4 s;first moving speed 10 m/s, last 15 m/s. Flat distance does not mean return travel.

8. 7 s and 12 km/h;6×7=42 and 12×2=24.

Teacher model and rubric

9. Teacher checks all nine objectives across the records; direct/intuitive/improvised comparisons require observation, not a quiz score.

安全速率探究

1 · 單一單位換算

把1.5小時換分鐘、90分鐘換小時、2.5分鐘換秒、180秒換分鐘，說明大小單位關係，不作小時分鐘秒混合繁複計算。

2 · 時間表與時段

步行09:35開始、10:50結束，求時段，再用時段及結束時間求開始時間，另作同日例子。

安全速率探究

3 · 直觀比較

安全觀察玩具車與慢速鐘指針或其他明顯不同動作，描述誰似較快及欠甚麼距離時間資料，不在馬路、樓梯或危險位置競賽。

4 · 直接公平比較

在平坦桌面或安全標記步行區，以相同時間或距離比較兩模型車，控制起始條件，記錄方法、距離／時間及重複測試。

安全速率探究

5 · 自訂單位

用相同地磚及等時拍手，或相同積木及一致沙漏，比較模型改動前後；討論不同地磚大小或拍手節奏為何不能公平比較。

6 · 米每秒與公里每小時

用距離時間圖說明3m/s及40km/h；量具許可時測量安全玩具行程的米及秒，不要求m/s與km/h互換。

安全速率探究

7 · 行程與停留圖

繪時間（秒）／距離（米）：(0,0)、(2,20)、(4,20)、(6,50)，坐標等距、按時序連線，描述行程及停留，比較兩移動線段速率。

8 · 速率距離時間問題

均速6m/s走42m求時間並用乘法驗算；24km用2h求平均速率，逐步說明單位。

安全速率探究

9 · 設計並報告安全行程

設計安全玩具車行程及停留，收集實際時間距離，繪圖、估一段速率並說明限制；清楚標示實測數據，並與預測作區分。

教師模型及準則

1. 90分鐘、1.5小時、150秒、3分鐘。

2. 75分鐘＝1小時15分鐘；10:50−1小時15分鐘＝09:35。

3. 記錄真正觀察，不預定結果，說明只憑感覺的限制。

4. 同時間較遠較快；同距離較少時間較快。不編造結果。

教師模型及準則

5. 距離及時間單位均須一致，這說明標準單位的需要。

6. 均速時每秒3米、每小時40公里；平均速率以總距離除總時間。

7. 2至4秒停留；首段10米／秒、末段15米／秒；水平距離線不是返回。

8. 7秒及12公里／小時；6×7＝42、12×2＝24。

教師模型及準則

9. 教師按紀錄核對九目標；直觀、直接及自訂單位比較須觀察，不由小測分數代替。
