P5 · Circles

1 · Find the centre

Trace a round lid. Propose two possible centres and test each by measuring distances to at least four boundary points. Keep the measurements and explain which centre is supported.

2 · Radii and chords

Mark a centre O and four boundary points. Draw and compare radii, a diameter and a chord not through O. Compare several chords and explain why a diameter is a longest chord. Explain why not every chord is a diameter.

P5 · Circles

3 · Set the compass

Draw a circle of radius 3 cm and another of diameter 8 cm on separate paper. Record the opening before drawing, then measure two radii and one diameter. Do not assume screen or print lengths are true size.

4 · Alternative construction

Use a fixed point, loop of string and pencil, with adult help. Keep the centre-distance fixed. Compare this construction with a traced non-circular shape and explain the difference.

P5 · Circles

5 · Design and explain

Create a design with two circles of different radii and shared centre, and one with different centres. Label the radii and state which points belong to each boundary.

6 · Challenge a claim

A pupil says any line across a circle is a diameter. Make a counterexample and a corrected statement. Record what you changed after a partner checks your drawing.

Teacher models, not observations

Compass openings: radius 3 cm → 3 cm; diameter 8 cm → 4 cm. All radii in one circle are equal; a diameter is a chord through the centre. Drawings and measurements must be observed.

Observation record

Record date, original work, explanation, assistance and next step. Mark independent / prompted / not yet / not observed.

P5 · 圓

1 · 找圓心

描畫圓蓋，提出兩個可能圓心，量度各點至至少四個圓周點的距離，保留量度記錄並說明哪個圓心較合理。

2 · 半徑與弦

標示圓心O及四個圓周點，畫半徑、直徑及不經O的弦，比較多條弦，解釋直徑為何是最長的弦，並解釋為何弦不一定是直徑。

P5 · 圓

3 · 設定圓規

另紙畫半徑3厘米及直徑8厘米的圓，先記錄圓規張開距離，再量兩條半徑及一直徑。不可假定屏幕或列印圖是實際大小。

4 · 另一種畫法

成人協助下，用固定點、線圈及筆保持中心距離不變。比較與描畫非圓形的方法，解釋差異。

P5 · 圓

5 · 設計並解釋

設計兩個半徑不同的同心圓，再設計不同圓心的圓。標示半徑並說明各圓周上的點。

6 · 檢驗說法

有人說穿過圓的任何線段都是直徑，畫反例並改正說法，記錄同伴檢查後的修改。

教師示例，並非觀察

圓規張開：半徑3厘米→3厘米；直徑8厘米→4厘米。同圓半徑等長；直徑是經圓心的弦。圖畫及量度須實際觀察。

觀察記錄

記錄日期、原作、解釋、協助及下一步，註明獨立完成／提示後／尚未／未觀察。
