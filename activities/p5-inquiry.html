P5 · Inquiry and investigation

1 · Choose an investigation

Choose either a display-design question involving area and occupied volume, or a two-series data question involving rounding and number operations. Write your own question, purpose and constraints.

2 · Plan and estimate

List needed measurements or data, safe tools, units, source and assumptions. Make an estimate before calculating. Explain why area and volume answer different questions, or why the selected chart suits your question.

P5 · Inquiry and investigation

3 · Try two methods

For a display, compare two decompositions into known shapes and build a cuboid model. For data, compare vertical/horizontal compound charts and two sensible scales. Record original work, not a copied example.

4 · Test a claim

Choose a claim involving a fraction, decimal or simple expression in your investigation. Test it using exact arithmetic and a drawing, measurement or table. Keep a failed attempt and explain its cause.

P5 · Inquiry and investigation

5 · Revise and communicate

Ask a partner to challenge assumptions, labels and units. Revise your work and explain what changed. Distinguish observed evidence, supplied data and model assumptions.

6 · Conclude with limits

Answer your original question, justify your method and state one limitation or further question. Use the process rubric, not a fabricated mastery score or a claim of lesson hours delivered.

Teacher models, not observations

Optional display model: two 12 × 8 rectangles have area 192 square units, while a 12 × 8 × 3 cuboid occupies 288 cubic units; these are different measures. Optional chart model: X totals 110,000 and Y 160,000, a difference 50,000. These are examples, not pupil conclusions.

P5 · 探究活動

1 · 選擇探究

選擇涉及面積與佔據空間的展示設計題，或涉及兩組資料、四捨五入與數運算的問題，自訂題目、目的及限制。

2 · 計劃及估算

列所需量度或數據、安全工具、單位、來源及假設，先估算再計算。解釋面積與體積回答不同問題，或圖表為何適合題目。

P5 · 探究活動

3 · 嘗試兩個方法

展示題比較兩種已知圖形分割並製作長方體模型；數據題比較直式、橫式複合圖及兩種合理刻度，記錄原作而非抄示例。

4 · 檢驗主張

在探究中選涉及分數、小數或簡單代數式的主張，用準確運算及圖畫、量度或表格檢驗，保留一次失敗嘗試並解釋原因。

P5 · 探究活動

5 · 修訂及溝通

請同伴質疑假設、標示及單位，修訂後說明改變，分清觀察證據、提供數據及模型假設。

6 · 有局限的結論

回答原題、說明方法理由，列一限制或後續問題，使用過程評量，不虛構掌握分數或已完成課時。

教師示例，並非觀察

可選展示模型：兩個12×8長方形面積共192平方單位；12×8×3長方體佔288立方單位，兩種量不同。可選圖表模型：X共110000、Y共160000，相差50000。這些是示例，不是學生結論。

觀察記錄

記錄日期、原作、解釋、協助及下一步，註明獨立完成／提示後／尚未／未觀察。
