P1 · Explore Hong Kong coins

Teacher-led pupil activity · 1M2 · Name Date

Use the real coins your teacher provides. Say your answers; you do not need to write prices or calculation sentences. Keep coins on the tray, not near your mouth.

1. Look, touch and name.

Turn over each of the seven coins. Say its value. Point to a number or word that helps you identify it. Compare two coins: what do you notice about their colour, outline, size, thickness or weight?

Tell the teacher one feature you noticed:

2. Read the price tags aloud.

All prices are in Hong Kong dollars. Use dollars and cents, not “point”.

$2.50$3.00$0.80$10.00

Which tag shows ten dollars? Point to it.

3. Count the coins on each tray.

Move one coin at a time to avoid counting it twice. Tell the teacher the total on tray A, then B, then C.

A:   B:   C:

Now make exactly eight dollars using the $1, $2 and $5 coins. No change is given.

4. Exchange without changing the value.

a. Exchange one $1 coin for 20-cent coins only. Move the coins to show your exchange.

b. Exchange one 50-cent coin using both 20-cent and 10-cent coins.

c. Exchange one $10 coin using exactly one $5 coin and some $2 and/or $1 coins.

For each exchange, count the value before and after. Did the number of coins change? Did their total value change?

Teacher: mark observations on the separate key. The writing lines are for your notes; pupils may respond by speaking, pointing and moving coins.

Teacher preparation, key and rubric

Materials: real circulating Hong Kong coins: 10¢, 20¢, 50¢, $1, $2, $5 and $10; three trays; a cloth for clearing each task. Have at least five 20¢ coins, five $1 coins, three $2 coins, two 10¢ coins, one 50¢, one $5 and one $10 coin. Reuse coins between tasks. Supervise handling. Do not use a worksheet circle as evidence of recognising a real coin.

For task 1, present one of each denomination in a mixed order, with both sides available. Do not label their positions with the answers. For task 3, set up only the following groups, keeping spare coins out of sight: A: $1 + 50¢ + 20¢ + 10¢; B: $5 + $2 + $1 + $1; C: one $10 coin. The pupil does not need to write these expressions.

EvidenceExpected response / acceptance
1 · Seven denominations and physical featuresNames 10¢, 20¢, 50¢, $1, $2, $5, $10 correctly. Record each separately. Checks visible wording/numerals and describes an actual observed feature. Do not infer value from size alone or demand one design for every circulating coin.
2 · Four price tags$2.50: two dollars and fifty cents. $3.00: three dollars. $0.80: eighty cents (or zero dollars and eighty cents). $10.00: ten dollars. Accept equivalent correct currency wording; “two point five” alone does not show the requested dollars-and-cents reading. No decimal-place explanation or writing of prices is required.
3 · Count and make an amountA: one dollar and eighty cents. B: nine dollars. C: ten dollars. Eight-dollar example: $5, $2, $1. Accept any available $1/$2/$5 combination totalling $8. Observe that each coin is counted once. Do not require a written expression.
4 · Equal-value exchangesa: five 20¢ coins. b: two 20¢ and one 10¢ coin, or one 20¢ and three 10¢ coins if extras are supplied. c: $5 + $2 + $2 + $1, or $5 + $2 + $1 + $1 + $1, or $5 + five $1 coins. Accept only combinations using the stated denominations and exactly one $5 coin. The coin count changes; the total value stays the same.

Scope guard: exchange tasks stay within {10¢,20¢,50¢,$1} or {$1,$2,$5,$10}. Do not add exchanges across those groups, amounts over $10, decimal calculations or giving-change questions. Mixed dollars/cents may be counted without asking for such an exchange.

Observation record

Use I = independent, S = supported, N = not yet shown. Record a mathematical response, not only participation.

Coin values: 10¢ __ 20¢ __ 50¢ __ $1 __ $2 __ $5 __ $10 __

Physical-feature observation __ · Price tags: $2.50 __ $3.00 __ $0.80 __ $10.00 __

Counts: A __ B __ C __ · Makes $8 __ · Exchanges: a __ b __ c __

Prompts/support and next step:

No prescribed total score or pass threshold. This activity supplies assessment material, not evidence that a class has used or mastered it.

小一 · 探索香港硬幣

教師帶領的學生活動 · 1M2 · 姓名 日期

使用老師提供的真實硬幣，口頭回答，不用寫價錢或計算算式。硬幣放在托盤上，不要放近嘴巴。

1. 觀察、觸摸及說出幣值。

把七種硬幣逐一翻轉，說出每枚的幣值，指出能幫助你辨認的數字或文字。比較兩枚硬幣：它們的顏色、外形、大小、厚度或重量有甚麼不同？

告訴老師你發現的一個特徵：

2. 讀出價目牌。

所有價錢均為港元。用元和角／分讀出，不用「點」。

$2.50$3.00$0.80$10.00

哪個價目牌表示十元？請指出來。

3. 數出每盤硬幣的總值。

每次移動一枚硬幣，避免重複數算。依次告訴老師A盤、B盤和C盤的總值。

A：   B：   C：

現在用一元、二元及五元硬幣湊出剛好八元，不用找續。

4. 換幣，總值不變。

a. 用一元硬幣換成只有二毫的硬幣。移動硬幣，展示你的換法。

b. 用一枚五毫硬幣換成二毫及一毫硬幣，兩種都要用。

c. 用一枚十元硬幣換成剛好一枚五元硬幣，以及一些二元或一元硬幣（可混合）。

每次換幣前後都數一次。硬幣的數目有改變嗎？總值有改變嗎？

教師：用另頁解答記錄觀察。橫線供教師記錄；學生可以口述、指示及移動硬幣作答。

教師準備、解答及評估準則

材料：真實的香港流通硬幣：一毫、二毫、五毫、一元、二元、五元及十元；三個托盤；一塊布，方便每題後清理。準備最少五枚二毫、五枚一元、三枚二元、兩枚一毫，以及五毫、五元、十元各一枚。各題可重用硬幣。請監督學生安全使用。辨認工作紙上的圓圈，不等於辨認真實硬幣。

第1題：把每種幣值各一枚混合排列，讓學生看到兩面，不要在位置上標示答案。第3題只擺放以下硬幣，其餘收起：A盤：一元、五毫、二毫、一毫各一枚；B盤：五元一枚、二元一枚、一元兩枚；C盤：十元一枚。學生不用寫算式。

評估項目預期答案及接受準則
1 · 七種幣值及實物特徵正確說出一毫、二毫、五毫、一元、二元、五元及十元，逐項記錄。查閱硬幣上的文字／數字，說出實際觀察到的特徵。不應只憑大小判斷價值，也不要假設每種流通硬幣只有一款圖案。
2 · 四個價目牌$2.50：二元五角／兩元五毫／二元五十分；$3.00：三元；$0.80：八角／八毫／八十分；$10.00：十元。接受意思正確的元、角、分或日常說法；只說「二點五」未能顯示要求的元及角／分讀法。不要求解釋小數位值或書寫價錢。
3 · 數算及湊出金額A：一元八角／一元八毫；B：九元；C：十元。八元例子：五元、二元、一元各一枚。接受用現有一元／二元／五元硬幣湊成八元的其他組合。觀察每枚硬幣只數一次，不要求寫計算算式。
4 · 等值換幣a：五枚二毫。b：兩枚二毫加一枚一毫；如提供更多一毫，也接受一枚二毫加三枚一毫。c：五元一枚、二元兩枚、一元一枚；或五元一枚、二元一枚、一元三枚；或五元一枚、一元五枚。只接受指定幣值，而且恰好有一枚五元的組合。硬幣數目改變，總值不變。

範圍限制：換幣只限於「一毫、二毫、五毫、一元」或「一元、二元、五元、十元」各組之內。不加跨組換幣、超過十元、小數計算或找續題。可數算含元及角／分的硬幣總值，而不要求跨組換幣。

觀察記錄

用「獨」表示獨立完成，「助」表示協助下完成，「未」表示尚未展示。記錄數學表現，不只記錄參與。
