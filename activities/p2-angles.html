P2 · How open is an angle?

Name ______ Date ______ · Use paper, pencil, ruler, set square, tracing paper and two safe strips. No protractor or degree labels needed.

1 · Make and identify an angle

Join two strips at one end and open them to make an angle. Point to their common point and the two sides. Keep the meeting point fixed while changing the opening; explain what changes. Find an angle on a book or frame. If you only extend a strip without changing the opening, does the angle grow? Show why.

2 · Compare by overlay

Trace A, B and C. Match their meeting points and one side, rotating the paper when needed. Which are equal? Which is larger? Explain using the opening, not side length.

3 · Find right angles

Use a set square’s right-angle corner to check book/paper corners and the activity angles, then D, E and F. Align the meeting points and one side, then inspect the other side. Identify the right angle, the acute angle (smaller than a right angle), and the obtuse angle (larger than one right angle but smaller than two). Rotate one angle and check again.

4 · Make perpendicular lines

Make a straight fold. Fold again so one part of that first crease lies exactly on its other part. Open the paper and use a set square to check whether the two creases meet at right angles. Explain why they are perpendicular. Find and check a pair of perpendicular lines around you; do not rely only on appearance.

Draw perpendiculars and investigate distance

5 · Draw in both situations

With ruler and set square, draw a line through P perpendicular to the given line in each diagram. First P lies on the line; second P lies off it. Show how you align and slide the tool, then check the right angle.

6 · Distance from a point to a line

In the second diagram, join P to two other points on the line, one on each side of the perpendicular meeting point. Compare the three lengths using paper strips or a ruler. Which is shortest? Try points farther away. Use a set square to explain the shortest connection’s relation to the line, and show which length measures the distance from P to the line.

Draw, make and explain angles

7 · Angles of different sizes

First make an acute, right and obtuse angle using strips or folds. Then draw all three on paper and check/classify them with a set square. Draw an additional example whose sides are shorter but whose angle is larger; ask a partner to check by overlay. Keep both made models and drawings as evidence. No degrees needed.

8 · Explain your methods

Explain to a partner: how can two angles be compared? Why do longer sides not necessarily mean a larger angle? How do you check perpendicular lines? Why is the distance from a point to a line not measured along a slanted connection? Use models, drawings and measurement records.

Teacher · Methods and key

Use several sessions with safe strips, paper, tracing paper, rulers and set squares. Elicit actions before giving answers. In task 1 pointing to the common point and two sides is sufficient; do not create a separate compulsory formal “vertex” vocabulary test.

2: A = B; C is larger. Arm lengths and orientations deliberately differ. Overlay meeting points and one side without stretching the drawing. 3: D right, E acute, F obtuse. Observe correct tool corner/alignment, not just category answers. Rotation does not change size. 4: folding the first crease onto itself produces perpendicular creases; open and verify.

5: in both diagrams the perpendicular must pass through P and meet the horizontal line at right angles. Demonstrate a ruler as a fixed guide, sliding the set square until its relevant side passes through P. Check both conditions, not just whether the result looks vertical. 6: the perpendicular connection is the shortest of the three; test further points on either side. Its length is the distance from point to line. Finite comparisons are exploratory evidence; explain the general relationship without requiring a formal proof. Responsive/printed diagrams can scale, so no fixed centimetre answer is imposed.

7: accept correct examples in different orientations and lengths; require both made models and drawings, not only labels. 8: angle size depends on opening; perpendicular lines meet at right angles; the perpendicular gives the shortest point-to-line distance.

Teacher · Rubric and boundaries

Record independent / prompted / not yet / not observed with models, drawings and pupil words. Record assistance; verbal answers do not replace drawing/folding evidence.

1

Models the opening and identifies sides/meeting point.

2

Overlays correctly; explains independence from length/orientation.

3

Uses tools for right angles/perpendiculars and acute/obtuse classification.

4

Makes perpendicular creases and draws both on-line/off-line cases.

5

Compares point-to-line connections and identifies shortest perpendicular distance.

6

Both makes and draws different angles, showing a checking method.

No degrees/protractor, straight/full/reflex-angle categories, numerical angle measurement, formal proof or slanted-length calculation: later angle content belongs to 6M1. Visual diagrams need tactile/equivalent support when necessary; no independently accessible visual assessment is claimed. Local draft, pending independent review and mobile/print verification.

小二 · 角的張開程度

姓名 ______ 日期 ______ · 用紙、筆、尺、三角尺、描圖紙和兩條安全紙條。不要用量角器，不需度數。

1 · 製作及指出角

把兩條紙條的一端相接，打開成角。指出共同的點和兩邊；保持接點不動，改變張開程度，說角怎樣改變。觀察書角或相架上的角。只延長紙條但不改張開程度，角會變大嗎？用實物解釋。

2 · 疊合比較

用描圖紙描 A、B、C；把接點及其中一邊對齊，必要時轉動紙張。哪些角一樣大？哪個較大？用張開程度解釋，不要用邊長判斷。

3 · 找直角

用三角尺的直角檢查書角、紙角及上方活動的角，再檢查 D、E、F。接點對齊，一邊貼齊，再看另一邊。指出哪個是直角；哪個比直角小（銳角）；哪個比一個直角大但比兩個直角小（鈍角）。把一個角轉向再檢查。

4 · 製作垂直線

先摺一條直摺痕，再把這條摺痕的一部分與另一部分重合來摺第二次。打開紙，用三角尺檢查兩條摺痕相交是否成直角。說明這兩條線為何互相垂直。再找身邊一對垂直線並檢查，不要只靠目測。

畫垂直線及最短距離

5 · 兩種位置都要畫

用尺和三角尺，分別過 P 畫一條與已知直線垂直的直線。第一圖 P 在直線上；第二圖 P 不在直線上。展示三角尺怎樣對齊及滑動，完成後用直角覆核。

6 · 從點到直線的距離

在第二圖把 P 連到直線上另外兩個點，分別在垂足兩旁。用紙帶或尺比較這三條連線的長度。哪一條最短？再選更遠的點試試。用三角尺指出最短連線與直線的關係，說明點到直線的距離應沿哪條連線量度。

畫角、做角及解釋

7 · 不同大小的角

先用兩條紙條或摺紙製作一個銳角、一個直角、一個鈍角，再把三個角畫在紙上，用三角尺檢查並分類。另畫一個邊較短但角較大的例子，讓同伴疊合檢查。保留實物與畫圖兩種證據。不需寫度數。

8 · 說明你的方法

向同伴說明：怎樣比較兩角？為何長邊不一定代表大角？怎樣驗證垂直線？為何點到直線的距離不用斜的連線？用實物、畫圖和量度記錄作證。

教師 · 方法及解答

各項可分多節完成。準備安全紙條、紙、描圖紙、尺和三角尺。先讓學生操作，不透露下列答案。活動 1 指共同點和兩邊即可，不把正式「頂點」術語另設為必考項。

2：A＝B，C 較大；圖的邊長和方向刻意不同。疊合時頂點和一邊對齊，不可拉伸圖。3：D 直角、E 銳角、F 鈍角。三角尺的正確角和對齊動作是證據；轉向不改角大小。4：第二次摺時把第一摺痕自我重合，得到互相垂直的摺痕，須打開驗證。

5：兩圖均須畫過 P、與橫線垂直的直線。示範尺固定作導軌，沿尺平移三角尺直至相關邊通過 P；檢查直角及是否過 P，不只看像不像直線。6：第二圖垂線是三條連線中最短；可選左右更多點比較。這是從點到直線的距離。有限次比較是探索證據，教師說明一般關係，不要求學生作形式證明。圖像可縮放，所以不預設厘米答案。

7：接受不同方向及邊長的正確例子；須同時有製作和畫圖，不能只交分類答案。8：角大小取決於張開程度；垂直線相交成直角；垂線給出點到線的最短距離。

教師 · 觀察準則及範圍

逐項記獨立／提示後／尚未／未觀察，附實物、畫圖和學生語句。協助操作須記錄；口頭答案不代替畫線／摺紙證據。

1

認識角的張開程度，能操作及指出兩邊和接點。

2

正確疊合比較，解釋與邊長和方向無關。

3

用工具辨認直角／垂直線，分類銳角和鈍角。

4

摺出垂直線；在點在線上及線外兩種情況畫垂線。

5

比較點到線的連線並指出最短垂線及距離意義。

6

實際製作和畫出不同大小的角，附檢查方法。
