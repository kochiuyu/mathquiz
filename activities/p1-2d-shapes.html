P1 · Explore and make flat shapes

1S2 · Name __________ Date __________

Work with your teacher in short sessions. Speak, point, draw and make models. Use extra paper for the drawing tasks.

1. Points and lines

Point to the locations marked A and B. Which mark is a dot? Which is a cross? Do these marks represent a location or a little area? Which of C and D is straight, and which is curved?

On spare paper, mark two points. Draw a straight path between them with a ruler. Trace it with a thicker pencil. Has its position changed? Now draw two different curved paths between those same points. How many different straight paths can join the two fixed points?

2. Draw and make lines

Draw a straight line by tracing a book edge. Draw a curve by tracing part of a cup rim. On your mat, make a straight line with string, then make a curve. Show your teacher each result.

3. Name the shapes

Name A–F. For each shape with straight sides, count the sides. What is different about E? Compare A and F: did turning the shape change its name?

4. Draw and make all five types

On separate paper, draw one triangle, one quadrilateral, one pentagon, one hexagon and one circle. You may use a ruler, templates or tracing objects. Check each closed outline.

Now make each of those five types on a mat, using sticks or string. Use string for the circle. Show the teacher each model before reusing the materials. Count the straight sides, and check that they meet end to end. Turn one model and name it again.

5. Build with shapes

Use the two matching triangle pieces your teacher provides. Fit them edge to edge, without overlap or gaps, to make one quadrilateral. Trace the outside and show the join.

Then create a flat picture or pattern using at least one triangle, quadrilateral, pentagon, hexagon and circle from your shape pieces. Name the pieces and show how you placed them. Turn the whole arrangement: which piece names stay the same?

Teacher preparation and rubric

Provide paper, pencils of different thickness, ruler, book, cup, safe short strings, blunt sticks and pre-cut shape pieces. For task 5, cut a square along one diagonal to supply two matching triangles; also supply at least one piece of each of the five types. Adults prepare pieces; scissors are not required for pupils. Use raised outlines or tactile pieces when needed, recording the adaptation. Keep this key separate until responses are recorded.

Expected evidence

  1. A dot and B cross both mark positions. A mathematical point has no size; ink marks do. C is straight; D curved. Between two fixed points there is one straight path, but different curved paths. Retracing more thickly changes the mark, not the mathematical line; a mathematical line has no breadth. Accept age-appropriate explanations, not memorised formal wording. Observe the pupil’s actual paths.
  2. Record drawing and making separately: a book-edge trace is straight, cup-rim trace curved; taut string straight, bent string curved. Do not accept naming pictures alone as making evidence.
  3. A and F: triangles, three sides; B: quadrilateral, four; C: pentagon, five; D: hexagon, six; E: circle, curved outline rather than straight sides. Rotation preserves A/F type. Naming B as a more specific familiar type is insufficient unless the pupil also recognises the four-sided group.
  4. For each of the five types, record both a drawing and a made model. Polygon outlines must close with the appropriate number of straight sides and no crossings; a circle has a recognisably round closed curve. Accept ordinary hand-drawing imprecision, tracing and different orientations. Do not demand equal sides, regular polygons or angle measurements. A tracing alone does not replace the separate construction.
  5. The prepared two triangles can form the original square: its outside has four straight sides; the diagonal is an internal join, not an extra outside side. Accept other valid quadrilateral arrangements. For free composition, check all five piece types are present and identified; no unique artistic answer or beauty score. Record the arrangement or photograph it with appropriate consent. Rotation does not rename the pieces.

小一 · 探索和製作平面圖形

1S2 · 姓名 __________ 日期 __________

與教師分段完成，口述、指示、畫圖及製作模型。畫圖題可另用白紙。

1. 點和線

指出A和B標示的位置。哪個用圓點標示？哪個用交叉標示？這些記號代表一個位置，還是一小塊面積？C和D哪條是直線，哪條是曲線？

在白紙上標出兩點，用直尺畫一條連接它們的直線，再用較粗的筆沿線描畫。線的位置改變了嗎？現在用兩條不同曲線連接相同的兩點。通過這兩個固定的點，可以有多少條不同的直線？

2. 畫線和製作線

沿書本邊緣畫直線，沿杯口的一部分畫曲線。在墊上先用繩子擺出直線，再擺出曲線。逐一展示給教師看。

3. 說出圖形名稱

說出A至F的名稱。逐一數出有直邊圖形的邊數。E有甚麼不同？比較A和F：轉動圖形會改變名稱嗎？

4. 畫出並製作五類圖形

另用白紙，分別畫一個三角形、四邊形、五邊形、六邊形及圓形。可用直尺、模板或沿物件描畫。檢查各圖形是否封閉。

現在在墊上，用小棒或繩子逐一製作這五類圖形，圓形用繩子製作。每個模型先給教師看，再重用材料。數直邊，檢查是否首尾相接。轉動其中一個模型，再說出名稱。

5. 拼砌圖形

用教師提供的兩塊相同三角形，沿邊拼成一個四邊形，不留空隙、不重疊。描出外框，指出拼接的位置。

再用圖形片自由創作平面圖畫或圖案，最少各用一塊三角形、四邊形、五邊形、六邊形及圓形。說出所用圖形名稱及擺放方法。轉動整個作品：哪些圖形的名稱不變？

教師準備及評估準則

準備白紙、粗幼不同的筆、直尺、書本、杯、安全短繩、鈍頭小棒及預先剪好的圖形片。第5題把一個正方形沿一條對角線剪成兩塊相同三角形，另準備五類圖形各最少一塊。由成人預備，學生不用剪刀。有需要時用凸線或可觸摸圖形，並記錄調整。記錄回答前把解答與學生材料分開。

預期證據

  1. A用圓點、B用交叉標位置；數學上的點沒有大小，紙上的記號則有。C直、D曲。通過兩個固定點只有一條直線，卻可有不同曲線。描粗只改變記號，不改變數學上的線；數學上的線沒有闊度。接受適齡表達，不要求背誦正式字句。觀察學生實際畫出的線。
  2. 分開記錄畫線及製作：書邊描線直，杯口描線曲；拉直的繩直，彎曲的繩曲。只辨認圖片不能當作製作證據。
  3. A及F為三角形，有三邊；B四邊形，有四邊；C五邊形，有五邊；D六邊形，有六邊；E圓形，外框是曲線，不是直邊。A轉成F，類別不變。B即使被叫出更細的熟悉名稱，仍須顯示知道它屬四邊形。
  4. 五類各記錄一幅畫和一個製作模型。多邊形須由相應數目的直邊首尾相接、封閉且不交叉；圓形須為可辨認的圓滑封閉曲線。接受一般手繪誤差、描畫及不同方向。不要求等邊、正多邊形或量角。只描畫不能代替另一項製作。
  5. 兩塊預備三角形可拼回原正方形，外框有四條直邊；對角拼縫在內部，不是多出的一條外邊。接受其他有效四邊形拼法。自由創作須用到並辨認五類圖形，沒有唯一美術答案或美感分數。記錄作品，或在適當同意下拍照。轉動不改變圖形片名稱。
