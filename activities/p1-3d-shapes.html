P1 · Explore 3-D shapes

1S1 · Name ________ Date ________

1. Handle and group the models.

Your teacher gives you ten models. Look all around each one, feel it and turn it. Put together models belonging to the same broad shape family. Name each group. Do not group only by colour or size.

Use: sphere · cone · prism · pyramid · cylinder.

Pick two models from one group. What helped you put them together? Turn one of them. Does it still belong?

2. Try rolling and stacking safely.

On a tray, gently try rolling each model. Try another position. Which roll? Which move differently after you turn them? Tell your teacher what actually happens.

Try placing one model on another to make a low, steady stack. Keep it below your hand height. Which placements stay steady? Which do not? Let the teacher help with pointed models.

Show one observation by pointing, speaking or demonstrating. A model can behave differently in a different position.

Keep models away from your mouth. Do not throw or build tall towers. You are exploring ready-made models, not constructing new solids.

4. Trace the outside of one picture.

Choose a drawing. With a finger or pencil, follow only its outside boundary. Show the corresponding real model. What can you see or feel on the model that the flat picture cannot show completely?

Tell or show your teacher. You do not need to draw or make a new solid.

Teacher preparation, key and rubric

Prepare two safe, rigid models of each of the five families (ten total). Vary size/colour. For prisms include two different polygonal-base examples; for pyramids likewise vary the base outline. Use right, not oblique, solids. Names of the different prism/pyramid subtypes are not tested. Use smooth full cylinders, cones and spheres, not mugs with handles, truncated cones or irregular balls. Provide a shallow tray.

TaskObservable evidence and key
1Five correct groups, each containing its two corresponding models, named prism, cylinder, pyramid, cone or sphere. Accept natural descriptions as emerging recognition, but record naming separately. Both different prism examples belong together; likewise the pyramids. Turning a model preserves its family. No formal definitions or face/edge/vertex counts required.
2Record what the pupil actually tries and observes. A sphere rolls in many directions; a cylinder may roll on its curved side but remain steady on a flat end; a cone may roll along a curved path. Prisms and pyramids do not smoothly roll like a sphere, though they can tumble. Stacking depends on orientation and contact, so do not mark “can stack” as an absolute property of a whole family. Shape recognition, not a universal roll/stack rule, is the criterion.
3A prism; B cylinder; C pyramid; D cone; E sphere. Match each with a real model in its family. The drawings show representative models, not every possible shape within a family. Read only neutral card labels aloud; do not reveal the shape name.
4Trace the external silhouette, not internal dashed lines; point to the matching solid. Accept observations such as feeling the back/underside or turning the solid to see another view. A circle-like outline alone does not establish that a drawing represents a sphere: use the full picture and the corresponding model.

Individual observation record

I independent · P prompted · N not yet shown · NA not observed. Record separate results for model recognition / naming / picture matching.

小一 · 探索立體圖形

1S1 · 姓名 ________ 日期 ________

1. 拿起模型，分辨類別。

教師給你十個模型。從不同方向觀察，觸摸並轉動每個模型。把屬於同一類立體的模型放在一起，說出各組的名稱。不要只按顏色或大小分類。

可用名稱：球體、圓錐體、角柱體、角錐體、圓柱體。

在一組內選兩個模型。甚麼特徵幫助你把它們放在一起？轉動其中一個，它仍屬於這組嗎？

2. 安全地嘗試滾動和堆疊。

在托盤上輕輕嘗試滾動每個模型，再換個擺放方向。哪些可以滾動？轉動方向後，哪些移動方式不同？把實際觀察告訴教師。

嘗試把一個模型放在另一個上面，堆成低矮而穩定的一疊，高度不超過你的手。哪些擺放方式穩定？哪些不穩定？尖頂模型請教師協助。

用指示、說話或示範展示一個觀察。模型換個方向，表現可能不同。

模型不要放近口部，不可投擲或堆高塔。這是探索現成模型，不是製作新的立體。

把圖畫與實物模型配對

4. 描出一幅圖畫的外框。

選一幅圖畫，用手指或鉛筆沿最外面的邊界描畫。指出相應的實物。實物有甚麼可以看到或摸到，但平面圖畫不能完全顯示？

向教師說明或示範。不用畫出或製作新的立體。

教師準備、解答及觀察準則

準備五類立體各兩個安全、堅固的模型，共十個，大小和顏色應有變化。角柱體使用兩種不同多邊形底部的例子，角錐體也用不同底部形狀。使用直立而非斜立的模型。不考核角柱體／角錐體的細分類名稱。圓柱、圓錐和球應完整光滑，不用帶柄杯、截去尖頂的圓錐或不規則球。另備淺托盤。

題目可觀察證據及解答
1分成五組，每組放入兩個相應模型，說出角柱體、圓柱體、角錐體、圓錐體、球體。自然語言描述可反映初步辨認，但名稱須分開記錄。兩個不同角柱體應同組，兩個不同角錐體也同組。轉動不改變類別。不要求正式定義或數面、邊、頂點。
2記錄學生實際嘗試及觀察。球可向不同方向滾動；圓柱側放可滾動，平端着地可穩定擺放；圓錐可能沿彎曲路徑滾動。角柱體和角錐體不像球般順暢滾動，但可以翻倒。堆疊取決於方向和接觸位置，不能把「可堆疊」當作整類立體的絕對性質。準則是辨認立體，而非背誦通用滾動／堆疊規則。
3A角柱體；B圓柱體；C角錐體；D圓錐體；E球體。每幅與相同類別的實物配對。圖畫是代表例子，並非該類別所有可能的形狀。口述時只讀題卡字母，不透露立體名稱。
4沿外輪廓描畫，不沿內部虛線；指出相應立體。接受例如能摸到背面／底部，或轉動實物看到另一面的觀察。單憑圓形外框不能確定是球，要看整幅圖並聯繫相應模型。

個別觀察記錄

獨：獨立完成；助：協助下完成；未：尚未展示；無：未觀察。實物辨認／說出名稱／圖像配對分開記錄。
