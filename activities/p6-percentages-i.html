Percentages I: name the whole

1 · The reference whole

Arrange 100 counters and mark 20. Name the whole and explain 20% using quantities of the same kind. Explain why 20 counters compared with 100 cm is not this percentage relation.

2 · More than the reference

Compare a reference collection of 40 cards with another of 50 cards. Model both and explain how the second can be 125% of the first, without claiming 125% is a subset of one whole.

Percentages I: name the whole

3 · Decimal links

Convert 0.99,1.22 and 0.005 to percentages. Reverse each conversion and explain the same reference whole.

4 · Fraction links

Convert 2.5% and(4/5)% to lowest-term fractions. Explain the extra division by 100. Convert 3/4 back to a percentage.

Percentages I: name the whole

5 · Spot the whole error

A learner says 50% always means 50 objects. Give two different same-kind reference collections that disprove the claim and explain both.

6 · Make a display

Create a three-representation display for a real classroom comparison: fraction, decimal and percent. Record the actual counts, reference whole, interpretation and a partner check.

百分數（一）：指出整體

1 · 參考整體

排100粒棋子，標20粒，指出整體，用同類量解釋20%，說明20粒與100厘米不能構成此百分數關係。

2 · 多於參考

比較參考40張卡及另一批50張，建模解釋第二批可為第一批125%，不能說125%是原整體的一部分子集。

百分數（一）：指出整體

3 · 小數連繫

把0.99、1.22、0.005化百分數，再逆向轉換，解釋整體不變。

4 · 分數連繫

把2.5%及(4/5)%化最簡分數，解釋須再除100，並把3/4化百分數。

百分數（一）：指出整體

5 · 找整體錯誤

有學生說50%永遠是50件，用兩個不同同類參考整體反駁，逐一解釋。

6 · 製作展示

就真實課室比較製作分數、小數及百分數展示，記錄實際數量、整體、解釋及同伴核對。
