P4 · Area (I)

Name ______ Date ______. Prepare paper, equal tiles, a ruler and suitable tape measure. Carry out the physical work and keep original records. Hide the teacher key.

1 · Surface, not boundary

Point to the surface covered by a textbook cover and a desktop. Explain the difference between the covered region and its boundary. Compare two clearly different physical surfaces by sight first; record your judgement and why it seems obvious. No ruler calculation is needed for this first comparison.

2 · Compare by actual overlay

Cut two paper shapes. Move or turn them and place one over the other WITHOUT stretching or cutting away parts. Record whether one is completely covered, they coincide, or the overlap is only partial. Explain what this placement proves. A partial overlap alone does not establish which entire area is larger. Keep your original cutouts or tracing.

4 · Compare through a third shape

Choose three physical paper shapes A, B and C. Use overlay or the same improvised tiles to compare A with B, and B with C. Record the evidence and infer only a relation those results support. Separately consider this GIVEN statement: A is smaller than B and A is smaller than C. What can you say about A? Can you determine the order of B and C from just this information?

Teacher key and physical checks

1. Area describes how much surface is covered, not distance around its boundary. Check actual pointing and a genuinely obvious visual comparison; no universal numeric answer is supplied.

2. Complete containment with an uncovered remainder supports a strictly smaller area for the contained piece. Exact coincidence supports equality. A partial overlap does not establish either size relation by itself; another placement or method may still compare the areas. Observe actual rigid movement and retain evidence.

3. Tiles must be congruent, cover the entire region and have no gaps or overlapping interiors. Compare counts only with the same unit. Accept physically sensible original counts, not a prefilled total. A larger unit gives fewer tiles for the same region when both chosen units cover exactly; no numerical conversion is required.

4. Actual conclusions depend on the recorded comparisons. For example A=B and BB and B>C implies A>C. For the separate given condition A

5. A square of side 1 cm has area 1 cm²; a square of side 1 m has area 1 m². cm/m label sides; cm²/m² label area. Verify physical references with tools. Paper measurement results depend on actual chosen objects and checked grids. A floor is more conveniently measured in m², but this does not mean cm² is mathematically impossible. Do not request numerical cm²–m² conversion.

6. No universal numerical key. Verify that the fresh-region estimate preceded measurement. Check equal units, complete coverage, unit labels and method. Accept reasoned estimates and honest discrepancies; never fill an estimate after seeing the measured result.

7. Counting r rows of c equal square units yields r×c square units. With corresponding side lengths, rectangle area=l×w; for equal square sides it is s×s. Inspect actual arrays. Transfer: 24÷6=4 cm, a LENGTH, since 6 cm × 4 cm gives 24 cm². Do not label the missing side cm².

8. L: 6×2+2×3=18 cm². Joined: 4×3+2×2=16 cm²; the common boundary has zero area, so do not subtract twice its length as in a perimeter calculation. U: 6×5−2×3=24 cm²; the removed notch opens to the exterior. Check by counting equal unit cells. If pieces overlap in area, count shared cells only once; do not blindly sum them. The labelled examples are mathematical schematics, not physical measurement evidence.

1 · 表面而非邊界

指出課本封面及桌面所覆蓋的表面，解釋覆蓋範圍與邊界的分別。先憑觀察比較兩個大小明顯不同的實際表面，記錄判斷及明顯的理由。這次比較不必用尺計算。

2 · 實際重疊比較

剪出兩個紙形，移動或轉動後把一個放在另一個上面，不拉伸也不剪去部分。記錄是否完全覆蓋、完全重合，或只有部分重疊，解釋這個擺法證明甚麼。部分重疊本身不能證明哪個完整面積較大。保留原紙形或描圖。

教師解答及實物核查

1. 面積表示覆蓋表面的大小，不是沿邊界的長度。檢查實際指出表面及確實明顯的目測比較，不提供統一數值答案。

2. 完全包含且仍有未覆蓋部分，可支持被包含紙形面積較小；完全重合支持相等。只有部分重疊不足以決定大小，但可再換擺法或方法比較。觀察實際不變形移動，保留證據。

3. 小片須全等，完整覆蓋且無空隙、內部不重疊。只有相同單位的片數才可直接比較。接受有實物依據的原有計數，不預填總數。兩種單位均能剛好覆蓋時，較大單位所需片數較少；不要求數值換算。

4. 實際結論依據所記比較。例如A=B且BB且B>C可推A>C。另給A

5. 邊長1厘米的正方形面積1平方厘米；邊長1米的正方形面積1平方米。厘米／米表示邊長，平方厘米／平方米表示面積。用工具核實實際參考正方形。紙形結果依實物及已核對方格而定。地板用平方米較方便，不表示用平方厘米在數學上不可能。不要求兩面積單位的數值換算。

6. 沒有統一數值答案。核實新區域先估後量，檢查相同單位、完整覆蓋、標記及方法。接受有理由估計及真實差異，不可看實測後補填估計。

7. r行、每行c個相同平方單位，合共r×c平方單位。對應邊長得長方形面積l×w，正方形s×s。檢查實際排列。遷移題24÷6=4厘米，是長度，因6厘米×4厘米得24平方厘米。不可把所求邊長標成平方厘米。

8. L：6×2+2×3=18平方厘米。Joined：4×3+2×2=16平方厘米；共同邊面積為零，不像求周界般減兩次邊長。U：6×5−2×3=24平方厘米，移除凹口與外部相通。可用相同單位格計數核查。若有面積重疊，共用格只算一次，不盲目相加。標示例是數學示意，不是實測證據。
