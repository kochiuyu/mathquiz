P2 · Across one thousand

2N4 pupil practical and oral sheet · Name Date

Use several short sessions. Show your counting with the materials. Read and write when asked. This is not a timed test.

A · Count, read, write

Count the units represented by the teacher’s collection. Organise the models to help you check. Say and write the total:

Read these numbers aloud: 1000 · 1008 · 2040 · 4072 · 9000 · 9999.

Write the three numbers the teacher says, without seeing a numeral card:

B · What is a thousand?

Build ten hundreds. Show how they make one thousand. Match them to the thousand model without changing the number of units.

Build 2034. Point to the 2: how many units does it stand for? . What does the 0 tell you?

Now build 3204. What does its 3 mean? Its 2? What does its 0 tell you? Explain why the 2 has a different value in these two numbers.

C · Cross the boundary

Count forward by ones from 990 to 1000. Then count backwards by hundreds from 1240 to 540. Point to the step where you cross between three-digit and four-digit numbers.

Say whether 1008, 2041, 9000 and 9999 are odd or even. Point to the digit that helps you decide and give a reason.

D · Bigger groups

The teacher shows six groups for each row. Check the quantity in one group. Point to each group and say the running total; record the final total. No multiplication or addition expression is needed.

Teacher preparation and evidence

Use base-ten models showing actual unit structure: 60 hundred grids/plates (bundle ten to represent each thousand), six 50-unit half-grids, ten tens rods and ten ones, reused between stations. A thousand can be a block with verifiable hundred layers, or ten visible hundred grids kept together. Do not use an arbitrary token marked “1000” as evidence of counting a thousand. Provide 50-unit half-grids for the 250 groups, trays, numeral cards and paper. The teacher prepares models; pupils need not manufacture them. Keep small pieces away from mouths. Hide this key and unlabelled collection totals.

TaskSetup and expected evidence
APrepare 1 thousand, 3 hundreds, 2 tens and 6 ones, without showing the total. Pupil counts the represented quantity and reports/writes 1326. Ask the pupil to read each printed number. Dictate 3006, 5080 and 7612 without showing numerals; accept natural local reading conventions, recording oral reading and independent writing separately.
BPupil matches ten hundreds to one thousand and explains the unchanged quantity. 2034: 2 thousands, 0 hundreds, 3 tens, 4 ones; 2 means 2000 and 0 means no hundreds. 3204: 3 thousands, 2 hundreds, 0 tens, 4 ones; 3 means 3000, 2 means 200, 0 means no tens. The same digit 2 represents different values in different places. Observe actual representation and spoken digit values separately.
CForward: 990,991,992,993,994,995,996,997,998,999,1000. Backward: 1240,1140,1040,940,840,740,640,540. Crossings: 999→1000 and 1040→940. 1008 even (8); 2041 odd (1); 9000 even (0); 9999 odd (9). The pupil must use the ones digit, not thousands or total digit sum.
DPrepare six groups at each size, reusing models. 200=two hundred grids; 250=two hundreds plus one visible 50-unit grid; 500=five hundreds; 1000=ten hundreds or a verified thousand model. Counts: 200,400,600,800,1000,1200; 250,500,750,1000,1250,1500; 500,1000,1500,2000,2500,3000; 1000,2000,3000,4000,5000,6000. E1000 > 999; 3050 < 3500; 7007 = 7007; 9000 > 8999; 4002 < 4020 < 4200. Listen before symbolic recording. Compare place values when thousands tie; distinguish an understood spoken comparison from a reversed symbol.

Materials can be sequentially regrouped: six thousand models supply up to 6000 represented units; unpack/replace groups as needed. Check the 50-unit subgroups before using them. Do not require pupils to count 6000 loose items one by one. The structure must still be countable and verifiable.

小二 · 跨過一千

A · 點算、讀、寫

點算老師材料表示的單位數量，可先整理核對。說出並寫下總數：

讀出：1000 · 1008 · 2040 · 4072 · 9000 · 9999。

B · 一千是甚麼？

擺十個百，展示怎樣組成一千。把它們與千的模型配對，單位總數不要改變。

表示2034。指出2，它表示多少個單位？。0表示甚麼？

再表示3204。3表示甚麼？ 2呢？ 0表示甚麼？解釋兩個數中的2為何數值不同。

C · 跨越位數

每次數一，從990順數至1000；再每次減一百，從1240倒數至540。指出哪一步跨越三位數與四位數。

說出1008、2041、9000、9999是單數還是雙數，指出幫助判斷的數字及說明理由。

D · 更大的組

每行老師展示六組。核對一組的數量，再逐組指出及口述累計總數，記錄最後總數。不用寫乘法或加法算式。

教師準備及評估證據

使用能顯示實際單位結構的十進制模型：60塊百格／百板（每十塊一組表示千）、六塊50單位半格、十條十及十個一，各站重用。千可用能核對百層的方體，或把十張可見百格放在一起。隨意標「1000」的物件不能當成點算一千的證據。另備50單位的半格板表示250的組、托盤、數字卡及紙。教師準備模型，不要求學生製作。小物件不要放近口部。隱藏本解答及無標示組的總數。

任務準備及預期證據
A準備1個千、3個百、2個十、6個一，不展示總數。學生點算所表示的數量，口述及寫1326。請學生逐一讀題上的數。口述3006、5080、7612，不展示數字，接受自然本地讀數，分開記錄口讀和獨立寫數。
B學生把十個百與一千配對，解釋數量不變。2034：2個千、0個百、3個十、4個一；2表示2000，0表示沒有百。3204：3個千、2個百、0個十、4個一；3表示3000，2表示200，0表示沒有十。同一數字2在不同位表示不同值。分開觀察實際表示與口述數值。
C順數990、991、992、993、994、995、996、997、998、999、1000。倒數1240、1140、1040、940、840、740、640、540。跨越為999→1000及1040→940。1008雙數（8），2041單數（1），9000雙數（0），9999單數（9）。根據個位，而非千位或各位數字總和。
D每次準備六組，重用模型。200用兩個百格；250用兩個百及一個可見50單位格；500用五個百；1000用十個百或已核對千模型。累計：200、400、600、800、1000、1200；250、500、750、1000、1250、1500；500、1000、1500、2000、2500、3000；1000、2000、3000、4000、5000、6000。觀察逐組指示及累計，不只看最後乘法答案。總數均不超過四位數。
E1000 > 999；3050 < 3500；7007 = 7007；9000 > 8999；4002 < 4020 < 4200。先聽口述再記符號；千位相同時比較其餘位值。口述理解與符號倒轉分開記錄。

材料可依次重新分組：六個千模型可表示最多6000單位，需要時拆開或換組。先核對50單位的小組。不要求逐個數6000件散物，但結構須可點算核實。
