5 · Solve a three-term story

A jug starts with 2 litres of water. A pupil pours out 3/4 litre and adds 1/6 litre. Write one three-term calculation and explain each sign. Find the final amount, label litres, and check it is consistent with the actions. Draw an equal-unit representation. Invent a related subtraction-and-addition story and ask a partner to solve it. Do not change the unit or the size of the whole midway.

6 · Estimate before calculating

Before exact work, estimate 7/8 + 5/6 and record a justified interval using nearby whole numbers or halves. Keep this first estimate visible. Then calculate exactly and judge whether 3/4 could be a sensible answer. Explain how an estimate detects a gross error but does not replace an exact result. For a second example, estimate 3 − 1 1/4 + 2/3, then calculate and revise only with a reason. Different defensible estimates are allowed.

Teacher · Models and prompts

Equal-whole models: 1/2 = 3/6, 1/3 = 2/6; their sum is 5/6. Compare C < A = B, and 4/5 < 5/6 < 1. Require reasoning about the same whole, not simply the apparent size of a sketch.

For subtraction, 2 1/4 = 27/12 = 1 + 15/12; remove 10/12 to leave 1 5/12 = 17/12. Adding back 5/6 recovers 2 1/4. In the jug story the signs are − then +, and the result is 1 5/12 litres. The same numerical answer in two tasks does not mean their reasoning is interchangeable.

The table contains exact worked models, not pupil observations. Row 2 is an intermediate unreduced form: it must be reduced to 5/6 for a final answer. Reduced improper and mixed forms above one are both acceptable. Whole-number results may be written as whole numbers. Ask for method, units and reasoning, not a copied key.

Estimate examples, not unique prescribed answers: both 7/8 and 5/6 exceed 3/4 and are below 1, so the sum lies strictly between 3/2 and 2; 3/4 is impossible. For 3 − 1 1/4 + 2/3, the remaining 1 3/4 plus a value between 1/2 and 1 gives a result between 2 1/4 and 2 3/4. Preserve the pupil’s original estimate and explain any revision. Formal three-number LCM calculation is not a success criterion.

教師 · 示範及提問

整體相同時，1/2＝3/6、1/3＝2/6，相加為5/6。比較結果為C＜A＝B，以及4/5＜5/6＜1。須解釋整體相同，不能只憑草圖外觀判斷。

減法：2 1/4＝27/12＝1＋15/12，減去10/12，剩1 5/12＝17/12。加回5/6可得2 1/4。水壺題的符號先減後加，答案是1 5/12升。兩題答案相同，不代表推理可以互相代替。

下表是精確示範，不是學生觀察。第2行10/12是未約簡的中間步驟，最後須約成5/6。大於1時可用最簡假分數或帶分數；整數結果可寫成整數。評估方法、單位及推理，不是抄寫答案。

估計例並非唯一指定答案：7/8及5/6都大於3/4而小於1，故和嚴格介乎3/2與2，3/4不合理。3－1 1/4＋2/3先剩1 3/4，再加一個介乎1/2與1的數，故結果介乎2 1/4與2 3/4。保留學生最初估計及修訂原因。三數最小公倍數的正式算法不是達標條件。
