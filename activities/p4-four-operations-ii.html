Teacher key

1. 6 × (10 + 4) = 6 × 10 + 6 × 4 = 60 + 24 = 84. Drawing or objects must show all six groups split into ten and four, not just one added four. 23 × 17 + 23 × 13 = 23 × (17 + 13) = 23 × 30 = 690. Accept equivalent grouping with a clear explanation; no terminology test.

2. 96 ÷ 8 × 3 = 12 × 3 = 36. Equal-priority division and multiplication proceed left to right. 96 ÷ (8 × 3) = 96 ÷ 24 = 4: do brackets first. 84 ÷ 7 + 9 = 12 + 9 = 21; 96 ÷ 8 − 5 = 12 − 5 = 7. Division precedes addition or subtraction.

3. 96 ÷ 8 × 3 + 17 − 5 = 12 × 3 + 17 − 5 = 36 + 17 − 5 = 53 − 5 = 48. 120 − 72 ÷ 8 × 3 + 6 = 120 − 9 × 3 + 6 = 120 − 27 + 6 = 93 + 6 = 99. (18 + 6) × (15 − 9) = 24 × 6 = 144. Both bracket calculations precede the final multiplication.

4. The first error is replacing 6 × (10 + 4) by 60 + 4: the four must also be multiplied by six. 60 + 4 = 64 is correct arithmetic for the wrong expression; the intended answer is 84. In the second example, combining 8 × 3 first inserts an unrequested bracket. The intended answer is 36, not 4. Require a correction with a reason, not just a new answer.

5. Unit price: HK$84 ÷ 7 = HK$12 per book. Exact total: HK$12 × 19 = HK$228. One suitable estimate uses 20 books at HK$12, about HK$240; 19 books should cost one book less. Other reasonable estimates with explained methods are accepted. Estimation may use the one-book price, but record it before calculating the exact target total. Do not require the single estimate 240 or retroactively replace the pupil’s estimate.

6. Unit price: HK$108 ÷ 9 = HK$12 per notebook. HK$180 ÷ (HK$12 per notebook) = 15 notebooks. Check 15 × 12 = 180. The second operation is division to find a quantity, not multiplication to find a total price. Constant price lets the same one-item price apply to the target purchase.

教師解答

1. 6 × (10 + 4) = 6 × 10 + 6 × 4 = 60 + 24 = 84。圖畫或實物須展示六組各分成十及四，不能只加一次四。23 × 17 + 23 × 13 = 23 × (17 + 13) = 23 × 30 = 690。可接受附清楚解釋的等值分組，不考術語。

2. 96 ÷ 8 × 3 = 12 × 3 = 36。乘除同級，由左至右計算。96 ÷ (8 × 3) = 96 ÷ 24 = 4，先算括號內。84 ÷ 7 + 9 = 12 + 9 = 21；96 ÷ 8 − 5 = 12 − 5 = 7。除法先於加減。

3. 96 ÷ 8 × 3 + 17 − 5 = 12 × 3 + 17 − 5 = 36 + 17 − 5 = 53 − 5 = 48。120 − 72 ÷ 8 × 3 + 6 = 120 − 9 × 3 + 6 = 120 − 27 + 6 = 93 + 6 = 99。(18 + 6) × (15 − 9) = 24 × 6 = 144。兩個括號內的計算都先於最後的乘法。

4. 第一例最先錯在把6 × (10 + 4)改成60 + 4，四也須乘六。60 + 4 = 64本身計算正確，但已改錯算式，原題應得84。第二例先合併8 × 3，等於自行加上原題沒有的括號。原題應得36而非4。須有修正理由，不只換答案。

5. 一本售價：港幣84元 ÷ 7 = 每本港幣12元。準確總價：港幣12元 × 19 = 港幣228元。一個合理估計是以20本、每本12元估算，約港幣240元；19本應少一本售價。接受其他有解釋的合理估計。估算可用一本售價，但須在算出所問準確總價前記錄。不可只接受240，或事後改寫學生的原估計。

6. 一本售價：港幣108元 ÷ 9 = 每本港幣12元。港幣180元 ÷ 每本港幣12元 = 15本。檢查15 × 12 = 180。第二步是除法求數量，不是乘法求總價。售價固定才可把同一本售價用於所問購買數量。
