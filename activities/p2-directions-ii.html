P2 · Four main directions

Name ______ Date ______ · Work with an adult in a small, clear, level area away from roads, stairs, water and vehicles. No blindfolds. An adult checks the whole route first. Stop to read the compass rather than walking while looking down.

1 · Names and short forms

Match east, south, west and north to E, S, W and N. Say each pair and locate the markings on a compass. Starting at north, point clockwise to the other three directions. Explain the relationship of east/west and north/south.

2 · Use a real compass

Stop at the adult-selected centre O. Hold a real compass level, away from magnets, metal objects and electronic devices, and wait for the needle to settle. Use this compass’s instructions to identify the north-seeking end; do not guess from colour alone. Find north, then east, south and west from the markings. Point and place N/E/S/W floor cards. Turn your body, read again and check that the cards remain correct. Record how you held, read and checked the compass.

Relative positions and safe movement

3 · Say what is where

An adult places toy A at checked north, B east, C south and D west of O. Verify with the compass rather than copying cards. Say four sentences: “___ is to the ___ of O.” Then say where O is relative to A, and where O is relative to B. What changes when you reverse the reference and target? Turn your body and repeat.

4 · Follow the compass and turn

The adult first verifies a short clear northward route followed by an eastward route. At O use the compass to find north, put it safely away, walk two small steps north and stop. Turn right, then check with the compass which direction you now face. Walk two small steps that way and stop. The adult guides you safely back to O. Face south, point to your right and verify with the compass without walking farther. Explain why “right” is not always “east”. If movement is inaccessible, direct a partner, recording your own compass reading separately from their walking.

5 · Show your method

Record O and the four toys in a sketch with a north arrow and complete relative-position sentences. Ask a partner to locate an object from your sentences; revise by naming the reference object if unclear. Demonstrate one independent compass reading and point in a requested direction, explaining how you avoid interference.

Teacher · Setup and key

Provide a reliable compass with its instructions, removable O/N/E/S/W floor cards and A/B/C/D toys or labels. Check the compass beforehand and remove magnets or nearby interfering metal/electronic objects. Choose a safe site with stable readings. If readings are unreliable, relocate or replace the instrument; do not force a walk on an unreliable bearing. Only the four main directions are required: no eight-point compass, numerical bearings or magnetic-declination correction.

1: east E, south S, west W, north N; clockwise N,E,S,W. East/west and north/south are opposite. 2: observe level holding, waiting, identifying the north-seeking end from the instrument instructions and checking directions. Reading cards alone is not measurement. Record any assistance in positioning cards.

3: A north of O, B east, C south, D west; O south of A and west of B. Body rotation does not alter object directions; swapping reference and target reverses the direction. 4: facing north, right is east; facing south, right is west. Supervise actual walking throughout; stop to read the instrument. 5: sketch north arrow/positions agree, sentences include target, direction and reference.

Teacher · Observable record

Record independent / prompted / not yet / not observed with actual words, actions and assistance. Ask “How do you know? What is your reference?” without giving the direction first. Sketches or choice answers cannot prove compass handling or walking.

1

Names four directions and short forms.

2

Actually holds/settles/reads the compass and finds directions.

3

Describes target relative to reference and reverses the relationship.

4

Walks safely in measured directions; distinguishes body-relative turns.

5

Rechecks after turning and explains the method.

小二 · 四個主要方向

姓名 ______ 日期 ______ · 與成人在平坦、暢通的小範圍活動，遠離道路、樓梯、水邊和車輛。不蒙眼；成人先檢查整條路線。拿指南針時停下，不邊看邊走。

1 · 認識名稱及縮寫

配對東、南、西、北與 E、S、W、N。讀出每一對，再指出指南針上的相應標記。從北順時針指出其餘三個方向，說東與西、南與北的關係。

2 · 真正用指南針

在成人選定的中心點 O 停下。把指南針平放，遠離磁鐵、金屬物件和電子裝置，待針穩定。按這個指南針的說明辨認指北端，不可只猜顏色。找北，依指南針標記找東、南、西，分別指出方向並放置 N、E、S、W 地面卡。轉身後重新量方向，檢查卡是否仍正確。記錄你如何拿穩、讀針及覆核。

相對位置及安全行走

3 · 說清楚誰在誰的哪方

成人在已核對的四方向卡旁放置玩具 A（北）、B（東）、C（南）、D（西）。你用指南針重新核對，不只抄卡。以 O 為參考說出四句「___ 在 O 的 ___ 方」。再以 A 為參考說 O 在哪方，及以 B 為參考說 O 在哪方。調換說話次序時方向會怎樣？轉身後再說一次。

4 · 按指南針行走及轉向

成人先用指南針確認一小段安全北向路線及其後東向路線。你在 O 用指南針找北，收好量具，沿北走兩小步，停下。向右轉，再用指南針核對現在面向哪方。沿該方向走兩小步，停下。成人帶你安全回到 O。再面向南，指出右邊的方向，用指南針核對但不用再走。說明為何「右」不是永遠「東」。如行動不便，可指示同伴移動，但須分開記錄本人讀指南針及他人行走。

5 · 展示你的方法

用畫圖和完整句記錄 O 及四個玩具的位置，畫上指北箭頭。請同伴按你的句子找物件；若不清楚，指出參考物件再修改。向成人展示一次獨立讀指南針並指出指定方向，說明怎樣避免干擾。

教師 · 準備及答案

準備有說明的可靠指南針、O/N/E/S/W 可移除地面卡及 A/B/C/D 玩具或標牌。成人預先核對指南針，清理磁鐵及可能干擾的金屬／電子物件，選一個讀數穩定的安全場地。若讀數不穩定，移位或換工具；不要強迫沿不可靠方向走。只用四主方向，不要求八方位、方位角或磁偏角修正。

1：東 E、南 S、西 W、北 N；順時針 N、E、S、W。東西相對，南北相對。2：觀察平放、等待穩定、依產品說明讀指北端和核對方向；只讀出卡片不算實測。成人可協助放卡，但須記錄支援。

3：A 在 O 北方、B 東方、C 南方、D 西方；O 在 A 南方、O 在 B 西方。轉身不改物件方位，交換參考和目標會反向。4：面北向右轉是東；面南的右方是西。真實行走須成人全程監督，停下才讀量具。5：圖有正確指北箭頭和相對位置，句子包含目標、方向和參考物件。

教師 · 可觀察記錄

逐項記獨立／提示後／尚未／未觀察，寫實際語句、動作和援助。問「你怎樣知道？參考的是甚麼？」不先給方向。畫圖或選擇題不能證明親自讀指南針或行走。

1

讀出四方向及縮寫。

2

實際平放指南針、待穩定並正確找方向。

3

用「某物在參考物的某方」描述及反向說明。

4

按量得方向安全行走，分辨轉向與固定方位。

5

轉身後重新覆核，並解釋自己的方法。
