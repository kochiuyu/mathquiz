Fractions (V): investigate and explain

1 · Share and compare

Use paper strips to share 3 equal loaves among 4 people. Draw each share and write a division equation. Then compare 3 red counters with 4 blue counters: write red as a fraction of BLUE, and separately of ALL counters. Explain why these differ. Invent another comparison greater than one.

Original estimate, drawing, reasoning and check. Separate paper is allowed.

2 · Measure and divide

Use a schematic 5 m ribbon and 3/4 m pieces. Predict the number of complete pieces, model them, and record the leftover in metres. Check used length plus leftover equals the original. Do not confuse the fraction of a piece with metres left. Physical rulers require calibration; screen lengths are schematic.

Original estimate, drawing, reasoning and check. Separate paper is allowed.

Fractions (V): investigate and explain

3 · Predict, model and check

Choose a positive whole number divided by a fraction below one, and a fraction divided by a number above one. Predict whether each result grows or shrinks, draw a model and verify by multiplication. Also test a zero dividend. Write your own examples and a benchmark estimate before exact work.

Original estimate, drawing, reasoning and check. Separate paper is allowed.

4 · Order and brackets

Use exactly three operand cards 1/2, 3/4, 2 and operations +, ×. Calculate with conventional order, then put brackets around the sum. Record both results and explain their difference. Create another three-operand pair with division; use no more than one mixed number and never divide by zero.

Original estimate, drawing, reasoning and check. Separate paper is allowed.

Fractions (V): investigate and explain

5 · Packing complete pieces

Model these amount/piece-length pairs in metres: 5 and3/4; 3 and1/2; 1/4 and2/3. For each find complete pieces and leftover. Identify an exact fit and an insufficient amount. Check original = complete count × piece + leftover, with leftover smaller than a piece. Invent a further example.

Original estimate, drawing, reasoning and check. Separate paper is allowed.

6 · Constant-rate reasoning

In a model shop, 4 identical notebooks cost $7 at a constant price per notebook. Estimate and find the cost of one and six. Create a quantity-cost table and an original suitable constant-rate story. Contrast a shop offering a bundle discount: explain why one fixed unit price may fail. These are supplied models, not observed prices.

Original estimate, drawing, reasoning and check. Separate paper is allowed.

分數（五）：探究與解釋

1 · 平均分配與比較

用紙條把3個相同麵包平均分給4人，畫出每份並寫除法式。再比較3個紅棋子與4個藍棋子：分別寫出紅棋子是藍棋子及全部棋子的幾分之幾，解釋差異。自擬一個大於1的比較。

記錄原創估算、圖畫、理由及驗算，可另附紙張。

2 · 量度與除法

以示意圖表示5米絲帶及每段3/4米，先估計完整段數，再畫模型，記錄剩餘米數。驗算已用長度加剩餘等於原長。不要把一段的分數誤當餘下米數。實物量度須校準尺，屏幕長度只作示意。

記錄原創估算、圖畫、理由及驗算，可另附紙張。

分數（五）：探究與解釋

3 · 預測、模型與驗算

自選正整數除以小於1的分數，以及分數除以大於1的數。先預測商變大或變小，畫模型並用乘法驗算。另試被除數為0。先寫自擬例子及估算，再作準確計算。

記錄原創估算、圖畫、理由及驗算，可另附紙張。

4 · 次序與括號

使用1/2、3/4、2三張數卡及＋、×，先依運算次序計算，再把加法部分加括號。記錄兩個結果及解釋差異。另自擬含除法的三數比較，最多用一個帶分數，不可除以0。

分數（五）：探究與解釋

5 · 分裝完整份量

以米為單位表示原長與每段長度：5及3/4；3及1/2；1/4及2/3。逐一求完整段數及餘長，找出剛好用完與不足一段的情況。驗算原長＝段數×每段長＋餘長，且餘長小於每段長。另自擬例子。

記錄原創估算、圖畫、理由及驗算，可另附紙張。

6 · 固定單價推理

在模擬商店，4本相同筆記簿售7元，每本單價固定。估算並求1本及6本的價格，製作數量與總價表，自擬合適的固定單價題。比較有套裝折扣的商店，解釋為何不能用單一固定單價。這些是提供的模型，不是實際觀察價格。

記錄原創估算、圖畫、理由及驗算，可另附紙張。

提供的示例解答，並非學生觀察

3÷4＝3/4，每人得3/4個麵包。紅與藍比較為3/4；紅佔全部為3/7。7/3是大於1的比較。

5÷(3/4)＝20/3；完整6段，餘1/2米，因5＝6×3/4＋1/2。

例：2÷(1/2)＝4；3/4÷2＝3/8；0÷(3/5)＝0。嚴格變大或變小的說法只適用於正被除數。須驗算學生自擬例子；估算須有理由，不限單一指定數值。

1/2＋3/4×2＝2；(1/2＋3/4)×2＝5/2。除法例：2÷1/2÷3/4＝16/3；2÷(1/2÷3/4)＝3。括號改變組合。

各組（段數，餘長米）為(6,1/2)、(6,0)、(0,1/4)。不可把商四捨五入作完整段數。

每本7/4元；6本21/2＝10 1/2元。1、2、4、6本分別售7/4、7/2、7、21/2元。折扣可能破壞固定單價假設，不可預填學生觀察。
