Teacher key

1. 7/3 = 2 1/3. All three strips are equal wholes; each must have three equal parts, with seven parts marked in total. Written 2/3 is proper; 3/3 and 7/3 are improper (equality is included); 2 1/3 is mixed; written 3 is a whole number, not a mixed number. With positive numerators and denominators, proper means numerator smaller than denominator; improper includes numerator equal to denominator. No classification convention for a zero numerator is assessed.

2. 9/4 = 2 1/4 and 2 1/4 = 9/4; 6/2 = 3 and 3 = 6/2 when denominator 2 is requested. The nine quarter-parts span two full strips and one quarter; six halves fill all three strips. Written 6/2 is improper even though its value is a whole number. Written 3 is not a mixed number 3 0/2: a mixed number has a strictly proper, positive fractional part.

3. 3/4 = 9/12: multiply numerator and denominator by 3, splitting each quarter into three equal parts of the same whole. 10/4 = 5/2: divide both by 2; 5 and 2 have no common factor greater than 1. 2 1/2 is also a lowest-term mixed form. Adding 1 does not preserve value here: 3/4 = 15/20, whereas 4/5 = 16/20. A same-whole picture is sufficient; unlike-denominator arithmetic is not required. An unreduced equivalent has the same value but does not meet a lowest-terms instruction.

4. 3/4 < 7/4 < 2 < 11/4. On this line the unit length is 100 coordinate units: 3/4 at x=95, 7/4 at x=195, 2 at x=220, 11/4 at x=295. 2 1/4 and 9/4 coincide at x=245. These coordinates are teacher checks only, not pupil labels. Compare on the same unit; 2 = 8/4.

5. 3 − 1 1/4 = 12/4 − 5/4 = 7/4 = 1 3/4. Alternatively regroup 3 as 2 4/4 before subtraction. 3 − 1 1/4 + 3/4 = 7/4 + 3/4 = 10/4 = 5/2 = 2 1/2. 2/6 + 3/6 + 5/6 = 10/6 = 5/3 = 1 2/3. 3/5 − 3/5 = 0: the entire original quantity is removed. Keep a common denominator while combining parts, then reduce. Zero is a valid whole-number answer, not a fraction-classification test.

6. Exact remainder: 3 m − 1 3/8 m − 3/8 m = 24/8 m − 11/8 m − 3/8 m = 10/8 m = 5/4 m = 1 1/4 m. A suitable estimate rounds the two cuts together to about 2 m, leaving about 1 m. Other justified estimates are valid. Record the estimate before revealing or calculating the exact remainder; do not replace it afterward. Each diagram must use the same metre unit.

教師解答

1. 7/3 = 2 1/3。三條紙條均為相同整體，各須分成三等份，合共標出七份。按寫法，2/3是真分數；3/3及7/3是假分數（包括分子等於分母）；2 1/3是帶分數；3是整數而非帶分數。對正分子及正分母，真分數的分子小於分母，假分數包括分子等於分母。不評核分子為零的分類慣例。

2. 9/4 = 2 1/4及2 1/4 = 9/4；6/2 = 3，指定分母2時，3 = 6/2。九個四分之一佔兩條整紙條及四分之一；六個二分之一填滿三條。6/2的寫法是假分數，即使數值是整數。寫作3並非帶分數3 0/2；帶分數的分數部分須大於零且小於1。

3. 3/4 = 9/12：分子、分母同乘3，把同一整體的每個四分之一再分成三等份。10/4 = 5/2：兩者同除以2；5和2沒有大於1的公因數。2 1/2也是最簡帶分數寫法。同加1在此不保持數值：3/4 = 15/20，而4/5 = 16/20。可用相同整體圖解，不要求異分母運算。未約簡的等值分數數值相同，但不符合最簡分數要求。

4. 3/4 < 7/4 < 2 < 11/4。這數線每個單位長100個座標單位：3/4在x=95，7/4在x=195，2在x=220，11/4在x=295。2 1/4與9/4同在x=245。座標只供教師檢查，不是預填學生標記。比較時用相同單位；2 = 8/4。

5. 3 − 1 1/4 = 12/4 − 5/4 = 7/4 = 1 3/4；也可先把3重組為2 4/4。3 − 1 1/4 + 3/4 = 7/4 + 3/4 = 10/4 = 5/2 = 2 1/2。2/6 + 3/6 + 5/6 = 10/6 = 5/3 = 1 2/3。3/5 − 3/5 = 0，原有量全部減去。合併份數時保留共同分母，最後約簡。零是有效整數答案，不是分數分類測驗。

6. 準確餘量：3米 − 1 3/8米 − 3/8米 = 24/8米 − 11/8米 − 3/8米 = 10/8米 = 5/4米 = 1 1/4米。一個合理估計是兩次合共剪去約2米，餘下約1米。接受其他有理由的估計。在透露或算出準確餘量前記錄估計，不可事後改寫。每幅圖都須用相同的米作單位。
