P1 · Length and distance (II)

Teacher-led centimetre activity · Name Date

Materials: a real centimetre ruler, a centimetre-marked tape measure, two strips or pencils, and three floor or desk points. This sheet has no printed measuring scale: use the real tools.

  1. Find one centimetre: Point to the 0 and 1 marks on the ruler. Show the space between them and say its name and short form: .
  2. Measure and compare objects: Choose the tool for each object. Put one end at the 0 mark, keep the tool straight, and read the other end. Record both lengths in centimetres below; circle the longer object.
  3. Measure and compare distances: Mark two straight point-to-point distances, A and B, on a desk or floor. Choose a suitable centimetre-marked tool, align its 0 with each start, and measure straight to each endpoint. Record both distances; circle the longer one.
  4. Estimate before measuring: First measure your own thumb width or hand span in centimetres. Use that familiar measure to estimate an object's length. Write the estimate before checking it with the ruler.

If an end falls between marks, record the nearest whole centimetre.

小一 · 長度和距離（二）

教師帶領的厘米活動 · 姓名 日期

材料：真實的厘米尺、有厘米刻度的捲尺、兩條紙帶或兩枝鉛筆，以及桌面或地面上的三個標記點。本工作紙沒有供量度的印刷刻度；請使用真正的量度工具。

  1. 找出一厘米：指出尺上的0和1刻度，展示兩者之間的一格，說出單位名稱和符號：。
  2. 量度和比較物件：為每件物件選擇工具。把物件的一端對準0刻度，尺放直，再讀取另一端的刻度。在下表以厘米記錄兩件物件的長度，圈出較長的一件。
  3. 量度和比較距離：在桌面或地面標示A和B兩段兩點之間的直線距離。選擇合適而有厘米刻度的工具，把0刻度對準每段距離的起點，沿直線量至終點。記錄兩段距離，圈出較長的一段。
  4. 先估計，後量度：先量出自己的拇指闊度或手掌張開的跨度（厘米）。利用這個熟悉的長度估計一件物件的長度，先寫估計，然後才用尺核對。

如果終點在兩個刻度之間，記錄最接近的整厘米數。
