Teacher preparation and rubric

Materials: real centimetre rulers, centimetre tape, two unit-tile sets (each set identical, small tiles 2 cm long and large tiles 3 cm long), a 12 cm strip, strips A=8 cm/B=14 cm, Start–C=20 cm/Start–D=40 cm, another object X=18 cm and marked straight span Y=30 cm. Teacher prepares and verifies these lengths with real tools; conceal numbers until responses are recorded. These are suggested setups, not printed scales. If you change materials, premeasure and record the new answers. Use clear tabletop spans and supervise tape use.

A · Standard unit

Same 12 cm strip: six small tiles, four large tiles. It has not changed; unit sizes differ. Counts need their unit to be meaningful. Both centimetre rulers show 12 cm when used correctly; a shared unit makes results comparable. Pupil identifies the 0–1 interval as one centimetre, symbol cm. Do not infer the need for a standard from scale reading alone.

B · Measurement procedure

Expected readings 8, 14, 20, 40 cm; B longer and D farther. Observe tool choice, zero alignment, straight placement and endpoint reading for each kind of measure. A tape is convenient for the 40 cm distance; a ruler is suitable for short strips. Accept another tool if used accurately. Choose whole-centimetre endpoints for these scored items; rounding or millimetre calculation is not an additional requirement. Schematic online diagrams remain scale-reading exercises, not physical rulers.

C · Calibrate, estimate, check

The child first measures their own reference after B. Body dimensions vary: use the observed value, not a fixed “all thumbs are 1 cm” claim. If it lies between marks, the teacher can supply an approximate whole-centimetre reference for this estimation task without teaching a rounding rule. Ask the pupil to keep the hand/thumb position consistent and mark successive endpoints without gaps/overlaps. They may imagine or repeat the familiar reference and use repeated addition; multiplication is not required. Record estimate and method before revealing/measuring X/Y. Actual targets are 18/30 cm in the suggested setup. Judge a plausible estimate supported by the reference and subsequent comparison, not a fixed error percentage. Correct measurement after counting out a known ruler scale is not evidence of prior estimation. Explain that another person may have a different body span.

教師準備及評估準則

材料：真正厘米尺、厘米捲尺、兩套方片（每套大小相同，小方片長2厘米，大方片長3厘米）、12厘米紙帶、A紙帶8厘米／B紙帶14厘米、起點至C為20厘米／至D為40厘米、另一物件X長18厘米及直線段Y長30厘米。教師用真實工具準備並核對，記錄回答前不要透露數字。這些是建議設定，並非供量度的印刷刻度。改材料時須先量度並記下新答案。使用清空桌面，監督捲尺使用。

A · 標準單位

同一條12厘米紙帶用六塊小方片或四塊大方片。紙帶沒有改變，單位大小不同，只有數目不足以表達長度。正確使用時，兩把厘米尺都讀12厘米；共同單位使結果可比較。學生指出0至1為一厘米，符號cm。只會讀刻度不足以顯示明白使用標準單位的需要。

B · 量度程序

預期讀數為8、14、20、40厘米；B較長，D較遠。每類量度都觀察工具選擇、對準0、放直及讀取終點。40厘米距離用捲尺較方便，短紙帶可用直尺；準確使用其他工具也接受。評估題準備整厘米端點，不把進捨或毫米計算加為要求。網上示意尺只評估讀刻度，不是真正量度工具。

C · 量參考、估計、核對

學生完成B後先量自己的參考長度。身體大小各有不同，採用實際值，不假定所有人的拇指都闊1厘米。如在兩格之間，教師可提供約整厘米的參考值用於本估計題，不必教進捨法。提示保持手掌／拇指姿勢一致，逐次標記端點，沒有空隙或重疊。可想像或重複使用熟悉的長度，以連加協助，不要求乘法。展示或量度X／Y前記錄估計及方法，建議設定的真實長度為18／30厘米。評估有參考依據的合理估計及其後核對，不設固定誤差百分比。先用已知尺刻度量出正確答案，不代表曾作估計。說明另一人的身體跨度可能不同。
