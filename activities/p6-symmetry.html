P6 · Symmetry

1 · Fold and test

Trace a square, a non-square rectangle and a non-equilateral isosceles triangle. Predict each mirror line, fold and record which halves coincide.

P6 · Symmetry

2 · Compare outlines

Test a rhombus, equilateral triangle and circle. Mark all axes you find and explain why the circle is different. Ignore decorative patterns.

P6 · Symmetry

3 · Complete a half-outline

On the grid draw the half-outline joining (column 2, row 2), (column 5, row 4), (column 6, row 7), (column 6, row 2). Use column 6 as the mirror. Complete the other half by counting squares, then join corresponding points.

P6 · Symmetry

4 · Make a symmetric shape

Fold paper and design a half-outline against the fold. With adult supervision cut it, unfold and label its mirror. Keep your original prediction and actual result.

P6 · Symmetry

5 · Challenge equal-area reasoning

Draw two equal-area halves that do not coincide on folding. Explain why equal area alone is not a test of axial symmetry.

P6 · Symmetry

6 · Create and verify

Design another outline with a horizontal mirror on the blank grid. Ask a partner to test each matching point and record any revision.

Teacher models and observation record

A square has four axes; a non-square rectangle has two; a non-equilateral isosceles triangle has one; an equilateral triangle has three; a non-square rhombus has two; a circle has infinitely many. In task 3 the matching points are (10,2), (7,4), (6,7), (6,2), written as column,row. Check actual pupil constructions separately.

P6 · 對稱

1 · 探究任務

描畫正方形、非正方形的長方形及非等邊的等腰三角形，預測鏡線再摺，記錄哪些兩半能重疊。

P6 · 對稱

2 · 探究任務

測試菱形、等邊三角形及圓，標出所有找到的對稱軸，解釋圓的不同之處，不考慮裝飾。

P6 · 對稱

3 · 探究任務

在方格上把（直欄2，橫行2）、（直欄5，橫行4）、（直欄6，橫行7）、（直欄6，橫行2）依次連成半邊輪廓，以直欄6為鏡線，數格補上另一半並連點。

P6 · 對稱

4 · 探究任務

摺紙，在摺線旁設計半邊輪廓，由成人指導剪開、攤開及標鏡線，保留預測和實際結果。

P6 · 對稱

5 · 探究任務

畫出面積相同但摺合不能重疊的兩半，說明為何面積相同不足以證明軸對稱。

P6 · 對稱

6 · 探究任務

在空白方格設計以橫線為鏡線的另一輪廓，請同伴檢查對應點並記錄修改。

正方形有四條、非正方形長方形兩條、非等邊等腰三角形一條、等邊三角形三條、非正方形菱形兩條，圓有無限多條。任務3對應點按直欄、橫行寫為(10,2)、(7,4)、(6,7)、(6,2)，學生實際圖作須另行檢查。
