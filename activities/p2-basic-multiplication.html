Use several short sessions. You may build or draw first. This is not a speed test.

A · Equal groups and repeated addition

Put 4 counters in each of 3 trays. Tell what each number means. Write the repeated addition, then a multiplication sentence for the total:

Point to × and name this sign. Point to the product in your sentence and tell what it means.

Now make trays with 2, 3 and 4 counters. Can one equal-group multiplication sentence describe these three trays as they are? Show why or why not.

B · Build a table, not just copy it

Use counters, rows or repeated addition to build the rows for 2, 5 and 10 first, then the other rows from 3 to 9. In each box write the row number times the top number. The two tables continue the same rows. Check examples with your model and explain one pattern you notice.

After C, complete rows 1 and 0 and the final column headed 0. Finish all boxes over several sessions. Keep a record of which rows you built with help.

Teacher setup, key and rubric

Materials: 100 large counters, ten trays/circles, squared paper, pencils and a blank pupil table. Reuse materials, keeping counters away from mouths. One complete table is constructed across sessions, not demanded in one sitting. Keep key hidden. Start with 2/5/10 counting, develop the remaining 2–10 rows, then 1/0. The final zero column follows C. This ordering supports understanding, not a speed/recitation requirement.

TaskExpected evidence
A3 equal groups of 4: 4 + 4 + 4 = 12; either 3 × 4 = 12 or 4 × 3 = 12. Pupil identifies multiplication sign and product as the result (12). Unequal trays 2/3/4 do not represent three equal groups as arranged; 3 × 3 has the same total but would require regrouping. Do not mistake equal totals for the same grouping representation.
BEach cell is row factor times column factor; full reference table follows. Check that pupils built/checked values through counting or repeated addition and can explain a pattern, not merely copy. Record completed rows and support. Completion is table construction evidence, not proof of unassisted recall of every fact.
C5 × 1 = 5 and 1 × 5 = 5; 5 × 0 = 0 and 0 × 5 = 0. Observe counters and empty trays/absence of groups. Ask for another example before accepting a general verbal claim. No division by zero or other division introduced.
D3 × 4 = 12 and 4 × 3 = 12, same 12 counters before/after turning. Check a second child-chosen pair. Both factor orders describe the same total. No required property name; no associative-property or three-factor task added.
EProducts 42, 72, 100, 0. Stories: 4 × 6 or 6 × 4 = 24 stickers; 7 × 3 or 3 × 7 = 21 cups. Require a multiplication representation with matching quantity/unit; both orders accepted. A correct total with unrelated factors does not demonstrate the story model. Stories involve one multiplication only, no later mixed operations.

Avoid reading answer tables aloud during pupil work. Suggested prompts: “What is repeated?” “Are the groups equal?” “What does this factor count?” “How could objects check that cell?” “Did turning add any counters?”

教師準備、解答及評估準則

材料：100個大圓片、十個盤或圈、方格紙、筆及空白乘法表。重用材料，圓片不要放近口部。跨幾節建立整表，不要求一次完成，並隱藏解答。先用2／5／10分組點算，建立2至10其餘各行，再處理1／0；最後的零列在C後完成。此次序幫助理解，不是速度或背誦要求。

任務預期證據
A3組各4個：4 + 4 + 4 = 12；3 × 4 = 12或4 × 3 = 12均可。辨認乘號及積為結果12。2／3／4三盤並非三組相等數量；3 × 3雖總數相同，但須先重新分組。不要把相同總數當成相同分組表示。
B每格為行數乘列數，完整參考表如下。核對學生透過點算或連加建立及核對，能解釋一個規律，而非只抄寫。記錄完成各行及支援。填完整表是建表證據，不證明每個算式都能獨立回憶。
C5 × 1 = 5及1 × 5 = 5；5 × 0 = 0及0 × 5 = 0。觀察圓片、空盤及沒有組的情況。接受概括說法前請學生再舉一例。不引入除以零或其他除法。

E積為42、72、100、0。故事：4 × 6或6 × 4 = 24張貼紙；7 × 3或3 × 7 = 21隻杯。須有符合情境數量的乘法表示及單位，兩種次序均接受。總數正確但因數無關，不能顯示情境理解。每題只有一次乘法，不加入後續混合運算。
