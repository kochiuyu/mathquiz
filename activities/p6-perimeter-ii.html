3 · Inverse length

For C=62.8 cm with π=3.14, find the diameter then radius. Draw labelled quantities and substitute back to check.

4 · Full boundary not just arc

Trace outside edges of a semicircle radius 7 cm and a quarter-circle radius 14 cm. Use π=22/7. Include straight edges and label which lengths are added.

5 · Composite boundary

Join two semicircles radius 7 cm with two straight lengths 10 cm to make a stadium. Mark the exterior and any internal joins separately; find perimeter using π=22/7.

6 · Construct and justify

Create a simple rectangle/semicircle composite with stated dimensions. Ask a partner to trace every exterior edge, estimate and calculate. Keep the drawing, equations and partner critique blank until produced.

Teacher model and rubric

1. Ratios should be near π subject to measurement error;π is not exactly 3.14 or 22/7.

2. Model:r=7 cm,π=22/7 gives C=44 cm;d=10 cm,π=3.14 gives C=31.4 cm.

3. d=20 cm;r=10 cm.

4. Semicircle 22+14=36 cm;quarter-circle 22+28=50 cm.

Teacher model and rubric

5. Two semicircular arcs make one circumference 44 cm;add 20 cm for 64 cm. Interior diameters are not exterior.

6. Check consistent radii/diameters, explicit π approximation and exclusion of internal joins; more than one valid design is possible.

教師模型及準則

1. 比值應受測量誤差影響而接近π；π不恰好等於3.14或22/7。

2. 模型：r＝7cm、π＝22/7得C＝44cm；d＝10cm、π＝3.14得C＝31.4cm。

3. d＝20cm；r＝10cm。

4. 半圓22＋14＝36cm；四分之一圓22＋28＝50cm。

教師模型及準則

5. 兩半圓弧合成周長44cm，加20cm得64cm；內部直徑不屬外圍。

6. 檢查半徑直徑相容、π近似值清楚且不計內部接縫，設計可不唯一。
