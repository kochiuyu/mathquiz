Circle area investigation

1 · Fold equal parts

Fold paper circles into 4,8 and 16 equal parts. Mark equal central pieces, cut safely if permitted, and record how edges look as pieces get narrower. Keep actual drawings/photos and observations.

2 · Rearrange and explain

Alternate equal pieces to approach a rectangle-like arrangement. Identify a base near half the circumference and height near the radius. Explain why the finite arrangement is not an exact rectangle.

Circle area investigation

3 · Use radius twice

Find a radius 7 cm circle area using π=22/7 and a diameter 10 cm circle area using π=3.14. Explain why circumference is not area.

4 · Half quarter and three-quarter

For a radius 14 cm circle with π=22/7, find half, quarter and three-quarter areas by fractions of the whole. Draw the shaded fractions. Do not use an angle/360 formula.

Circle area investigation

5 · Estimate and check

Estimate a drawn circle area using a square grid, then compare with the formula using measured radius. State that printing and measurement affect the comparison; do not infer radius from a supplied area.

6 · Explain an error and reflect

A pupil uses 2πr to answer an area question. Explain the units and formula error, and create a correctly labelled fractional-circle example for a partner. Optional history: research contributions of Liu Hui or Zu Chongzhi without presenting invented quotations.

Teacher model and rubric

1. Equal parts can support an approximation argument; finite curved pieces do not become exact triangles.

2. A≈(C/2)×r supports A=πr² as refinement is considered. Do not claim finite dissection proves an exact rectangle.

3. 154 cm² and 78.5 cm²; the second radius is 5 cm.

4. Whole 616 cm²;half 308;quarter 154;three-quarter 462 cm².

Teacher model and rubric

5. Record actual counted whole/partial squares and measured radius; boundary estimates need a stated rule.

6. 2πr is a length;πr² is area. Keep required area reasoning distinct from optional history.

Teacher observations

圓面積探究

1 · 摺等份

把紙圓摺4、8、16等份，標等份，獲准時安全剪開，記錄小塊變窄時邊緣的改變，保留原圖／照片及觀察。

2 · 重排並解釋

把等份交錯重排成接近長方形，指出底接近半周長、高接近半徑，說明有限排列並非精確長方形。

圓面積探究

3 · 半徑用兩次

用π＝22/7求半徑7cm圓面積；用π＝3.14求直徑10cm圓面積，解釋周長不是面積。

4 · 半圓四分之一及四分之三

半徑14cm圓、π＝22/7，用整圓分數求半圓、四分之一及四分之三面積，畫陰影分數，不用角度／360公式。

圓面積探究

5 · 估算及核對

用方格估紙上圓面積，再以實測半徑代公式比較，說明列印及測量誤差，不從已知面積反求半徑。

6 · 解釋錯誤與反思

學生以2πr回答面積題，解釋單位及公式錯誤，作有標示的分數圓例子給伙伴。可選研究劉徽或祖沖之貢獻，不編造引文。

教師模型及準則

1. 等份支持近似推想；有限曲邊小塊不是精確三角形。

2. A近似(C/2)×r，在分割細化下支持A＝πr²；不可說有限分割已成精確長方形。

3. 154cm²及78.5cm²；第二個半徑5cm。

4. 整圓616cm²；半圓308、四分之一154、四分之三462cm²。

教師模型及準則

5. 記錄真正完整／部分方格及半徑，邊界估算須說明規則。

6. 2πr是長度，πr²才是面積；必要面積推理與可選歷史分開。
