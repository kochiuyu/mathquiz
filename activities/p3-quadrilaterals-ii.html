P3 · Parallel lines

Name ______ Date ______ · Use ruler, set square, pencil, plain/grid paper and safe pre-cut strips or straws. An adult prepares materials. Do not treat printed grid spacing as centimetres.

1 · Extend and compare

On paper draw two straight lines that remain equally far apart. Mark three short perpendicular gaps with a set square and compare them. Explain what would happen if both lines continued. Now draw two short segments that do not meet on the page but WOULD meet when extended. Explain why not meeting inside a small picture is insufficient evidence of parallel lines.

2 · Draw AND make parallel lines

Draw one straight line. Hold a ruler firmly as a guide, align a set-square edge with the line and slide the set square along the ruler without turning it. Draw a second line with that same edge. Check equal perpendicular gaps. Separately make a pair using two straight strips or taut strings on paper; fix them and check the gaps. A partner explains and checks your construction.

3 · Two pairs of parallel sides

Check each card with a ruler and set square. Select ALL parallelograms, including any squares and rectangles. Mark both pairs of opposite parallel sides on your selected cards. Measure and compare both pairs of opposite side lengths. Does turning the page change the properties? Explain.

4 · Squares and rectangles count too

For card A, cross out only names that are NOT true from: square; parallelogram; trapezium. For card B use: rectangle; parallelogram; trapezium. Explain each retained name using opposite sides. We are not asking for one exclusive name.

5 · Exactly one pair

Select ALL trapeziums using the definition “exactly one pair of opposite sides parallel”. Mark that pair as the two bases and show that the other pair is not parallel. Turn card E until its bases are horizontal, then identify its upper and lower base. Explain why A, B and C are not trapeziums under this definition.

Draw and make quadrilaterals

6 · Draw two required shapes

On the grid or your own paper draw (a) a parallelogram with NO right angles and (b) a trapezium with EXACTLY one pair of opposite sides parallel. Label/check the required pairs; for (a) check equal opposite lengths, and for (b) label upper/lower bases after orienting them horizontally. Make each shape again in a different size. Squares or rectangles do not meet the no-right-angle instruction in (a), though they are parallelograms.

7 · Make real models

Use pre-cut strips or straws on paper to make a parallelogram with no right angles, then a trapezium with exactly one parallel pair. Arrange, fix and check them with your tools; record a sketch and explain your checks. Make one differently sized version. Do not infer parallelism solely from equal-looking lengths.

8 · A different instruction

Now draw ANY parallelogram. Is a square acceptable this time? Explain why the instruction differs from task6. A pupil chose only C as a parallelogram from A–F: explain what is missing and why.

Try parallel-pair classification. Screen choices do not certify drawing or making skill.

Teacher key and construction checks

  1. Parallel straight lines in the plane maintain equal perpendicular separation and never meet when extended. Finite segments may appear separate even when their extended lines intersect. Check actual pupil examples.
  2. Observe ruler/set-square alignment, sliding without turning, drawing and separate strip/string construction. Gaps must be perpendicular. Accept another valid construction method with checks.
  3. A, B and C are parallelograms: A square, B rectangle, C a non-right-angle parallelogram. Both opposite side pairs are parallel and equal in length. Rotation preserves these properties.
  4. A: retain square and parallelogram; cross out trapezium. B: retain rectangle and parallelogram; cross out trapezium. No ambiguous exclusive naming question.
  5. D and E are trapeziums. D has horizontal top/bottom bases; E has vertical left/right bases, which become upper/lower after a quarter-turn. Each has exactly one parallel pair. A/B/C have two. F has no parallel pair and is neither group.
  6. Check closed four-sided shapes, required parallelism and equal opposite lengths for the parallelogram; no right angles as explicitly requested. For trapezium verify the second pair is NOT parallel. Accept varied sizes and valid orientations; screen grid is not a calibrated ruler.
  7. Observe each child making and checking both models, not merely copying a photograph. Tools and side-pair evidence are required. Store models/sketches and note help given.
  8. Any parallelogram permits a square or rectangle. Task6 explicitly excludes right angles. Missing A and B must be included because they each have two opposite parallel pairs.

Teacher assessment record

Record independent / prompted / not yet / not observed for each of six objectives: recognise parallel lines; draw AND make parallel lines; parallelogram properties; square/rectangle inclusion; trapezium and bases; draw AND make both shape types. Keep pupil annotations, drawings, models or observations. Separate identification from production.

小三 · 平行線

姓名 ______ 日期 ______ · 用尺、三角尺、鉛筆、白紙／方格紙及安全的預先剪好紙條或飲管。成人準備材料。列印方格不一定是厘米。

1 · 延長及比較

在紙上畫兩條一直保持相同距離的直線，用三角尺標示三處短的垂直間距並比較。解釋兩線延長會怎樣。再畫兩段在紙內不相交、延長後卻會相交的短線段，說明為何小圖內沒相交不足以證明平行。

2 · 繪畫及製作平行線

畫一直線。固定直尺作引導，把三角尺的一邊對準直線，沿直尺平移三角尺而不轉動，用同一邊畫第二線，檢查垂直間距相等。另外用兩根直紙條或拉直的線在紙上製作平行線，固定後檢查間距。請同伴解釋及檢查作品。

3 · 兩對相對邊平行

用尺和三角尺檢查每張圖卡，選出所有平行四邊形，包括符合條件的正方形和長方形。標示選中圖卡的兩對相對平行邊，量度及比較兩對相對邊長。轉動紙張會改變性質嗎？解釋。

4 · 正方形和長方形也包括在內

對圖A，刪去不正確名稱：正方形、平行四邊形、梯形。對圖B：長方形、平行四邊形、梯形。用相對邊性質解釋每個保留名稱，不是只選一個互不相容的名稱。

5 · 只有一對

用「只有一對相對邊平行」的定義選出所有梯形，標示這對邊為兩底，並說明另一對不平行。轉動E使兩底水平，指出上底和下底。解釋按此定義A、B、C為何不是梯形。

繪畫及製作四邊形

6 · 畫兩種指定圖形

在方格或自己的紙上畫(a)沒有直角的平行四邊形；(b)只有一對相對邊平行的梯形。標示及檢查所需邊對；(a)檢查相對邊長分別相等，(b)把底轉至水平後標上底、下底。再畫每種圖形的不同大小版本。(a)指定沒有直角，所以正方形或長方形不符合該指令，雖然都是平行四邊形。

7 · 製作實物模型

用預先剪好的紙條或飲管在紙上製作沒有直角的平行四邊形，再製作只有一對平行邊的梯形。排列、固定及用工具檢查，畫草圖並解釋。再做一個不同大小的版本，不能只憑邊長看來相等就判斷平行。

8 · 不同的指令

現在畫任何一個平行四邊形。這次正方形可以嗎？解釋與任務6的指令有何不同。有同學在A至F只選C作平行四邊形，說明漏了甚麼及原因。

試做平行邊對數分類。屏幕選擇不證明會繪畫或製作。

教師解答及製作檢查

  1. 平面上的平行直線保持相同垂直距離，延長不相交。有限線段看來分開，延長後仍可能相交，須查學生例子。
  2. 觀察直尺及三角尺對齊、平移不轉動、繪畫和另外用紙條／線製作。間距須垂直，接受其他有效且能檢查的方法。
  3. A、B、C是平行四邊形：A正方形，B長方形，C沒有直角。兩對相對邊分別平行且等長，旋轉不改變性質。
  4. A保留正方形及平行四邊形，刪梯形；B保留長方形及平行四邊形，刪梯形。不用含糊的唯一命名題。
  5. D、E是梯形。D的上下邊為水平兩底；E的左右垂直邊為兩底，轉四分一圈後變成上底／下底。各只有一對平行邊。A、B、C有兩對，F沒有，兩類都不是。
  6. 檢查封閉四邊形、所需平行邊；平行四邊形相對邊分別等長且按指令無直角。梯形另一對必須不平行。接受有效的不同大小和方向，屏幕方格不是校準尺。
  7. 觀察每位學生製作及檢查兩類模型，不能只看抄圖，須有工具及邊對證據。保留模型／草圖及所給協助。
  8. 任何平行四邊形允許正方形或長方形，任務6卻明言不要直角。A、B各有兩對相對平行邊，因此不能漏選。
