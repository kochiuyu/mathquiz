P6 · Averages

1 · Share equally

Put 4, 7 and 10 counters in three trays. Estimate a fair level, then move counters without adding or removing any. Record before and after.

P6 · Averages

2 · Estimate from bars

Draw bars for 6, 10, 8 and 12 books. Draw an estimated average line, then transfer the areas above it to fill the gaps below. Compare with calculation.

P6 · Averages

3 · Calculate a mean

For supplied counts 9, 13, 11, 15, 12, record the total and the number of values. Explain what the divisor counts and retain units.

P6 · Averages

4 · Find missing data

Four daily counts have mean 15. Three are 12, 18 and 14. Find the missing count and substitute it back to verify the mean.

P6 · Averages

5 · Collect a small dataset

Choose a safe nonpersonal count, such as books on five shelves. Record actual observations separately from estimates. Compute and explain the mean.

P6 · Averages

6 · Communicate your method

Show a partner how equalising and total divided by count agree. Ask them to check the total, divisor and unit, and record feedback.

Teacher models and observation record

P6 · 平均數

1 · 探究任務

三盤放4、7、10粒棋子，先估平均水平，再不增減棋子地移動，記錄前後數量。

P6 · 平均數

2 · 探究任務

按6、10、8、12本畫棒形圖，畫估計平均線，把線上部分填到線下空位，再與計算比較。

P6 · 平均數

3 · 探究任務

示例數據9、13、11、15、12，記錄總數和數據個數，解釋除數代表甚麼並保留單位。

P6 · 平均數

4 · 探究任務

四天數量平均15，其中三天為12、18、14，求缺少數據並代回核對。

P6 · 平均數

5 · 探究任務

選安全且不涉個人資料的數據，如五層書架的書數，分開記錄實測和估計，計算並解釋平均數。

P6 · 平均數

6 · 探究任務

向同伴說明平均分配與總數除以個數如何一致，請核對總數、除數及單位並記錄回饋。
